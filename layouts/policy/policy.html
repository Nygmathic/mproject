Privacy Policy

Veridus (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the website veridus.space (the “Service”). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

Information Collection And Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily contact us via email. We do not use cookies or tracking scripts. Our analytics (if enabled) are anonymized and privacy‑friendly.

Log Data

Like many site operators, we collect information that your browser sends whenever you visit our Service (“Log Data”). This may include your computer’s IP address, browser type, browser version, the pages of our Service that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and other statistics. This data is anonymized and never sold or shared with third parties.

Email Communications

If you contact us via email, we retain your message and email address solely to respond to your inquiry. We will never add you to any marketing list without your explicit consent.

Third‑Party Services

We do not embed third‑party tracking scripts, advertising pixels, or social media widgets that collect data. The search feature uses Fuse.js client‑side only – no queries are sent to any server.

Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@veridus.space.
