Devastating floods have swept across Kenya, leaving a trail of destruction and chaos in their wake, as the country struggles to cope with the aftermath of heavy rains that have claimed the lives of at least 62 people. The deluge, which has been described as one of the worst in recent memory, has caused widespread damage to homes, infrastructure, and livelihoods, with over 12,000 homes either damaged or destroyed. The disaster has sparked a massive relief effort, with the government, aid agencies, and local communities working together to provide assistance to those affected.

The full extent of the damage is still being assessed, but it is clear that the floods have had a catastrophic impact on many parts of the country. The Ministry of Interior and National Administration has confirmed that the floods have affected several counties, including Kisumu, Homa Bay, and Migori, where thousands of people have been displaced and are in urgent need of food, shelter, and medical care. The government has responded quickly to the disaster, with the President declaring a national emergency and deploying troops to assist with the relief efforts. However, the scale of the disaster is enormous, and it will likely take several weeks, if not months, to fully recover from the devastation. The economic impact of the floods is also likely to be significant, with many businesses and farms affected, and the country's already fragile economy likely to suffer as a result.
