The stakes are high, with the global economy already facing significant headwinds, including a slowdown in trade and a decline in business investment. The sudden spike in oil prices threatens to unleash a wider inflationary problem, eroding consumer purchasing power and undermining the fragile recovery in emerging markets. The Federal Reserve, however, is grappling with the complexities of the situation, weighing the need to support the economy against the risk of overheating and destabilizing financial markets.
