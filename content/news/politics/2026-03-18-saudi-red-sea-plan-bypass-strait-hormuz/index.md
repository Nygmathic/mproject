By rerouting tankers, including Chinese very large crude carriers (VLCCs), through the port of Yanbu on the Red Sea, Saudi Arabia is attempting to mitigate the risks associated with the strategic waterway, which has been at the center of escalating tensions between Iran, the United States, and Israel.

The stakes are high, with the conflict now in its third week and global energy markets on edge. The activation of the Red Sea plan is seen as a pragmatic move by Saudi Arabia to safeguard its oil exports, which are crucial to the kingdom's economy. However, analysts warn that the Red Sea route has limited capacity, and it remains to be seen whether it can adequately replace the Strait of Hormuz as a primary shipping lane.
