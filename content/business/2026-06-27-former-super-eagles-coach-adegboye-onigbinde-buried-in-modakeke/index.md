## Eulogies and Tributes Pour in for Adegboye Onigbinde

In the shadow of a cathedral, where the devout gather to pay respects to the deceased, a gathering of football legends, esteemed dignitaries, and the community at large came together to bid farewell to Adegboye Onigbinde, the man who left an indelible mark on Nigerian football. The former Super Eagles coach, a stalwart of the sport, was laid to rest in Modakeke, Osun State, on a crisp Friday morning, his legacy etched in the memories of those who knew him.

The 88-year-old's passing in March, though a personal loss for his family, was a collective sorrow for the football fraternity, which he served with dedication and passion. His achievements, though numerous, were not limited to the pitch. Mr Onigbinde's influence extended beyond the confines of the game, as he became a beacon for indigenous coaches, advocating for greater opportunities and better coaching education.

Throughout his illustrious career, Mr Onigbinde's name was synonymous with excellence. He was the first Nigerian coach to lead the Super Eagles to the FIFA World Cup, a feat he accomplished in 2002 at the tournament in South Korea and Japan. Prior to this, he had become the first indigenous coach to guide Nigeria to the Africa Cup of Nations final, finishing runners-up to Cameroon in 1984. His impact on Nigerian football was palpable, earning him a place among the country's most revered coaches.

Former Super Eagles captain and current NFF Technical Director Augustine Eguavoen, who represented the federation at the burial, captured the essence of Mr Onigbinde's impact on Nigerian football. "The NFF recognises and appreciates the service of late Chief Onigbinde to our country. He was very energetic and rose to the pinnacle of the profession by dint of hard work, dedication and discipline. He was a towering figure in coaching globally. His legacy is stout and impressive."

The late coach's association with Shooting Stars Sports Club of Ibadan was another significant chapter in his illustrious career. During his tenure at the club, he enjoyed some of his most successful years in club football, leaving an enduring mark on the team's history.

As the nation mourns the loss of this football icon, his family, the football community, and the people of Modakeke are left to grapple with the void his passing has created. The NFF's provision of financial support to the family as part of the funeral arrangements is a testament to the respect and admiration in which Mr Onigbinde was held.

In the wake of his burial, the football fraternity will continue to draw inspiration from his achievements, his passion, and his unwavering commitment to the sport he loved. As the nation says goodbye to a true football legend, it is a poignant reminder that the legacy of Adegboye Onigbinde will continue to inspire generations to come.
