## NDLEA's Enforcement Drive in Enugu: A Glimmer of Hope in the Fight Against Illicit Drugs

Enugu, the capital of Enugu State, has witnessed a significant crackdown on illicit drug trafficking in recent months, with the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) seizing a staggering 929.463 kilogrammes of various substances between November 2025 and June 2026. This remarkable achievement is a testament to the agency's unwavering commitment to reducing the scourge of drug abuse in Nigeria. As the country continues to grapple with the challenges of economic development and youth empowerment, the NDLEA's successes in Enugu offer a glimmer of hope that the tide of illicit drug trafficking can be turned.

At the heart of the NDLEA's efforts in Enugu is the tireless work of Commander Owoputi Adekunle, who has led the charge against drug abuse in the state. Speaking at the commemoration of the 2026 International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking, Commander Adekunle revealed that his team has not only seized a substantial quantity of illicit substances but has also secured the conviction of 53 drug-related offenders. Furthermore, the commander disclosed that 157 clients were counselled, and 23 individuals are currently undergoing rehabilitation as part of the agency's efforts to promote a drug-free society.

The NDLEA's achievements in Enugu are a significant departure from the narrative of a country plagued by the menace of illicit drug trafficking. The sheer scale of the seizures and convictions is a testament to the agency's dedication to reducing the supply of illicit drugs. Moreover, the Commander's emphasis on the need for community engagement and collaboration with schools, religious organisations, and traditional rulers underscores the importance of a multi-stakeholder approach in addressing the complex issue of drug abuse.

The Enugu State Government has also taken a proactive stance in addressing the issue of drug abuse, with Governor Peter Mbah urging young people to reject the temptation of illicit substances and instead take advantage of the numerous opportunities created by the state government. The governor's emphasis on the importance of hard work and productivity in building a robust economy is a welcome shift in focus, particularly in a country where youth empowerment is a pressing concern.
