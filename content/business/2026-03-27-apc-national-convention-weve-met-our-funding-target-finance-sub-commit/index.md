## A Nation's Politics Take Center Stage

As the 2024 national convention of the All Progressives Congress (APC) draws near, stakeholders in Nigeria's ruling party are breathing a sigh of relief. After weeks of anxious deliberations, the finance sub-committee has announced that the party has met its funding target for the crucial event. The APC's national convention, set to take place in the coming months, is a critical milestone in the party's preparations for the 2025 general elections.

## The Stakes are High

The convention's significance cannot be overstated. Not only does it serve as a platform for the party to unveil its presidential candidate, but it also sets the tone for the party's internal dynamics in the run-up to the general elections. A smooth convention will likely bolster the party's morale and reinforce its grip on power. Conversely, any hiccups could lead to infighting, undermine party cohesion, and potentially create openings for opposition parties to capitalize on APC's weaknesses.

The party's finance sub-committee has been working tirelessly to raise funds for the convention. Their efforts have paid off, with the committee now confirming that the required amount has been collected. According to sources, the party had initially set a target of ₦5 billion, but the finance sub-committee has managed to exceed this target, securing a significant surplus to cover any unforeseen expenses.

## A Complex Web of Interests

The APC's national convention is not just about party politics; it also reflects the intricate dance of interests within Nigeria's ruling elite. Regional and ethnic tensions are likely to simmer beneath the surface as party leaders jockey for position. The outcome of the convention will have far-reaching implications for the party's internal balance of power and its ability to present a united front in the face of mounting economic challenges.

In this context, it is essential to contextualize the APC's struggles within the broader narrative of Nigeria's economic trajectory. The country's debt-to-GDP ratio continues to rise, while growth rates remain sluggish. The party's ability to navigate these challenges and present a convincing economic agenda will be crucial in determining its electoral fortunes.

## Historical Parallels and Emerging Trends

The APC's struggles evoke memories of the 2014 APC national convention, which was marred by controversy and infighting. That convention ultimately led to the emergence of General Muhammadu Buhari as the party's presidential candidate, a move that reshaped the contours of Nigeria's politics. While it is impossible to predict the outcome of the current convention with certainty, there are emerging trends that suggest a more nuanced and complex party dynamic.

One notable trend is the growing influence of the party's youth wing. This development is significant, as young Nigerians are increasingly becoming a decisive force in the country's politics. The youth wing's activism and advocacy have already begun to shape the party's internal debates, and their presence at the convention is likely to be felt.

## Reactions and Implications

The finance sub-committee's announcement has been met with a mix of relief and caution. While some party leaders have expressed satisfaction with the committee's work, others have tempered their enthusiasm, warning that the party still faces significant challenges in the run-up to the convention.

Beyond the party's internal dynamics, the implications of the convention will be far-reaching. As the convention approaches, stakeholders will be watching with bated breath, eager to see how the party navigates its internal complexities and presents a united front in the face of mounting economic challenges.
