## Governance in Osun: A Mixed Bag

In a closely watched gubernatorial election, Osun residents are set to decide whether Governor Ademola Adeleke deserves a second term in office. The Adeleke administration has maintained that it has delivered on its campaign promises, but the performance of the government is a subject of debate among the populace.

While some residents praised the Adeleke administration for its performance, particularly in workers' welfare, infrastructure, and education, others said the government had fallen short of expectations in areas such as healthcare, security, tourism, investment, and equitable distribution of infrastructure. The mixed bag of opinions underscores the complexities of governance in Osun.

During the campaigns for the 2022 Osun State governorship election, Governor Ademola Adeleke repeatedly assured residents that his administration would chart a different course if elected. Running as the candidate of the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP), he promised improved welfare, economic growth, infrastructure development, and people-centred policies, arguing that Osun deserved better governance.

Addressing thousands of supporters at a rally in Osogbo on 14 May 2022, Mr Adeleke unveiled a five-point agenda as the blueprint for his administration from 2022 to 2026. The agenda promised improved welfare for workers and pensioners, economic expansion through business partnerships and support for enterprises, and a homegrown infrastructure policy aimed at ensuring "Osun money serves Osun people." The agenda also prioritised people-focused social policies centred on skill-based education, affordable healthcare, security, and social protection, alongside agro-based industrialisation to create jobs and wealth for the youth and women.

Barely two months later, he defeated the incumbent governor, Gboyega Oyetola, the All Progressive Congress (APC)'s flagbearer, in the 16 July 2022 governorship election by more than 28,000 votes. Nearly four years into his administration, Mr Adeleke maintained that his government has delivered on its campaign promises.

Last month, he said his administration had recorded achievements across key sectors, including the construction of more than 300 kilometres of roads, the rehabilitation of over 200 primary healthcare centres and more than 150 public schools, as well as the continued payment of salary arrears and pension debts. He also cited the disbursement of nearly N2 billion in cooperative financing, the provision of tractors and other farm inputs to support agricultural production, and the ongoing development of a standard stadium.

The administration had earlier, in November 2024, announced that it had cleared more than N47 billion in inherited pension liabilities within two years in office. The government also stated that it approved the implementation of the N75,000 minimum wage for state workers as part of its commitment to improving workers' welfare.

Mr Adeleke, who is seeking a second term under the Accord Party umbrella in Saturday's election, said his administration fulfilled its pledge to make the University of Ilesa fully operational through the commissioning of several landmark projects. He also pointed to investments in infrastructure at Osun State University (UNIOSUN), the advancement of the state's ICT and digital economy agenda, and initiatives aimed at improving education and student welfare.

Some residents of the South-west state, in separate interviews with this publication, said they were not entirely convinced by the Adeleke administration's performance. They pointed to the ongoing challenges in the healthcare sector, citing inadequate facilities and shortage of medical personnel. Others lamented the lack of investments in the tourism sector, which they argued had the potential to generate significant revenue for the state.

The divergent views underscore the complexities of governance in Osun, where different stakeholders have different expectations from the government. As the state prepares to elect a new governor, residents are weighing the pros and cons of the Adeleke administration's performance.

## Residents' Perspectives: A Mixed Bag

While some residents praised the Adeleke administration's performance, particularly in workers' welfare, infrastructure, and education, others said the government had fallen short of expectations in areas such as healthcare, security, tourism, investment, and equitable distribution of infrastructure. The mixed bag of opinions underscores the complexities of governance in Osun.

Resident A, who preferred not to be named, said: "The administration has done well in terms of infrastructure development and workers' welfare. I have seen improvements in the road network and the payment of salary arrears. However, I have concerns about the healthcare sector, where I think there is a need for more investments in facilities and personnel."

Resident B, who also preferred not to be named, said: "I think the administration has done well in terms of education, particularly in the rehabilitation of schools. However, I have concerns about the lack of investments in the tourism sector, which I think has the potential to generate significant revenue for the state."

The mixed bag of opinions underscores the complexities of governance in Osun, where different stakeholders have different expectations from the government. As the state prepares to elect a new governor, residents are weighing the pros and cons of the Adeleke administration's performance.

## Implications of the Election

The outcome of the election will have significant implications for the state's governance and development. If Governor Adeleke is re-elected, it is likely that his administration will continue to focus on its people-centred policies and infrastructure development. However, if a new governor is elected, it is likely that the administration will have a different agenda.

The election also has implications for the state's economy, which has been impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic and other global economic challenges. The new governor will have to navigate these challenges and develop strategies to stimulate economic growth and development in the state.
