## Fubara Presents Ambitious N1.85 Trillion Budget for 2026 Fiscal Year

Rivers State Governor Siminalayi Fubara's presentation of the 2026 appropriation bill of N1.85 trillion to the state House of Assembly on Friday marks a significant milestone in the state's fiscal planning. The proposed budget, tagged "Budget of Resilience for Growth and Development," reflects the governor's commitment to driving economic growth, human capital development, and socio-economic infrastructure development in the state.

The proposed total operating revenue for 2026 is projected to increase by 24.49 per cent over the 2025 adjusted budget projections, indicating a positive economic outlook. This optimism is further solidified by the capital budget, which has increased from over N713 billion in 2025 to over N1.4 trillion in 2026, representing a 22.49 per cent rise in infrastructure services expenditure. This significant investment in capital projects is expected to drive growth and development in various sectors of the economy.

A breakdown of the budget reveals that over N1.4 trillion has been assigned to capital projects, while over N413 billion has been budgeted for recurrent expenditure. The recurrent expenditure allocation includes N154 billion for total personnel costs, including salaries for Ministries, Departments, and Parastatals, as well as N15 billion for new recruitments. Consolidated revenue fund charges have been allocated N772 million, and overheads for MDAs are projected to be N36 billion.

The governor has also emphasized the importance of increasing overhead expenditure by at least 50 per cent to enable MDAs to cover their operational costs. This move is expected to boost the efficiency and effectiveness of state institutions.

## Revenue and Expenditure: A Delicate Balance

The revenue sources for the budget funding include internally generated revenue projected to be more than N487 billion. Projected revenue from FAAC, including derivation funds, value-added tax, and exchange gain, is N936 billion, while N48 billion is expected to be generated from opening and closing balances. The total capital receipts, including domestic loans, grants, and assets sales, is estimated to be more than N382 billion.

The breakdown of capital expenditure includes N533 billion allocated to works and infrastructure; N315 billion for educational development; N105 billion for healthcare delivery; and N41 billion for the Rivers State House of Assembly. The governor has stated that the 2026 budget focuses on the objectives of his administration to build a secure, prosperous, and resilient state.

## Priority Areas and Fiscal Objectives

The governor has outlined his administration's primary priorities for the 2026 financial year, which include economic growth, human capital development, and socio-economic infrastructure development. The budget is also in consonance with the needs and aspirations of the people of the state. According to the governor, the details of the 2026 budget assumptions and fiscal objectives are outlined in the medium-term expenditure framework 2026-2028, which the house has already considered.

The presentation of the 2026 appropriation bill marks a significant step towards achieving the governor's vision for a prosperous and resilient Rivers State. As the state House of Assembly deliberates on the budget, stakeholders are eagerly awaiting the outcome, which is expected to have far-reaching implications for the state's economy and development.
