## Lancet Report Sparks Global Outcry as USAID Cuts Linked to 14 Million Deaths

Donald Trump's administration is facing scathing criticism after a new report from The Lancet medical journal revealed that the devastating cuts to the United States Agency for International Development (USAID) have claimed the lives of at least 14 million people worldwide. At the heart of the controversy is the Trump administration's decision to drastically slash USAID's budget, which has led to a significant reduction in critical healthcare services, humanitarian aid, and economic support to vulnerable countries. The Lancet report, which analyzed data from 2018 to 2020, found that the cuts have had a disproportionate impact on low-income and conflict-affected countries, exacerbating existing health crises and pushing millions to the brink of disaster.

The report's findings have sparked widespread outrage, with global health experts, aid organizations, and governments condemning the Trump administration's policies as catastrophic and inhumane. "The Lancet report is a stark reminder of the devastating consequences of the Trump administration's reckless cuts to USAID," said Dr. Amira Al-Harthi, a leading public health expert and former WHO official.
