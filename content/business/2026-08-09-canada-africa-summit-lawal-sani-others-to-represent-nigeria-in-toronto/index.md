## Nigeria Takes Centre Stage at Canada-Africa Summit

Nigeria's delegation to the Canada-Africa Summit, set to take place in Toronto from 10 to 15 August 2026, has been finalized, with key officials and governors representing the country in a bid to strengthen diplomatic and economic ties between Nigeria and Canada. The summit, which will focus on digital rails and inclusive growth, promises to be a significant platform for Nigeria to showcase its economic potential and attract investment.

At the forefront of Nigeria's delegation is Zamfara State Governor, Dauda Lawal, who will play a key role in representing the country at the summit. According to a statement issued by Sulaiman Bala Idris, spokesperson to the Zamfara State Governor, Governor Lawal's participation has been facilitated by the Presidency's invitation, which highlights his role as a key participant in the Nigeria Diaspora Investment Economic Conference (NDIEC). This development is seen as a strategic move by the Presidency to strengthen Nigeria's diplomatic and economic standing at the summit.

The Nigeria Diaspora Economic Conference (NIDEC) is a leading global platform where governments, investors, multinational companies, development finance institutions, foundations, and partners form strategic partnerships and explore investment opportunities. The summit is expected to provide a unique opportunity for Nigeria to showcase its economic potential and attract investment in key sectors such as agribusiness, strategic minerals development, human capital growth, and digital innovation.

Governor Lawal's involvement in the Canada-Africa Investment Summit is expected to position Zamfara State as Nigeria's emerging hub for economic development. The Zamfara State Government sees NIDEC as more than just a diplomatic event; it is a strategic economic mission to attract investment, technology transfer, development funding, and technical partnerships to drive the State's economic transformation.

Nigeria's delegation to the summit also includes other key officials, including the President's Chief of Staff, Femi Gbajabiamila; Governors Charles Soludo of Anambra State, Babagana Zulum (Borno), Uba Sani (Kaduna), and Caleb Muftwang of Plateau State. Additionally, the delegation will comprise Minister of Foreign Affairs, Mrs Bianca Odumegwu-Ojukwu; Minister of Industry, Trade and Investment, Dr Jumoke Oduwole; and Minister of Interior, Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo, among others.

The Canada-Africa Summit is set to take place from 10 to 15 August 2026, and it promises to be a significant platform for Nigeria to strengthen its economic and diplomatic ties with Canada. With a strong delegation representing Nigeria, the country is expected to make a significant impact at the summit and attract investment in key sectors.
