## The Future of Business in Africa: UBA Business Series Brings Together Visionaries

In a continent where consumer needs and market realities often diverge from global standards, African businesses are pioneering a new approach to innovation – one that puts the customer at the forefront. This Thursday, 16 July 2026, the United Bank for Africa (UBA) Plc will host the latest edition of its quarterly UBA Business Series, a platform that has become a benchmark for knowledge-sharing and entrepreneurship in Africa.

Titled "Building for Africa's Realities: Turning Consumer Feedback into Technology-Driven Solutions," this edition promises to deliver candid insights from African business leaders who have successfully navigated the complexities of the continent's markets. The session will be hosted by media entrepreneur and founder, Adaora Mbelu, and will feature a lineup of esteemed speakers, including rapper and entrepreneur, M I Abaga; venture capitalist and co-founder of Octerra Capital, Ashim Egunjobi; and co-founder and chief executive of Chowdeck, Femi Aluko.

The UBA Business Series has grown into a major platform, equipped with the insights, tools, and strategies to build sustainable enterprises and navigate an evolving business environment. With over 25,000 employees and more than 45 million customers globally, UBA operates in 20 African countries, as well as the United Kingdom, the United States, France, and the United Arab Emirates, providing retail, commercial, and institutional banking services while advancing financial inclusion through innovative technology.

Through this edition, UBA aims to put the discipline of listening, adapting, and building accordingly within reach of every entrepreneur, creating a platform for honest conversations about product, technology, and growth. As Alero Ladipo, UBA's Group Head, Brands, Marketing, and Corporate Communications, notes, "When our businesses build for Africa's realities, the whole continent benefits." This commitment to the entrepreneurs and businesses driving Africa's growth underscores the bank's dedication to empowering innovation and entrepreneurship on the continent.

The session will also explore the importance of staying close to the customer in a fast-moving market. As the African business landscape continues to evolve, entrepreneurs and business leaders must be willing to adapt and innovate in response to changing consumer needs. By sharing their personal journeys and perspectives, the panelists will provide valuable insights into designing products that work in African conditions, rather than solutions borrowed wholesale from elsewhere.

The event will be streamed live across UBA's digital platforms, including the Group's YouTube channel, allowing participants to join virtually and follow the conversation online. This accessibility reflects UBA's commitment to making knowledge-sharing and entrepreneurship accessible to a wider audience.

As the UBA Business Series continues to grow, it has become a benchmark for knowledge-sharing and entrepreneurship in Africa. This edition promises to deliver valuable insights and practical strategies for entrepreneurs and business leaders, while highlighting the critical role that businesses can play in driving Africa's growth and development.
