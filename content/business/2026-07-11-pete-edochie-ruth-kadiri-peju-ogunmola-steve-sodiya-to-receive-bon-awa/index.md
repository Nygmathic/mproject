## Honoring Legacy and Excellence in Nigerian Cinema

The Best of Nollywood (BON) Awards has announced the recipients of this year's Special Recognition Awards, honoring individuals who have made significant contributions to the growth and development of Nigeria's film industry. These four industry stalwarts have been selected for their contributions to filmmaking, mentorship, and the growth of the industry. Pete Edochie, a veteran actor and Member of the Order of the Niger (MON), is being recognized for his decades-long acting career and his portrayal of characters that have become synonymous with authority, wisdom, and African culture. With a career spanning over four decades, Edochie has become an icon in the Nigerian film industry, and his influence extends beyond the screen to inspire new generations of filmmakers.

Ruth Kadiri, a talented actress, producer, and screenwriter, is being honored for her work in producing and starring in several successful films. Her contributions to digital filmmaking through YouTube and her efforts to support emerging talent behind the scenes have not gone unnoticed. Peju Ogunmola, a veteran actress, is receiving the Special Recognition Award for her contributions to Yoruba and English-language films and her role in promoting indigenous culture through theatre and cinema. With a career spanning over three decades, Ogunmola has become a household name in Nigeria, and her influence extends beyond the screen to inspire new generations of filmmakers.

Steve Sodiya, a filmmaker, editor, and director, is being recognized for his work in talent development and his contributions to filmmaking both behind and in front of the camera.
