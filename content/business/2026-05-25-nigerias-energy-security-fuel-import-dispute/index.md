## Nigeria's Energy Security Under Siege: A Brewing Storm in the Downstream Sector

Tensions are running high in Nigeria's downstream petroleum sector, where the Nigerian National Petroleum Corporation (NNPC), regulators, and government officials have rallied behind the Attorney-General of the Federation (AGF) in a high-stakes legal battle against billionaire Aliko Dangote's Dangote Group. The suit, currently before the Federal High Court in Lagos, has exposed deep-seated fault lines in Nigeria's fuel import regime and crude supply obligations, raising concerns about the country's energy security.

At the heart of the dispute is Dangote's decision to import refined petroleum products into Nigeria, allegedly in contravention of the NNPC's exclusive right to import petroleum products. The NNPC, which has long been the sole importer of petroleum products into the country, claims that Dangote's actions are unlawful and threaten the country's energy security. The AGF, representing the federal government, has thrown his weight behind the NNPC, arguing that Dangote's actions are a clear contravention of the country's petroleum regulations.

The dispute has significant implications for Nigeria's energy security, which has long been a major source of concern for the country.
