## A New Era in Nigerian Archery: Team Gold and Seven Medals at African Championships

Nigeria's archers made history at the 15th African Archery Championships in Oran, Algeria, winning a historic team gold medal and seven medals in total, including five silver and one bronze. The team's outstanding performance marked Nigeria's best-ever outing at the continental competition, solidifying the country's growing status in African archery.

The championships, held from 21 to 26 July, featured 138 archers from 18 countries competing under the auspices of World Archery Africa (WAAf). Nigeria's largest contingent ever at the African Championships, comprising 18 athletes, was led by Nigeria Archery Federation (NAFED) President Mohammed Baba Abdullahi and Vice-President Augustine Obiefuna Chigbolu, with Aliyu Garga serving as coach.

At the heart of Nigeria's success was the barebow women's team, comprising Faith Adekogbe, Mandu Eteidung, and Precious Fanny-Amun, who defeated hosts Algeria 5-3 in the final to claim the continental title. This historic gold medal marked Nigeria's first-ever team gold medal at the African Archery Championships and capped an impressive campaign for the trio.

Faith Adekogbe emerged as Nigeria's standout performer, winning three medals during the competition. In addition to helping the women's team secure gold, she claimed individual silver after reaching the final as the top seed. Adekogbe's performances were a testament to her skill and dedication, as she defeated Algeria's Malia Ait Messaoudene 6-4 in the quarter-finals and then overcame compatriot Precious Fanny-Amun by the same score in an all-Nigerian semi-final.

The barebow men's team, consisting of Abdul Rahman Obasa, Abdulbasit Sanni, and Abubakar Usman, also reached the final, finishing runners-up to Algeria. Usman finished the championships with two silver medals, while the men's team claimed silver after defeating Algeria 205-183 in the semi-finals before losing 197-182 to Mauritius in the final.

Nigeria added three more medals in the compound events, with the men's team of Aliyu Abubakar, Seun Emmanuel Oyeleke, and Damilola Bartholomew Sholademi defeating Algeria 205-183 in the semi-finals before losing 197-182 to Mauritius in the final to settle for silver. The mixed compound team of Hareke Evans and Seun Emmanuel Oyeleke also reached the final after beating Algeria 143-102 in the last four, but lost 142-138 to Namibia to earn another silver medal.

Evans rounded off a successful outing by winning bronze in the compound women's individual event, defeating Algeria's Sihem Akli 129-90 in the third-place match. Oyeleke ended the championships with two silver medals from the men's team and mixed team competitions.

The Director of Marketing for the Nigeria Archery Federation, Emmanuel Oyeleke, emphasized that the success of the team should inspire greater investment in the sport. "This achievement is a testament to the hard work and dedication of our athletes and coaches. We hope that this success will encourage more investment in archery in Nigeria, enabling us to continue pushing the boundaries of what is possible for our athletes."

The performance builds on Nigeria's recent progress in the sport, following medal-winning campaigns in Pretoria (2022) and Nabeul, Tunisia (2023), as well as gold medal performances at the 2025 African Championships and African Games. As the sport continues to grow in Nigeria, fans and enthusiasts can look forward to more thrilling performances from the country's talented archers.
