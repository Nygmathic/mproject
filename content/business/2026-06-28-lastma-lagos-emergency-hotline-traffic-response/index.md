## **LASTMA Takes a Major Step Towards a Safer Lagos**

Lagos, the economic hub of Nigeria, continues to grapple with the challenges of managing its ever-growing traffic congestion. However, a significant development in this regard took place recently, as the Lagos State Traffic Management Authority (LASTMA) launched a toll-free emergency hotline to improve traffic response in the state. The new hotline, 3367, allows motorists and other road users to report incidents such as broken-down vehicles, road crashes, and traffic obstructions without incurring any call charges.

## **Improved Accessibility and Response**

The 3367 platform is available free of charge to subscribers on MTN, Glo, and 9mobile (formerly Etisalat), providing millions of users with a faster and more accessible channel for reporting traffic-related emergencies. Through this initiative, LASTMA aims to strengthen transparency, accountability, and service delivery, as well as improve emergency response times to traffic incidents. The agency has also ensured that callers will be able to communicate with operators in English, Yoruba, or Pidgin English to cater to the diverse linguistic needs of the population.

## **A Technology-Driven Approach**

The launch of the toll-free emergency hotline marks the culmination of LASTMA's earlier commitment to introduce a dedicated toll-free short code as part of efforts to make its services more accessible to residents. The agency has recently stepped up the deployment of technology in its operations, including surveillance drones, GPS-enabled patrol vehicles, and a central command-and-control system aimed at reducing response times to traffic incidents. This technology-driven approach is seen as a major step towards institutionalising a safer, more efficient, and highly responsive transportation ecosystem across Lagos State.

## **A Major Milestone in LASTMA's History**

According to the General Manager of LASTMA, Olalekan Bakare-Oki, the inauguration of the 3367 Toll-Free Short-Code Hotline represents a monumental stride towards improving emergency response and deploying technology to address Lagos' traffic management challenges. Mr Bakare-Oki assured residents that all reports received through the hotline would be handled professionally and confidentially before being forwarded to the appropriate operational units for prompt action. The rapid growth of Lagos as one of Africa's largest cities necessitates a technology-driven traffic management system capable of responding quickly to evolving transportation challenges.
