However, the recent surge in gas prices has highlighted the country's vulnerability to disruptions in global energy supplies. According to data from the U.S. Energy Information Administration, the average price of gasoline in the U.S. has risen by over 20% in the past month alone, with some states seeing prices increase by as much as 30%.

In response, the U.S. has imposed new sanctions on Iran, which have further strained relations between the two nations.
