## Foiling Terror: Military Thwarts Attack at Benue Yam Market

In a daring display of speed and precision, troops of Operation Whirl Stroke (OPWS) and the Benue State Civil Protection Guard have successfully foiled a terrorist attack at Tor Donga Yam Market in Benue State. The swift response, which neutralized two suspected terrorists and recovered a cache of arms and ammunition, highlights the growing collaboration between security agencies in Nigeria's fight against terrorism.

According to Major General Moses Gara, the Force Commander of OPWS, the troops responded swiftly to a distress call while conducting fighting and confidence-building patrols around the market. "The troops engaged the attackers in a fierce gun battle, neutralising two suspected terrorists identified as Dantaraba and Golozo," he revealed in a statement issued on Sunday in Makurdi. The successful operation ensured the safety of traders and residents, allowing commercial activities at the yam market to continue without disruption.

The recovered items, which included one AK-47 rifle, one AK-47 magazine, one damaged pistol, one pistol magazine, six rounds of 7.62mm NATO ammunition, nine rounds of 9mm pistol ammunition, one ATM card, one Moniepoint ATM card, and other sundry items, underscore the sophistication of terrorist networks operating in the region. The presence of ATM cards and other personal items suggests that the terrorists may have been attempting to blend in with the local population, highlighting the need for vigilance and cooperation from residents.

The successful operation has been hailed as a testament to the growing collaboration between security agencies in Nigeria. Major General Gara commended the troops for their professionalism, resilience, and swift response, saying their timely intervention prevented what could have resulted in heavy civilian casualties and disruption of economic activities. He also praised the Benue State Civil Protection Guard for its collaboration with the military in efforts to rid the state of criminal elements.
