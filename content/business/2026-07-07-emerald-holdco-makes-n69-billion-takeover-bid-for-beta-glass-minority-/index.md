## A Bid to Consolidate Control

Emerald HoldCo's audacious takeover bid for Beta Glass' minority shares has sent shockwaves through the Nigerian stock market, with the company tabling a staggering N6.9 billion offer for the shares. The core investor, Emerald HoldCo B.V, has launched a mandatory takeover offer to acquire up to 11.74 million shares held by other shareholders of the glass container maker, placing the offer at a 5 per cent premium above the market price.

At the heart of the deal is Emerald HoldCo B.V, the new majority owner of Beta Glass, which has taken indirect ownership of 331,260,999 shares in the company, translating into roughly 55.2 per cent of the company's issued share capital. The company's board of directors approved the offer on 5 February 2026, authorising a takeover offer to be made to other shareholders of the Company, other than Packaging Industries Nigeria Limited and Emerald Nigeria Intermediate Holdings B.V, for the acquisition of the offer shares representing 1.96% of total Issued and fully paid share capital of the company.

The offer has also received the approval of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), with the qualification date for the offer set at Thursday, 25 June 2026. The offer opened on 7 July and will end on 4 August, giving shareholders a four-week window to accept or reject the offer. The offer price of N590.94 per unit is a 5 per cent premium above the market price of N562.8 per unit at the start of trade on Lagos' Customs Street on Tuesday.

Emerald HoldCo's bid is the culmination of a deal announced last December, which saw the company acquire Frigoglass Group's stake in Beta Glass for $100 million. The cash settlement for the transaction was to be executed in March 2026. The acquisition has given Emerald HoldCo B.V, a special purpose vehicle ultimately controlled through two investment funds managed by Helios, indirect ownership of 76.03% of Packaging Industries Nigeria Limited.
