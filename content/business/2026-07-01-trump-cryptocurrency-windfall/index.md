The phenomenon of the $TRUMP token serves as a prime example of a cryptocurrency bubble. A bubble occurs when a large number of investors drive up the price of an asset, often fueled by hype and speculation, rather than any fundamental value. In the case of $TRUMP, the token's value skyrocketed due to the widespread expectation that its price would increase in tandem with Trump's presidency.

The consequences of this bubble are evident in the losses incurred by many of Trump's followers who invested in the token.
