## Strengthening Revenue Collection through Digital Means

In a bid to enhance transparency, reduce leakages, and improve public sector accountability, the Federal Government has embarked on a digital initiative to modernise revenue administration. The Revenue Optimisation Assurance Platform (RevOp), a centralised digital platform designed to streamline revenue collection, has been introduced to address longstanding inefficiencies associated with manual processes. The platform, which enables government agencies to generate bills, collect payments, monitor transactions, and report revenue in real time, is now being implemented with the support of commercial banks.

According to officials, RevOp is a critical tool in the government's drive to improve revenue administration and reduce leakages. The initiative aims to leverage technology-driven solutions to strengthen revenue generation and improve financial management across public institutions. With over 70 per cent of federal government-owned entities already onboarded, the platform is expected to revolutionise the way revenues are collected and managed. As the Minister of Finance and Coordinating Minister of the Economy, Taiwo Oyedele, pointed out, commercial banks play a crucial role in ensuring the initiative's success, extending their responsibilities beyond collecting payments to supporting efficient revenue administration.

However, challenges persist in the implementation of RevOp. Inadequate understanding of the platform's processes by frontline banking staff has affected customer experience and transaction efficiency. These operational challenges, though significant, can be addressed through the sensitisation workshop organised by the Office of the Accountant-General of the Federation (OAGF) in Abuja. The workshop aimed to educate commercial banks on the importance of RevOp and the role they play in its success. By ensuring that knowledge gained from the workshop reaches branch managers, customer service officers, and tellers, financial institutions can improve their understanding of the platform's processes and provide better customer experience.

The integration of RevOp with existing licensed payment service providers and commercial banks nationwide is a testament to the government's commitment to leveraging technology to improve public finance management. By presenting RevOp-generated bills to commercial banks for payment, people can expect prompt collection, reducing the likelihood of leakages and enhancing transparency. As the platform continues to be implemented, it is crucial that commercial banks remain committed to supporting RevOp's success, ensuring that the initiative achieves its desired outcomes.
