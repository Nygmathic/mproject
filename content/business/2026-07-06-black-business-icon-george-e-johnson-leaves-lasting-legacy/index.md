## A Legacy of Style and Entrepreneurship

George E. Johnson, the visionary founder of Johnson Products Company, a pioneering Black-owned hair-care empire, has passed away at the age of 99. Johnson's remarkable life's work, which spanned over six decades, left an indelible mark on the beauty industry and the African American community. His company, which was the first Black-owned concern to be listed on a major American stock exchange, was a testament to his unwavering dedication to innovation and his unshakeable commitment to the economic empowerment of his people.

Johnson's entrepreneurial journey began in the early 1950s, when he founded Johnson Products Company in a small Chicago apartment. Initially, the company focused on producing hair care products catering to the needs of the African American community, which at the time faced significant challenges in accessing quality hair care products that were tailored to their unique hair characteristics. Through his relentless efforts, Johnson not only created a product line that was both effective and accessible but also established a business model that would serve as a beacon of inspiration for generations of entrepreneurs to come.
