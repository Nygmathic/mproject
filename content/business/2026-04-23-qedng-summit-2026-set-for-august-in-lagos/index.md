As the warm, humid air of Lagos envelops the city in a perpetual embrace, a flurry of activity is set to unfold in August, when the QEDNG Summit 2026 converges on the vibrant metropolis. This year's summit promises to be a defining moment for the country's burgeoning arts and culture sector, as it seeks to position itself firmly on the world stage.

At its core, QEDNG Summit 2026 is a platform for Nigerian creatives to showcase their talents, connect with international peers, and explore new financing and growth opportunities. The event will feature a series of keynote speeches, panel discussions, and curated sessions, all designed to stimulate innovation, foster collaboration, and provide valuable insights into the global creative industry. With a focus on innovation, financing, growth opportunities, and the global positioning of Nigerian creative talent, the summit aims to demystify the creative economy and unlock its vast potential for the benefit of all stakeholders.

The creative economy is a rapidly growing sector, with Nigeria at the forefront of this trend. According to recent estimates, the country's creative industry is valued at over $10 billion, with a growth rate of 15% per annum. This is largely driven by the country's vibrant music, film, and visual arts scenes, which have gained international recognition in recent years. From Wizkid to Davido, from Nollywood to Afrobeats, Nigeria has produced a plethora of world-class artists who have transcended borders and cultures, captivating audiences worldwide. However, beneath this success story lies a more complex narrative of challenges, opportunities, and contradictions.

However, the sector's growth has been hindered by various factors, including inadequate infrastructure, lack of funding, and limited market access. The government's recent efforts to invest in the creative economy, through initiatives such as the Creative Industry Financing Initiative (CIFI), are a testament to the sector's growing importance.

With a focus on innovation, financing, and growth opportunities, the summit aims to unlock the creative economy's potential, creating new opportunities for creatives, entrepreneurs, and industry leaders alike.
