## Fire Incident at Polaris Bank Branch: Polaris Bank Debunks Death Reports

A fire incident that occurred on Thursday afternoon at a multi-storey building housing a Polaris Bank branch on Broad Street, Lagos Island, has sparked widespread speculation and reports of fatalities. However, Polaris Bank has come out to debunk these claims, stating that there were no casualties or fatalities in the incident.

According to the bank, the fire originated from a customer's vehicle parked on the third-floor car park and was swiftly contained following the activation of its emergency response procedures and the prompt intervention of firefighters. In a statement released on X, the bank confirmed that there were no fatalities and that reports claiming over 34 persons were feared dead are false and misleading.

"We confirm that there were no casualties or fatalities. Reports claiming that over 34 persons were feared dead are false and misleading," the bank stated. "Investigations into the incident are ongoing. We thank emergency responders, our staff, customers, and stakeholders for their support."

The bank has urged the public to rely only on credible news platforms and its official channels for accurate information and to disregard false reports circulating online. This incident highlights the need for accurate and timely reporting, especially in situations where misinformation can lead to panic and speculation.
