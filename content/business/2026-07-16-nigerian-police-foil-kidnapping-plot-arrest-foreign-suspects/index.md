## Foiling a Kidnapping Plot: Police in Kwara State Thwart Attempt by Foreign Suspects

In a daring operation, the Kwara State Police Command has successfully foiled a kidnapping plot, arresting two foreign nationals accused of threatening to abduct a resident unless he paid a ransom of ₦22 million. The suspects, Yakubu Ismaila and Hadiza Kosia, are from the Republic of Togo, and their arrest is a testament to the authorities' commitment to ensuring public safety.

According to police spokesperson Adetoun Ejire-Adeyemi, the suspects were apprehended after the complainant, S. Ismaila, reported receiving repeated threatening telephone calls. The caller had threatened to abduct him and kill his younger brother, Musa Ismaila, if the ransom was not paid. The Okuta Police Division, collaborating with the Intelligence Response Team (IRT), successfully traced the suspects using technical intelligence.

The investigation revealed that the suspects had allegedly used a mobile phone to make the threatening calls, which was recovered from one of them. This incident highlights the growing concern over the use of technology in facilitating crimes, particularly kidnappings.
