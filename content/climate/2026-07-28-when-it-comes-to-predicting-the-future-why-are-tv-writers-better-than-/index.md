The Rapture, an apocalypse drama that premiered in 2026, was adapted from Liz Jensen's 2009 novel and features a UK engulfed in a terrifying heatwave. The show's preview, watched in May, coincided with the onset of the highest ever recorded June temperature in the UK, rendering the fiction uncomfortably prescient. The heatwave's impact was so severe that even the door handles and street furniture in central London became unbearably hot, a stark reminder of the devastating effects of climate change.

The show's creator, Paul Rutman, committed to a narrative that was both prescient and unsettling, highlighting the dangers of a riven politics that has yet to be fully confronted.
