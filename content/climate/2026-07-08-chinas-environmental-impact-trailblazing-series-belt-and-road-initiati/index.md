## China's Global Environmental Impact in the Spotlight

A recent series of investigative reports by Inside Climate News has shed light on the often-hidden environmental consequences of China's trillion-dollar global development push. The trailblazing series, which spans four continents, has been recognized as a finalist for the prestigious John B. Oakes Award for Distinguished Environmental Journalism.

Through in-depth reporting from Indonesia, Ecuador, Argentina, Peru, Zambia, Zimbabwe, and Hungary, the "Planet China" series revealed the devastating impacts of China's Belt and Road Initiative on the environment and public health. From threatening one of Earth's largest glacial ice fields in Patagonia to displacing local communities in Zambia, the consequences of China's globalization efforts are far-reaching and alarming.

Chinese officials have hailed the Belt and Road Initiative as a "win-win partnership" with countries long oppressed by Western exploitation. However, Inside Climate News' findings paint a starkly different picture. The series highlights the urgent need for more nuanced and critical reporting on China's environmental impact, particularly in areas where local journalists have faced intimidation and suppression for speaking out against the problems caused by Belt and Road projects.

## A Complex Web of Environmental Consequences

The "Planet China" series is notable for its comprehensive approach, which goes beyond highlighting specific project impacts to expose the larger systems of policies and practices that prioritize speed over caution. This approach is crucial in understanding the intricacies of China's globalization efforts and the environmental costs they entail.

For instance, the series reveals how Chinese-built dams threaten the world's most endangered great ape in Indonesia and imperil one of Earth's largest glacial ice fields in Patagonia. In Zambia, a Belt and Road mine's waste-pit spill wiped out local livelihoods, while in Peru, a new Chinese-backed megaport could tip the Amazon rainforest over the edge.

## A Critical Role for Independent Reporting

The Oakes Award judges praised Inside Climate News' reporting as "trailblazing" and "exceptionally contributory to the public's understanding of environmental issues." The recognition underscores the critical role of independent reporting in shedding light on the environmental consequences of globalization.

However, the challenges faced by journalists in reporting on these issues are significant. In many Belt and Road countries, local journalists have faced intimidation and pressure from authorities to downplay or suppress reporting on environmental problems. The "Planet China" series is a testament to the importance of fearless and rigorous reporting in holding polluters accountable and exposing environmental injustice.
