The influx of migrants has put an unprecedented strain on the continent's resources, with many countries struggling to accommodate the sheer numbers of people seeking shelter. The situation has been exacerbated by the EU's fragmented response to the crisis, with member states adopting varying strategies to address the issue. The escalating tensions have led to a sharp increase in oil prices, which has had a devastating impact on Europe's economies, particularly in countries like Greece and Italy that are heavily reliant on imported energy.

The economic consequences of the crisis are being felt across the continent, with many businesses and industries struggling to stay afloat amidst the uncertainty.
