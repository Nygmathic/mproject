The stakes are high, not only for the future of the Boundary Waters, but also for the people who call this region home. However, environmentalists warn that the mine poses a significant threat to the fragile ecosystem of the Boundary Waters. In recent years, the debate over mining in the Boundary Waters has taken on a new dimension, with the rise of climate change.
