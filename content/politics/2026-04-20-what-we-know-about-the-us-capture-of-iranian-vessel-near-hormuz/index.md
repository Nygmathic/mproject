## A Show of Force in the Gulf

The incident has sparked a heated debate over maritime law, territorial sovereignty, and the implications for regional stability.

At the heart of the controversy lies the US claim that the Maersk Fortune was attempting to circumvent the maritime blockade, which has been in place since 2019, aimed at restricting Iranian oil exports. The US has maintained that the blockade is a legitimate measure to curb Tehran's nuclear ambitions and support for militant groups in the region. However, Iran has consistently rejected the blockade as an act of aggression, arguing that it has a legitimate right to export its oil and engage in international trade.
