## Miraculous Escape on the Hume Highway

The Hume Highway, a major artery connecting Sydney to Melbourne, was transformed into a scene of utter devastation Tuesday morning, as six truck drivers "miraculously" emerged nearly unscathed from an explosive crash involving semi-trailers carrying tonnes of flammable materials. The incident, which occurred near Breadalbane in New South Wales' southern tablelands, has left authorities and emergency services stunned by the lack of serious injuries.

According to Acting Insp Vladimir Mijok, the six male truck drivers, who have now been accounted for, were fortunate to escape with minor injuries, despite being involved in a horrific collision. The incident, which occurred just after 5am, saw two trucks collide while entering a roadside rest stop, resulting in a chain reaction of events that would have been catastrophic in any other circumstances.

"It is nothing short of a miracle that all six drivers managed to make it out and none of them with serious injuries either," Mijok said, echoing the sentiments of emergency services personnel who were left grappling with the scale of the destruction.

The six trucks, which included one carrying eight tonnes of butane cans, were completely destroyed in the ensuing blaze, which was eventually brought under control by Fire and Rescue NSW. The fire, which was fueled by the flammable materials on board the trucks, posed a significant challenge for firefighters, who deployed seven fire trucks, including two hazardous materials vehicles, and up to 40 firefighters to the scene.

The Hume Highway, which was closed in both directions as of 11am, remained shut down, with motorists advised to allow for an additional hour of travel time due to diversions through Canberra via the Federal and Barton highways. The road closure is expected to remain in place for some time, given the extent of debris and potentially hazardous materials left in the wake of the crash.

The crash investigation unit will assist with investigations, as a crime scene has been established. The incident has sparked widespread concern, not only for the safety of truck drivers but also for the environmental and public health implications of the crash.

While the investigation is ongoing, authorities have been quick to praise the bravery and quick thinking of the truck drivers, who managed to escape the inferno with relative ease.
