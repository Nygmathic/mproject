Tensions in the Middle East have reached a boiling point as Iran's strikes on ships in the Strait of Hormuz have raised the specter of a catastrophic disruption to the global oil supply. The attacks, which follow a period of relative calm, have sparked a heated response from the United States, which has re-imposed sanctions on Iran.

The strikes, which have targeted several vessels in the strategic waterway, have sent shockwaves through the global energy market, with analysts warning of a potentially disastrous impact on oil prices. Any significant disruption to this flow could have far-reaching consequences for economies around the world.
