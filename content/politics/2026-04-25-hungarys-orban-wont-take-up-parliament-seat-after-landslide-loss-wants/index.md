## A Leader's Exit Strategy

Hungary's Viktor Orban, the man who has dominated Hungarian politics for over three decades, announced that he would forego his parliamentary seat following his party's crushing defeat in the recent elections. The decision marks a significant turning point in the country's politics, as Orban attempts to redefine his role and steer his party towards a path of "renewal." His move has sent shockwaves throughout the nation, raising questions about the future of Fidesz, the party he has led to unprecedented success, and the trajectory of Hungarian politics.

Orban's decision to step back from parliament may seem counterintuitive, given his party's landslide defeat in the elections. Fidesz, which has been Orban's power base for over a decade, lost its majority in the parliament, marking a significant shift in the country's political landscape. However, Orban's reasoning behind his decision is rooted in his desire to lead a process of "renewal" within his party and the country. He believes that his presence in parliament would not be necessary, given the party's reduced numbers and the need for a new, more inclusive approach to governance.

Orban's 36-year tenure in Hungarian politics has been marked by significant achievements and controversies. Since the fall of Communism in 1989, he has been a dominant force in the country's legislature, serving as premier for the past 16 years. His leadership has been characterized by a strong nationalist tone, a robust economy, and a distinctive approach to EU politics. Orban's populist rhetoric and his party's increasingly authoritarian tendencies have, however, been a subject of concern for many within the European Union, who have accused him of undermining democratic norms and eroding the rule of law.

Orban's desire for renewal within Fidesz and the country is a response to the party's electoral defeat and the changing landscape of Hungarian politics. The party's loss of its parliamentary majority has exposed deep divisions within its ranks, with some members calling for a more inclusive approach to governance and others advocating for a return to the party's traditional nationalist stance. Orban's leadership, which has been characterized by a strong sense of control and direction, is seen by some as a key factor in the party's defeat. His decision to step back from parliament may be an attempt to rebrand Fidesz and reposition the party for success in future elections.

Orban's efforts to redefine his role and lead a process of renewal within Fidesz are not without precedent. The party has undergone significant changes in the past, including a shift from a more liberal, centrist stance to a more nationalist, populist one. Orban's leadership has been instrumental in this transformation, and his decision to step back from parliament may be a recognition that the party needs to adapt to changing circumstances and respond to the concerns of its voters. The challenge facing Orban and Fidesz is to balance their nationalist rhetoric with a more inclusive, pragmatic approach to governance, one that addresses the economic and social concerns of Hungarians.

The reactions to Orban's decision have been mixed, with some welcoming his efforts to lead a process of renewal within Fidesz and others expressing concern about the party's future direction. The European Union, which has been critical of Orban's leadership and his party's authoritarian tendencies, has yet to comment on his decision. However, some analysts believe that Orban's move may be an opportunity for the EU to re-engage with Hungary and promote a more inclusive, democratic approach to governance.

As Orban embarks on this new chapter in his leadership, many are watching with interest to see how he will navigate the complex web of Hungarian politics. His decision to step back from parliament marks a significant shift in the country's politics, one that has far-reaching implications for the future of Fidesz and the trajectory of Hungarian politics. Orban's leadership is a key factor in this shift, and his ability to redefine his role and lead a process of renewal within his party will be crucial in determining the country's future direction.
