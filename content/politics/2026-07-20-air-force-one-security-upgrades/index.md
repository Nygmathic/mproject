The exact timeframe for the upgrades and the plane's expected return to service has not been disclosed.

The Qatari-donated plane has been at the center of a contentious debate over its suitability for presidential transportation. Critics have raised concerns about the country's diplomatic ties and potential security risks associated with the aircraft's foreign origin. Advocates, on the other hand, have argued that the plane offers unparalleled comfort and luxury for high-ranking officials.

Air Force officials have consistently maintained that the Qatari-donated plane meets all necessary safety standards and has undergone rigorous testing and evaluation procedures. However, critics argue that the aircraft's security features fall short of what is required for the safety of U.S. presidents and other high-ranking officials.

The decision to remove the Air Force One from service has sparked a heated debate within the U.S. government and among lawmakers. Some have called for greater transparency and accountability in the decision-making process, while others have expressed concerns about the potential impact on the country's diplomatic relationships.

As the debate continues, officials will be working closely with contractors and security experts to upgrade the aircraft's defenses. The upgraded plane is expected to return to service with enhanced security features, addressing lingering concerns about its suitability for presidential transportation.
