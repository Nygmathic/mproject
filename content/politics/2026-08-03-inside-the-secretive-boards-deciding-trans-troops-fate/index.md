The lawyer's account paints a troubling picture of a system that prioritizes expediency over due process, where the rights and welfare of transgender service members are callously subordinated to the perceived imperatives of national security. At the heart of this issue lies the contentious policy of banning transgender individuals from serving in the military, a decision that has sparked heated debate and a series of high-stakes court challenges.

Moreover, the lawyer alleges that the hearings often fail to provide due process, with transgender service members being denied the opportunity to adequately defend themselves against the allegations being leveled against them.

The use of closed-door hearings in this context is particularly problematic, as it denies transgender service members the right to a fair and transparent process. By limiting access to these proceedings, the military is effectively gagging the very individuals who are most affected by the policy, while also preventing independent observers from monitoring the process and holding officials accountable for their actions.
