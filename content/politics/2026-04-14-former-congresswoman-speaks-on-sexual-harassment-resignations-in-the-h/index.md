As the morning sunlight streamed through the windows of the House of Representatives, casting a pale glow over the empty chamber, a sense of quiet desperation hung in the air. But the fallout of this changing culture extends far beyond the confines of the House of Representatives. The #MeToo movement has sparked a global conversation about power, consent, and accountability, with far-reaching implications for the way we think about politics, governance, and human relationships. As the boundaries between the public and private spheres continue to blur, the imperative for greater transparency and accountability has never been more urgent. For Speier, the resignations of her former colleagues are a welcome development, a testament to the power of collective action and the unwavering commitment of women and marginalized communities to creating a more just and equitable society.
