## Context: A Global Health Concern

Hantavirus is a rare but potentially deadly virus that can cause severe respiratory illness. The disease is usually spread through contact with infected rodents, their urine, or their feces. While it is not typically transmitted from person to person, the close quarters on a cruise ship create a unique environment where the virus can easily spread. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) has identified Hantavirus as a "serious public health concern" and has been working closely with state and local health authorities to contain the outbreak.
