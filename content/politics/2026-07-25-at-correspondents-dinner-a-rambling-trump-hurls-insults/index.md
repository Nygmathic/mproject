At a gala where the nation's press corps typically comes together to poke fun at itself, President Trump hijacked the Correspondents' Dinner with a rambling, vitriolic address that left the audience in stunned silence.

The annual event, a long-standing tradition of levity and camaraderie between journalists and the administration, has been strained in recent years. This year's dinner was no exception. President Trump, despite being the guest of honor, used his keynote speech to launch a scathing attack on the press, veering wildly off-script and departing from the evening's lighthearted tone.

President Trump's address, which lasted over 45 minutes, was peppered with personal and professional insults directed at various journalists and news organizations. He repeatedly referenced the Russia investigation, branding it a "witch hunt" and calling Special Counsel Robert Mueller a "highly conflicted" individual. The President also singled out CNN's Jim Acosta, repeatedly targeting him with personal jabs and mocking his news organization.

The audience, comprised of some of the nation's most respected journalists and politicians, was visibly taken aback by the President's tirade. Many attendees looked on in discomfort, while others were seen whispering to one another and glancing at their watches. The evening's usual jests and humor, which typically provide a welcome respite from the pressures of the job, were noticeably absent.
