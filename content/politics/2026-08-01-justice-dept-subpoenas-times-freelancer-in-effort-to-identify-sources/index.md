The article cited sources within the US military and intelligence community, but the exact details of the operation remain classified.

The Justice Department's subpoena, which was first reported last week, seeks to compel the Times freelancer to reveal the identities of the sources who contributed to the article. The move is part of an expanding campaign by the government to investigate and prosecute leaks of sensitive information. The Times freelancer at the center of the controversy has refused to comply with the subpoena, citing the journalist's privilege to protect sources.
