## Investigation into Football Association Deepens as Police Raid Offices

South Korean police have carried out a raid on the offices of the Korea Football Association (KFA) as part of an investigation into the appointment process of former coach Hong Myung-bo. The move follows a parliamentary inquiry into the matter, with lawmakers questioning whether Hong received special treatment in his reappointment to the role.

His appointment was the subject of controversy when it emerged that the KFA had abandoned a months-long search for a foreign coach and instead appointed him following a brief meeting. The decision was seen as unusual, given the extensive search process that had been undertaken.

The controversy surrounding Hong's appointment resurfaced after South Korea's disappointing FIFA World Cup campaign, which ended in a group-stage exit. At a parliamentary hearing last month, Hong rejected suggestions that he had received special treatment in his appointment. However, he did take responsibility for the team's failure at the tournament.
