## The Reflecting Pool's Troubled Waters

However, experts have long pointed out that the stagnant, shallow water – a result of the pool's design – is a perfect breeding ground for algae. Moreover, the presence of duck droppings and phosphate in the water serves as a natural fertilizer, exacerbating the problem.

The president's account of the polyurethane peeling off the pool's surface is equally vague, with him attributing it to someone having "sliced the pool with probably a box cutter or a knife of some kind."
