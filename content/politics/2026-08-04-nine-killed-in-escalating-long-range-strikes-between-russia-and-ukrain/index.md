## Escalating Conflict: Russia and Ukraine Trade Heavy Blows

At least nine people have been killed in a series of long-range strikes between Russia and Ukraine, with civilians bearing the brunt of the escalating violence. The attacks have targeted warehouses, oil refineries, and residential areas, resulting in a mounting toll of non-combatant casualties on both sides.

The strikes have been characterised by their precision and scope, with Ukrainian drones and Russian missiles hitting critical infrastructure and civilian targets. In one incident, a Ukrainian drone struck a warehouse in the Moscow region, killing five people and injuring 10. Regional Governor Andrei Vorobyov confirmed the attack, which was one of three overnight attacks on warehouses across Russia. Another warehouse belonging to e-commerce giant Wildberries was also struck by drones in the Tver region, northwest of Moscow, sparking a blaze.

The targeting of Wildberries' warehouses is particularly significant, as the company has been accused by Kyiv of playing a key role in Moscow's war effort by supplying dual-use items that can also be used for military purposes. In response, the company banned smartphones on Monday, prohibiting employees from sharing images or footage that could be analysed by Ukrainian authorities to better bypass Russian air defences near Wildberries' warehouses. Employees are now only permitted to carry phones without cameras.

In Ukraine, the situation is equally dire. At least three people, including two children, were killed in Russian strikes on the northern city of Sumy. An elderly woman was also killed in an overnight attack on the southern Ukrainian port city of Mykolaiv, near the Black Sea. Separately, Russia's state-run news agency RIA reported that Russian forces struck seven cargo vessels in the city's port.

The number of non-combatants killed in both Russia and Ukraine is continuing to rise, with Monday's attack on a beach in Russia's southern Krasnodar region resulting in seven fatalities and over 50 injuries. Reports suggest that the drone was intercepted, causing it to crash into the beach. Ukraine has not commented on the strike, but has repeatedly said it does not deliberately target civilian sites.

The conflict has also taken a toll on international relations. The country's maritime authority reported that a Turkish cargo ship was attacked by a drone in Russia's Black Sea port of Novorossiysk, resulting in three serious injuries among the crew. All 22 crew members were evacuated following the incident.

However, he cautioned that his goal might not be achievable, accusing Putin of attempting to "continue dragging out" the war.
