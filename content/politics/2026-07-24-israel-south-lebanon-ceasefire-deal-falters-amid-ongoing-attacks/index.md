## Explosive Consequences of a 'Ceasefire' in South Lebanon

The southern Lebanese town of al-Mansouri was shaken on Friday when Israeli forces launched a surprise attack, sending shockwaves through the nearby village of Majdal Zoun. The explosion, reportedly heard as far away as the city of Tyre, some 15 kilometers away, marks a stark reminder that, despite ongoing talks between the two countries, the 'ceasefire' deal brokered by the United States has yet to materialize.

This latest incident is merely the latest in a long series of attacks and demolitions that have become a grim reality for those living in southern Lebanon. The Israeli army's systematic levelling of entire villages near the border has left residents grappling with the uncertain future. According to Israeli officials, the objective of these actions is to erase Lebanese towns from existence, creating a 'security zone' in their place.

The implications of this strategy are far-reaching, and eerily reminiscent of Israel's tactics in Gaza. The situation is further complicated by the lack of a clear timeline for Israel's withdrawal from [occupied territories](/tags/occupied-territories/). US President Donald Trump's promise to "help" Lebanon during a meeting with President Joseph Aoun earlier this week has done little to alleviate concerns. When pressed on the issue of a withdrawal timeline, Trump merely stated that Israel is "in the process of doing that," without providing any concrete details.

The deal signed in Washington on June 26, which outlines the framework for a 'ceasefire' and eventual peace, has been met with skepticism by many. Hezbollah's leader, Naim Qassem, has flatly rejected the agreement, labeling it "null and void" and calling for armed resistance to push Israel out of Lebanon. The Lebanese army's deployment to the first pilot-zone villages this week has done little to ease tensions, with residents returning to Froun and Ghandouriyeh claiming that their villages were never occupied by Israeli forces to begin with.

The lack of progress in implementing the deal has significant implications for the region. The Lebanese government's goal to end hostilities with Israel "forever" remains a distant dream, and the ongoing attacks and demolitions are a stark reminder of the challenges ahead.
