## Escalating Violence in Gaza: Israeli Strikes Kill Two Palestinians

Amidst the fragile ceasefire that has been in place since October 2025, Israel's military has intensified its strikes on the Gaza Strip, killing at least two Palestinians and sparking widespread destruction. The latest escalation, which began with displacement orders for several areas in Gaza, has left residents facing a daunting and uncertain future.

The Israeli military's actions have been met with condemnation from Arab and Muslim countries, with Egypt's Foreign Minister, Badr Abdelatty, urging the United States to accelerate the deployment of an international security force in Gaza. The Egyptian call for action coincides with reports that Morocco has dispatched 30 officers to prepare for the deployment of security personnel as part of the International Stabilization Force.

The humanitarian situation in Gaza remains dire, with an estimated 1.4 million people, representing 67 percent of the territory's population, experiencing crisis or worse levels of acute food insecurity. The United Nations World Food Programme's director, Ross Smith, has described the situation as "critical and desperate," highlighting the need for continued attention and support to alleviate the suffering of the Gazan people.

The UK Foreign Office has joined the chorus of criticism, demanding that Israel end restrictions on humanitarian support for people in Gaza. The office has emphasized that averting famine cannot be the benchmark for success, and has condemned Israeli aid restrictions for driving disease, water scarcity, and decimating health services.

As the international community grapples with the complexities of the Gaza situation, the Israeli military's actions have raised questions about the efficacy of the ceasefire. The strikes on Thursday are the latest in a series of deadly escalations, despite the United States-brokered agreement that aimed to bring peace to the region.

The situation on the ground is stark, with families being forced to flee their homes and seek shelter elsewhere. Al Jazeera's Ibrahim al-Khalili has reported that a strike on Gaza City sparked a huge fire that Civil Defence crews are still trying to extinguish. The destruction has been widespread, with Israeli air strikes targeting homes in the Bureij and Jabalia refugee camps, as well as in the Zeitoun and Tal al-Hawa neighbourhoods of Gaza City.

For the people of Gaza, the displacement orders issued by the Israeli military have meant more than just moving from one place to another; it has meant abandoning whatever shelter they have managed to find, often with little notice and in the dead of night. Al-Khalili's account highlights the trauma and fear that has become an all too familiar reality for the people of Gaza.

As the international community continues to grapple with the challenges of the Gaza situation, the need for sustained attention and support has never been more pressing. The deployment of the International Stabilization Force, as well as the urgent need to address the humanitarian crisis, will be crucial in preventing further escalation and promoting a lasting resolution to the conflict.

The path forward is fraught with uncertainty, but one thing is clear: the people of Gaza cannot afford to wait any longer for a solution. The international community must come together to address the humanitarian crisis, support the deployment of the International Stabilization Force, and work towards a lasting peace that acknowledges the rights and dignity of all parties involved.
