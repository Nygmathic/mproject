## **Rising Tides, Rising Stakes**

Hong Kong's labyrinthine streets and densely packed neighborhoods are no strangers to the fury of typhoons and torrential rains. But as the city's residents prepare for another onslaught of extreme weather events, a new era of flood forecasting is about to change the game. At the forefront of this revolution is a cutting-edge artificial intelligence (AI)-driven flood alert system, poised to revolutionize the way authorities and residents alike respond to flooding. This latest innovation is the culmination of a multi-year effort to enhance Hong Kong's flood risk management capabilities, and it promises to be a game-changer in a city perennially on the front lines of climate change.

At its core, the new system is an AI-powered tool that leverages advanced data analytics and three-dimensional simulations to predict flood-prone areas, providing authorities with crucial hours – or even minutes – of warning time. This, in turn, enables them to deploy emergency services and resources more effectively, reducing the risk of casualties and damage to property. The technology has been integrated into the existing territory-wide flood risk visualisation system, which was first deployed during Super Typhoon Ragasa last year. This system's success in providing critical insights and supporting decision-making has laid the groundwork for the AI-driven upgrade, which promises to take flood forecasting to an entirely new level.
