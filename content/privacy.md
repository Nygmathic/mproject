**Veridus Post** ("Veridus Post," "we," "us," or "our") operates the digital publication accessible at **veriduspost.com** (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what limited information is processed when you read or interact with the Site, how that information is used and protected, and what rights you have regarding your personal data.

By accessing or using the Site, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please discontinue use of the Site.

## 1. Privacy-First Architecture & Data Minimization

Veridus Post is designed around data minimization. You may read our reporting, browse section archives, and search our catalog without creating an account, logging in, or submitting personal details. We do **not** run third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking pixels, cross-site retargeting scripts, or commercial analytics cookies.

## 2. Information We Collect

We process only the minimum information necessary to operate the publication reliably and fulfill requests you initiate:

- **Newsletter Subscriptions:** If you voluntarily subscribe to our Morning Briefing, we collect the email address you submit. You may unsubscribe at any time by writing to [contact@veriduspost.com](mailto:contact@veriduspost.com).
- **Direct Correspondence & Corrections:** If you email us with editorial tips, press inquiries, correction requests, legal notices, or supporter inquiries (including alternative giving arrangements such as bank transfers or M-Pesa), we retain your email address, message contents, and any attachments you choose to send solely to review and respond to your communication.
- **Technical & Server Access Logs:** Like all internet servers, our hosting infrastructure automatically records standard technical request metadata—such as IP address, timestamp, requested URL path, HTTP status code, browser user-agent string, and referring page—strictly for service availability, rate limiting, security monitoring, fraud prevention, and abuse mitigation.
- **Local Browser Preferences & Search:** To remember your light or dark display preference, your browser stores a functional key (`veridus-theme`) locally via `localStorage`. It contains no personal identifiers and is never transmitted to third parties. Site search inspects our locally served article index directly in your browser without sending search keystrokes to external search vendors.

## 3. How We Use Information & Legal Bases

Where applicable data protection laws require a legal basis (such as the EU/UK GDPR), we rely on:

- **Consent:** Delivering email briefings when you actively subscribe.
- **Legitimate Interests:** Maintaining editorial infrastructure, defending against automated scraping, denial-of-service attacks, or unauthorized access, and responding to reader or press communications.
- **Legal Obligations & Defense:** Complying with valid court orders, subpoenas, regulatory mandates, or establishing, exercising, and defending legal claims.

## 4. Third-Party Platforms & External Links

Articles and support pages contain outbound links to external platforms, including supporter checkout services (**Patreon**, **Buy Me a Coffee**) and social sharing destinations (**X**, **Facebook**, **LinkedIn**, **Bluesky**, **WhatsApp**, **Reddit**, or your email client).

- Clicking a third-party link or making a financial contribution on an external platform subjects you exclusively to that third party's privacy policy, cookie practices, and terms of service.
- Veridus Post does not receive, process, or store your payment card numbers or banking credentials submitted through third-party supporter platforms.
- We encourage you to review the privacy notices of any external site you visit.

## 5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do **not** sell, rent, lease, or trade reader personal information to data brokers, advertisers, or third parties. We share information only in the following narrow circumstances:

- **Infrastructure & Email Providers:** Trusted hosting, DNS, security, or email delivery providers acting strictly on our instructions and subject to confidentiality and security obligations.
- **Legal Compliance & Protection:** When required by applicable law, subpoena, or enforceable governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, enforce our [Terms of Use](/terms/), protect journalistic sources, or safeguard the rights, property, and safety of Veridus Post, our contributors, or our readers.
- **Organizational Continuity:** In connection with a reorganization, transfer, or stewardship transition of the publication, subject to continued adherence to privacy protections materially consistent with this policy.

## 6. Data Retention & Security

Technical access logs are rotated and purged on short operational schedules unless retained temporarily to investigate a security incident or legal claim. Email correspondence and newsletter records are kept only as long as needed to maintain your subscription, resolve your inquiry, or satisfy legal recordkeeping obligations.

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and transport-level safeguards (including HTTPS encryption in transit) to protect information under our control. However, no internet transmission or storage system is completely immune from risk, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. International Transfers

Because the Site is accessible globally and relies on distributed cloud infrastructure, technical request data or correspondence may be processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence, including the United States and the European Economic Area. Where required by law, we rely on lawful transfer mechanisms to protect such data.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or comparable laws), you may have the right to:

- Request access to personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate information or deletion of your personal data.
- Withdraw consent (including opting out of newsletter emails) or object to certain processing.
- Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory or data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email [contact@veriduspost.com](mailto:contact@veriduspost.com) with sufficient detail for us to verify your request.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for a general adult readership and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law applies). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us, contact [contact@veriduspost.com](mailto:contact@veriduspost.com) and we will promptly delete it.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect legal, operational, or technical changes. Updates take effect immediately when published on this page.
