A pair of tornadoes has hit Marsala, a coastal town located in western Sicily, resulting in dozens of injuries and widespread structural damage. The intense weather overturned cars and severely damaged several buildings throughout the area, including a local school. Video recordings circulating on Friday morning captured two waterspouts moving inward from the sea toward the Sicilian town while residents braced for their arrival.

The impact of the storm led to numerous injuries across the area. At least 11 individuals required hospital treatment after being struck by shattered glass and airborne debris. The most severely injured victim identified was a 70-year-old man, who was taken to Mazara del Vallo hospital in critical condition with multiple fractures.

The storm also struck an educational facility while 273 pupils were present inside the building. Despite the powerful winds hitting the school, all of the students were successfully evacuated to safety. Six children suffered minor cuts and abrasions during the event. Aerial footage gathered by drones following the tornado showed significant destruction to the school premises, highlighting flipped chairs, scattered desks, and blown-out windows.

Such storm systems are capable of producing violent winds exceeding 60 miles per hour along with sea waves as high as five meters. Meteorologically, this recent weather system formed due to a combination of intense heat over North Africa and elevated water temperatures across the Mediterranean Sea.
