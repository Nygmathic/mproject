A three-day period of national mourning has been declared in Nigeria by President Bola Tinubu following a fatal military plane crash in southern Ondo State. The Nigerian Air Force confirmed on Tuesday that 25 people lost their lives in the accident, including five crew members and 20 passengers, 19 of whom were military personnel. The fatality figure was revised down from an earlier statement on Monday by Aviation Minister Festus Keyamo, who had initially reported 32 deaths.

The crashed aircraft, identified as an ATR-42 with the tail number NGR 931, was conducting a routine flight from Benin City to Lagos when it came down in a swamp near a naval base in Igbokoda on Monday morning. Keyamo noted that attempts to contact the plane via radio at 09:13 hours were unsuccessful prior to confirmation of the crash. Responders arriving at the site discovered the plane submerged in water, while a local resident informed BBC News that rising smoke, clothing, and footwear were visible at the scene. Search and rescue operations remain active.

In response to the disaster, President Tinubu ordered national flags to be flown at half-mast across the country. Expressing his condolences to the families of those killed, Tinubu described the event as a "painful moment for our Armed Forces and for the entire nation." He emphasized the continuous sacrifices made by Air Force personnel to protect Nigeria and directed the military branch to immediately conduct a comprehensive investigation to determine what caused the crash and prevent future incidents.

The event marks the deadliest crash involving Nigerian military aircraft since 1992. According to its manufacturer, the ATR-42 model typically carries between 30 and 50 passengers. The Air Force plays a vital role in national defense, particularly in addressing insecurity and combating armed groups throughout the country. This incident follows other recent military aviation tragedies, including a February 2021 crash near a runway in the capital, Abuja, that claimed seven lives, and a May 2021 crash in Kaduna State caused by bad weather that killed Army Chief Lt-Gen Ibrahim Attahiru along with 10 officers.
