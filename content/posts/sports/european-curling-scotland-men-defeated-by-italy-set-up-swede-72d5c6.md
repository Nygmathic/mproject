Scotland's men's curling team lost their undefeated round-robin record at the European Curling Championships after a 6-5 defeat against Italy. Skip Grant Hardie and his rink completed Group A in second place behind Stefano Spiller's Italian side. The outcome sets up a semi-final matchup for Scotland against unbeaten Group B winners Sweden, skipped by world and Olympic champion Oskar Eriksson. Sweden confirmed top spot in their group by defeating Poland 7-2. Meanwhile, Italy will meet the Czech Republic in the other last-four match. Lukas Klima's Czech team qualified as Group B runners-up despite falling 8-4 to Sergio Vez's Spanish lineup in their second round-robin game.

Throughout their contest with Italy, the Scottish squad was forced to play catch-up after giving up two points in the opening end. Hardie's rink eventually moved ahead 4-3 following the sixth end, but Italy answered by scoring two points in the seventh. Scotland subsequently leveled the match to force an extra end, where lead Ross Whyte saw his final stone fall short to concede the match.

In addition to the men's semi-final, Scotland's women's team will also compete in the last four, facing Switzerland at 12:30 GMT. Both semi-final encounters will be streamed live on the BBC iPlayer and online. Fay Henderson's Scottish rink secured their place in the semi-finals with a match to spare following a tight extra-end triumph against Estonia on Thursday.

That victory came after Henderson's team suffered their first group-stage defeat, a 10-2 loss to Sweden. In the game against Estonia, the Scots mounted a comeback from an 8-3 deficit by scoring three points and stealing two more to force an extra end. Scotland then clinched a 9-8 win with another steal after Estonian skip Marie Kaldvee failed to clear a Scottish rock with a raise-takeout. The Scottish women completed their Group B matches in second place behind Isabella Wranaa's Sweden after defeating Lithuania 7-5. Switzerland topped Group A with five consecutive wins under Alina Paetz, a 2023 world and European gold medalist, finishing ahead of Evelina Barone's Latvian squad.
