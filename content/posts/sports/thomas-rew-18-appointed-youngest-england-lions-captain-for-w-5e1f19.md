Teenage wicketkeeper-batter Thomas Rew has been appointed captain of the England Lions for an upcoming three-match T20 series against West Indies A in St Kitts and Nevis this November. At 18 years old, he is expected to become the "youngest captain in Lions history" when he leads the squad, which also includes his older brother, James Rew. The leadership role capped a significant week for Thomas, who earned his first senior England call-up for a T20 series in Australia and was awarded a central development contract.

Rew’s rapid promotion reflects his high standing among the England hierarchy. Earlier this year, he captained the England Under-19 side to the World Cup final before scoring his first two first-class centuries for Somerset during the summer. Rew, who turns 19 on November 29, could achieve further milestones if he plays on the senior tour in Australia. An appearance there would make him the youngest player ever to represent England against Australia and the third-youngest English debutant in any international format, behind Rehan Ahmed and Brian Close.

Three players—Thomas Rew, Hampshire seamer Eddie Jack, and Sussex all-rounder James Coles—will participate in the Caribbean tour before traveling to Australia. Following the T20 fixtures, the Lions will play five one-day matches against West Indies A. Test batter Dan Lawrence will captain the one-day team, which includes Rew, his brother, and seamer Sam Cook. Both Lawrence and Cook are using the fixtures to prepare for the senior team's three-Test series in South Africa in December. Former England batter Jonathan Trott is directing the Lions' winter tours following the departure of head coach Andrew Flintoff.

The Lions T20 squad also features Hampshire seamer Scott Currie. Born in Poole, Currie previously played three one-day matches for Scotland in 2014, while his brother Brad has earned 39 caps for the Scottish national team. Scott was named in England's squad for a T20 series in Ireland last year; although he did not play, his role as an England substitute fielder in Dublin barred him from switching back to Scotland for this year's T20 World Cup. Additional players selected across the winter squads include Rehan Ahmed, Mason Crane, Adam Finch, Calvin Harrison, Jack Haynes, Ben Kellaway, Ben Mayes, Ben McKinney, Liam Patterson-White, Naavya Sharma, Ekansh Singh, and Asa Tribe.
