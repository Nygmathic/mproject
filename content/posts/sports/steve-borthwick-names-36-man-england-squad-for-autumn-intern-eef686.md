With less than a year remaining until the 2027 Rugby World Cup in Australia, England head coach Steve Borthwick has selected a 36-man training squad ahead of three November international matches. The home nations have just nine competitive fixtures left before the tournament begins next October. England are scheduled to open their autumn campaign against Australia on 8 November, followed by matches against Japan on 14 November and New Zealand on 21 November.

Borthwick’s selection combines established Test veterans with a small number of additions. Center Joe Marchant has earned a recall, while Will Joseph was selected following a strong start to the season with Gloucester. The squad also includes uncapped players Archie McParland as a third scrum-half option and Kepu Tuipulotu as a third hooker. However, speaking on the Rugby Union Weekly podcast, former England wing Chris Ashton noted that the group relies heavily on experience and existing relationships rather than recent domestic form, with several regular choices having played limited minutes this season.

Several in-form players were left out of the squad. Former player Ugo Monye pointed to the omissions of Sale hooker Nathan Jibulu, Northampton back George Hendy, and Bristol back-rower Fitz Harding. Meanwhile, injuries continue to affect Borthwick's team selection. Benhard Janse van Rensburg, Seb Atkinson, and Immanuel Feyi-Waboso are currently sidelined or undergoing rehabilitation. Additionally, fly-half Fin Smith was forced to miss training this week due to a calf issue, prompting the call-up of Charlie Atkinson on Monday.

While England’s forward line remains largely predictable if players are fit, selection in the backline remains less clear, particularly in the midfield. Marchant and Joseph are competing for the outside center position and both offer versatility on the wing. Seb Atkinson and Henry Slade, who formed the midfield partnership in England’s victory over Argentina in July, could potentially start together again in the opening match against Australia.

The squad will remain in training until Tuesday evening before returning to their respective clubs for one round of domestic league action. The players will reassemble next Sunday, one week prior to the Australia fixture. Following a difficult 2026 campaign in which England achieved only three wins from eight matches, Borthwick faces significant pressure to establish consistency during the upcoming November series.
