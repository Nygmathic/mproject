Bayern Munich bounced back from a two-match winless run with a commanding 6-1 victory against RB Leipzig at the Allianz Arena. The emphatic win followed a frustrating week that included conceding early goals away at Augsburg and Viking, alongside a red card for Michael Olise after being provoked into receiving two quick bookings in midweek. Leipzig, managed by former Bayern figure Martin Demichelis, struggled to contain the host's attack, with goalkeeper Maarten Vandevoordt preventing the scoreline from surpassing Leipzig's record 7-0 loss to Manchester City.

Head coach Vincent Kompany observed the match quietly from the bench, remaining seated through every goal. Speaking in the post-match press conference on his behalf, sporting director Max Eberl clarified that Kompany was suffering from illness alongside a hip issue that made moving around uncomfortable. 

On the field, Luis Díaz delivered a standout performance, beginning in the 12th minute when he converted a pass from Joshua Kimmich to open the scoring. Díaz then assisted Aleksandar Pavlovic for another goal, following an earlier strike from Olise created by Harry Kane. In the second half, Díaz added two more goals to complete his hat-trick, securing his first Bundesliga scores since August. Kimmich later praised Díaz's impact, pointing to his ability to penetrate defensive lines and reminding reporters that the winger provided 22 goals and 17 assists across domestic and European competition last season.

Díaz's form arrives at a vital time for Bayern as they prepare to face Arsenal on Wednesday. Olise will miss the European encounter due to his suspension. The match against the English side gives Bayern a chance to address their 3-1 loss at the Emirates Stadium 11 months prior, which stood as their first defeat of the previous campaign. Eberl noted that the team remembers that setback and intends to "translate those memories into energy" for the upcoming game.
