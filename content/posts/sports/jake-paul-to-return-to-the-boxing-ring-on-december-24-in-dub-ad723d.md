American influencer and professional boxer Jake Paul is scheduled to return to the ring on December 24 in Dubai, marking his first fight since suffering a defeat against Anthony Joshua. The 29-year-old will headline a Christmas Eve event in the United Arab Emirates, though his opponent has not yet been officially confirmed. The upcoming match follows a period of uncertainty regarding his athletic future, as Paul had previously indicated in May that his boxing career was in serious doubt.

Paul’s return comes after sustaining severe injuries during his heavyweight bout against Joshua in December 2025. In that match, which took place in Miami, the British two-time heavyweight world champion knocked Paul down twice before ending the fight via a sixth-round stoppage. The severe contest left Paul with a broken jaw, requiring medical procedures to install two titanium plates and remove several teeth. Furthermore, the defeat led to Paul losing his WBA cruiserweight ranking in January, an achievement he had secured in July 2025 following a victory over former world champion Julio Cesar Chavez Jr.

Despite these setbacks, Paul expressed strong confidence about his return, remarking that he remains "hungrier than ever" while looking to finalize a contract with one of several potential opponents. Throughout his professional boxing career, Paul has achieved 12 wins in 14 fights, with his only two losses coming against Tommy Fury and Joshua.

Speaking to the Sun, Most Valuable Promotions co-founder Nakisa Bidarian stated that the organization is currently finalizing a worthy opponent for Paul's return in the United Arab Emirates. Bidarian revealed that former UFC competitor Darren Till is among the candidates being considered. The 33-year-old Liverpudlian last fought in the UFC in 2022 and made his professional boxing debut in 2025, defeating Luke Rockhold in his sole contest. Till has since competed in the Bare Knuckle Fighting Championship, most recently defeating Yoel Romero in September.
