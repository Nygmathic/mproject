Former Republic of Ireland international James McClean has strongly criticized Derry City over an ongoing contract dispute, describing the League of Ireland team's attempts to cancel his contract as completely unacceptable. In a public statement shared through the Professional Footballers' Association of Ireland (PFAI) website, the 37-year-old expressed deep frustration with his treatment by his hometown club, insisting that he alone will decide the timing of his retirement.

Reports indicate that Derry City sought to end the player's contract due to ongoing injury concerns. The PFAI statement revealed that the club tried to utilize Section 18 of the Standard Players Contract, which allows for a contract to be terminated with six months' notice if an independent doctor determines a player has a "permanent incapacity." McClean strongly rejected this assessment, stating that he is nowhere near permanently incapacitated, trains as intensely as any teammate, and remains capable of performing well in the league.

Addressing his health, the PFAI explained that McClean had previously suffered from a hip injury that led to missed matches and training sessions. However, after seeking medical care, he is now healthy and fit. The union noted that McClean has participated in full training sessions over the past nine weeks, competed in an internal match organized by the club, and offered himself for selection, yet has received no satisfactory reason for his exclusion from matchday squads.

McClean rejoined Derry City from Wrexham in January during the close-season. He has not been selected for a squad since the team's Uefa Conference League defeat to Rijeka in early July, remaining sidelined under interim manager Mark Connolly. McClean acknowledged that recent months have marked one of the toughest times in his life and offered an apology for some misjudged actions, stating his frustrations should have stayed in-house.

When BBC Sport NI reached out to Derry City regarding the matter, a club spokesperson declined to comment directly on the dispute. The spokesperson stated that the club's focus is presently directed entirely toward their upcoming FAI Cup semi-final against Galway United on Saturday.
