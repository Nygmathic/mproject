British tennis player Harriet Dart secured the first WTA Tour singles title of her career by defeating Austrian teenager Lilli Tagger in the Guangzhou Open final. The 30-year-old emerged from the qualifying rounds of the WTA 250 event before earning a 4-6, 6-3, 6-4 victory in the final match.

During the deciding set, the 18-year-old Tagger twice erased leads held by Dart. However, Dart broke serve for a third time to lead 4-3 before holding serve to move within one game of victory. Serving at 5-4, the 53rd-ranked Tagger saved a championship point after Dart hit an inviting mid-court ball into the net on her second chance. Nevertheless, Dart converted her third championship point with a service winner to end the match.

Dart’s tournament run was highlighted by her resilience, as she recovered from dropping the opening set in four of her seven matches. This included the final against Tagger, who had previously won the 2025 French Open junior title and defeated seven-time major champion Venus Williams earlier in the event. Dart told BBC Sport that it had been "a wild week" and noted her pride in navigating the tournament's challenges.

Ranked 115th globally and standing as the British number five, Dart will return to the world's top 100 following her victory. The ranking boost guarantees her direct entry into the main draw of the Australian Open when the season's first Grand Slam takes place in January. Dart previously reached a career-high ranking of 70th in 2024 after advancing to the third round at Wimbledon and the second round at the US Open, though she spent much of the past two seasons outside the top 100.

A key factor in Dart's recent form has been her work with former Wimbledon champion Marion Bartoli. Since Wimbledon, Dart has recorded 19 wins in 24 tour-level matches played on hard courts. That sequence has brought her overall WTA Tour record for the season to 23 victories from 34 matches, representing the highest win percentage of her career.
