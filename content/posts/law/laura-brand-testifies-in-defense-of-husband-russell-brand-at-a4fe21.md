Laura Brand has defended her husband, entertainer Russell Brand, during his sexual assault trial at Southwark Crown Court in London, describing him as a compassionate spouse and devoted parent. The 39-year-old testified on Thursday in support of the media personality, who faces seven charges, including three counts of rape, three counts of sexual assault, and one count of attempted indecent assault. Brand denies all allegations and has pleaded not guilty.

Laura Brand, the daughter of golfer Bernard Gallacher and sister of television presenter Kirsty Gallacher, told the jury that the couple married in 2017 after originally meeting at the Edinburgh festival in 2006. Recalling the start of their relationship, she explained that they engaged in oral sex shortly after meeting, though she chose not to have full intercourse at the time. When asked by the defence whether her husband respected her decisions when she did not want to engage in sexual activity, she confirmed that he did. Brand, who has displayed little emotion during the proceedings, was seen smiling broadly and laughing at points during his wife's testimony.

Her evidence followed Brand's own turn in the witness box on Wednesday. The entertainer told jurors that the accusations had a devastating effect on his personal and professional relationships, leaving former friends and work colleagues unwilling to touch him "with a bargepole." He also stated during his testimony that he regards sexual consent as a fundamental and essential requirement.

The court also heard a statement on Thursday from Elia Alvarez Diaz, a former partner who lived with Brand for about a year between 1998 and 1999 after they met at a drama centre in London. She characterized Brand as persuasive, gentle, and affectionate, noting that he always accepted her refusal whenever she said no. She stated that their relationship ultimately ended because he repeatedly cheated on her.

The trial continues.
