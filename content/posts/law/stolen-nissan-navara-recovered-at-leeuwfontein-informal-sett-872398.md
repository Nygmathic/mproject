Law enforcement officers from the Tshwane Flying Squad have recovered a stolen black Nissan Navara after discovering it left abandoned within an informal settlement. The vehicle was found parked at the Leeuwfontein informal settlement, which is situated inside the boundary of the Kameeldrift policing area. The recovery occurred at approximately 10:30 a.m. on Saturday, during a period when police were carrying out visible patrols and general crime prevention operations across the area.

Police spokesperson Warrant Officer Frans Span stated that officers present at the scene conducted an immediate check on the registration number displayed on the vehicle. This verification process allowed law enforcement personnel to confirm that the Nissan Navara had previously been reported to authorities as stolen in Pretoria.

In response to the discovery, specialized personnel from the Local Criminal Record Centre, also known as the LCRC, were dispatched to the scene in the informal settlement to carry out further technical investigations. After these preliminary investigative procedures were completed on site, the recovered Navara was officially transported away from the location.

The vehicle was taken directly to the Pretoria West Vehicle Crime Investigation Unit, or VCIU, where officers formally booked it into police custody under SAPS 13. This specific system functions as the official police register used to track and record all property brought under the care of law enforcement.

According to Warrant Officer Span, official inquiries regarding the stolen vehicle are currently ongoing. Police investigators are attempting to gather more information about the incident, including establishing the exact circumstances of how the vehicle came to be left unattended at the settlement site. As noted in coverage by IOL News, no arrests have been reported by police in connection with the theft or the recovery of the vehicle.
