Four individuals are on trial at Newport Crown Court facing murder charges under the legal doctrine of joint enterprise following the fatal stabbing of a 17-year-old boy. Prosecutors state that 19-year-old Georgie Mears, from Thornhill, Cardiff, used a zombie knife to inflict the fatal injury on Tristan Kerr, from Abertridwr, Caerphilly, during an incident in February. Mears denies the charge of murder alongside three co-defendants: Connor Palfrey, 24, also of Abertridwr; Ricardo Elliot, 27, of no fixed address; and Elexi Manny, 24, from Senghenydd, Caerphilly.

The court heard that Kerr was visiting a friend's house on Lower Francis Street in Abertridwr to play Fortnite and smoke cannabis. Shortly after 17:30 BST, Manny drove the group to the address in her Volkswagen Polo. Mears allegedly entered the residence brandishing a purple and silver curved knife measuring roughly 10 inches with holes in the blade. According to Prosecutor Michael Jones KC, the entire attack took less than two minutes. Witness Connor Davies reported seeing Mears stab and swing at Kerr, who attempted to push his attacker away before the large blade penetrated his coat.

During the assault, Palfrey followed Mears inside while holding a wooden stick—which a witness noted may have contained a nail—above his head to intimidate bystanders and warn them against intervening. Prosecutors described Palfrey's role as acting like a "foreman" to prevent Kerr's friends from stepping in, while Elliot waited in the hallway to serve as a lookout. Kerr sustained a 13.5cm wound that damaged his heart and lung. After bleeding heavily, he collapsed in a friend's arms and was pronounced dead at the scene.

While the exact reason for the attack remains unclear, text messages exchanged between Palfrey and Elliot revealed involvement in dealing cocaine, ketamine, and cannabis, alongside discussions regarding the collection of drug debts. Palfrey had turned off his mobile phone approximately 40 minutes prior to the assault. Following the stabbing, Manny entered the residence before acting as the getaway driver. All four defendants were later arrested in Hereford, and the trial continues.
