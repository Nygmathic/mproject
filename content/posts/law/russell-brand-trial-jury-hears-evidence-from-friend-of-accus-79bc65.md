A jury has heard evidence from friends of a woman who alleges she was raped by Russell Brand, detailing her immediate distress following the reported incident. The 51-year-old comedian has denied all seven charges against him, which include three counts of rape, three charges of sexual assault, and one count of indecent assault. The allegations in the trial cover a ten-year timeframe between 1999 and 2009 and involve six female complainants.

Giving evidence via video link, a friend of one complainant recalled going to Brand's comedy performance at the Hammersmith Apollo theatre in London in 2009. After the show, both women were invited to the performer's dressing room. Shortly after this encounter, Brand dispatched a car to bring the woman to his residence in Hampstead. The witness stated that when she spoke with her friend shortly after the visit, the woman was crying, severely distressed, and described Brand as aggressive. According to the witness, the complainant stated that it "really, really hurt", that she was in severe pain and unable to sit down, and that she had repeatedly told Brand no before he assaulted her.

A second friend of the same complainant testified directly from the witness box, recalling that the woman told her she instructed Brand to stop during the assault. The witness noted that her friend found the moments after the act particularly traumatic, describing Brand as cold and anxious to get her gone. She testified that Brand told the woman she had been dirty and needed to clean up her mess.

Following these disclosures, the second witness advised her friend to obtain a medical check-up for anal tears and physical discomfort. She also recommended that the woman report the matter to the police and seek guidance from her parents. However, the witness testified that her friend was unsure and felt overwhelmed, believing at the time that taking further action was not worth it.
