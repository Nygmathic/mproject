Jurors at Lewes Crown Court have been shown text messages sent by Katie Price to a teenager who alleges she was sexually assaulted and raped by Price's former husband, Kieran Hayler. Hayler is currently on trial regarding three counts of rape and one count of sexual assault targeting the girl in 2016 in West Sussex, when she was 13 years old. He denies all of the charges against him.

The court heard that Price messaged the girl when she was 14 years old after receiving information that Hayler had kissed her and made improper advances. In the exchange, Price emphasized that it was a serious matter and requested the truth, offering: "I will make you a little star." At the time, the teenager answered that nothing had happened and characterized the interactions as flirting, noting she had viewed his remarks about liking her as a joke. Questioned in court about these earlier statements, the complainant, now an adult, testified that she had been attempting to brush off the situation and chose not to respond to Price's offer.

The proceedings also addressed deleted text messages between Hayler and the complainant. The jury was told that Hayler sent an affectionate text following the third alleged rape, to which the girl sent a warm reply. Defense counsel Arlette Piercy argued that the sexual offenses did not occur and disputed that these messages were ever sent. The complainant rejected the suggestion that she invented the allegations, explaining that she had removed the messages years ago at Hayler's request.

Hayler and Price were married at the time the alleged offenses took place in 2016, but they separated in 2018 and finalized their divorce in 2021. Hayler contends that Price initiated a campaign against him following their breakup and that the allegations against him are linked to it. The court noted that the complainant is not a member of either Hayler's or Price's extended family. The trial is continuing.
