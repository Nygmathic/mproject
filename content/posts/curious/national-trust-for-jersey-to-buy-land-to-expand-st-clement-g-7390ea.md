Conservationists in Jersey are preparing to purchase a new plot of land as part of an effort to enlarge an existing green space within a densely populated area of the island. According to the National Trust for Jersey, the organization has secured the legal rights to acquire land located close to Maupertuis Farm, which is situated in the parish of St Clement. The charity expects to finalize the property transaction in the near future.

This effort aims to enlarge what conservationists refer to as a green lung, an environment made up of open fields, parklands, or natural greenery located inside heavily developed or urbanized settings. These areas serve an important role by offering habitats for native wildlife while simultaneously helping to mitigate pollution levels in the surrounding community.

The project follows a previous land acquisition by the charity in the same parish. In 2021, the organization successfully gathered £50,000 over a period of 37 days to purchase a neighboring parcel of land situated along Samares Lane. Representatives from the trust noted that the latest purchase will serve to safeguard the historical agricultural character of both Maupertuis Farm and the broader surrounding territory.

Speaking on the initiative, the chief executive of the National Trust for Jersey, Alan Le Maistre, emphasized the growing importance of protecting natural sites. He noted that natural areas are becoming "ever more precious" across the island, particularly within districts that have experienced extensive construction and development. Le Maistre expressed that it is a privilege for the organization to be granted the chance to broaden this green lung, benefiting both local human populations and resident wildlife species.

The National Trust for Jersey currently oversees roughly 1,800 vergées of land spread across various parts of the island. The charity, which marked its 90th anniversary in August, is reaching out to the public for assistance. Individuals who wish to offer financial or practical support for the acquisition near Maupertuis Farm have been invited to reach out to the charity directly.
