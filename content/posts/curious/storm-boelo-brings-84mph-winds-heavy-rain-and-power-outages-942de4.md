Consecutive weekend storms have brought severe flooding, power outages, and travel disruption across Wales. Following the impacts of Storm Austen, Storm Boelo brought heavy rainfall and strong winds, including an 84mph (135km/h) gust logged in Capel Curig, Conwy county, on Saturday. The location also received 2.1 inches (54.6mm) of rain, while Bute Park in Cardiff recorded 2.2 inches (55.2mm) during the same day.

The adverse weather left hundreds of households without electricity on Sunday morning. Power cuts affected 555 homes in Rhayader, Powys, along with roughly 100 properties in south Wales. Furthermore, SP Energy stated it was managing 14 separate outages in north and mid Wales. The National Grid apologized for the disruption and stated its engineers were working to reconnect affected homes as rapidly as possible. Natural Resources Wales reported four flood warnings along the River Ely in south Wales at 09:20 on Sunday, advising impacted individuals to seek safety and evacuate their homes if safe to do so. While most of Wales had no weather warning for Storm Boelo, a yellow wind warning remained active for parts of north Wales until 21:00 GMT on Sunday.

Flooding and fallen trees created widespread transport challenges. Transport for Wales substituted buses for several rail routes, citing a blocked line between Cardiff Central and Bridgend as well as track flooding between Bridgend and Maesteg. Rhondda Cynon Taf council responded to numerous reports of downed trees, while high water forced the closure of Cowbridge Road in Ely, Cardiff. North Wales police cautioned motorists about fallen trees and debris along the A470 between Llanrwst and the Black Cat Roundabout. Maritime transport was also disrupted, as Stenaline cancelled its 10:00 Holyhead to Dublin service, Irish Ferries cancelled two sailings on the same route, and the ferry service between Pembroke and Rosslare was cancelled.

The weekend damage follows Friday's disruption from Storm Austen, which generated a sandstorm that forced the Tafell a Tân cafe in Llangrannog, Ceredigion, to close. Cafe employees praised community members who helped clean up the property on Saturday, sharing a social media message calling the local support "Llangrannog at its absolute finest" following the storm.
