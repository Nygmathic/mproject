A former Conservative activist who was recorded during an undercover investigation appearing to offer services to act as a broker for contract killers previously worked for the party's central headquarters, according to reporting by BBC News.

Jack Ross had been contracted to provide social media advice to the Conservative Party for a brief duration before his arrangement concluded earlier this year. Information provided to BBC News indicated that while he was paid for this consulting role, he was never a pass holder for Conservative Campaign Headquarters nor an official employee of the organization.

The situation follows undercover footage broadcast by Al Jazeera, in which Ross appears to suggest he could arrange the hiring of hitmen. The investigation involved an undercover reporter who initially approached Ross in 2022 with a fake scenario involving a family business under threat of a takeover by an organized criminal gang. In response, Ross allegedly asked the reporter what specific assistance was required from him. He then listed a range of violent actions available for purchase, explicitly mentioning stabbings, shootings, and killings.

Addressing the report, Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch described the accusations as "shocking allegations" and expressed her belief that Ross was no longer a member of the political party. Following these developments, a spokesperson for the Conservative Party announced that Ross's party membership had been suspended with immediate effect while an official investigation is conducted.

Beyond his short-term consulting work for the Conservative Party, Ross held the position of chief executive at Turning Point UK, which operates as an offshoot of a right-wing organization based in the United States. Through his leadership role at the group, he met Badenoch on several different occasions. Furthermore, Ross has met with other prominent right-wing political figures, including former Prime Minister Boris Johnson and the leader of Reform UK, Nigel Farage.

In response to the allegations, Ross has completely denied the findings of the report. Reports indicate he stated that his comments during the interaction were not meant to be taken seriously and that everything he said to the undercover journalist was made in jest. Turning Point UK has also been approached for comment on the matter.
