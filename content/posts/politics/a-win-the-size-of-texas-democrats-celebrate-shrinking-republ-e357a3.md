Democrats secured major victories in Texas on Tuesday night, ending a 32-year drought without a statewide win. James Talarico was elected as the youngest member of the United States Senate after defeating Republican Ken Paxton, a scandal-plagued candidate endorsed by Donald Trump. At the same time, Gina Hinojosa defeated incumbent Governor Greg Abbott—who was running for a record fourth term—to become the state's first Latina governor. The outcome helped Democrats regain control of the U.S. Senate and delivered a significant blow to the Republican Party.

The election marks the Democratic Party's first statewide triumph in Texas since 1994. Previously, the last Democrat elected to the U.S. Senate from the state was Lloyd Bentsen in 1988, while Ann Richards was the last Democratic governor, elected in 1990. The victory followed a race where Republicans targeted Talarico with personal insults, false claims regarding his identity and lifestyle, and an artificial intelligence advertisement depicting him in a dress. Despite these campaign tactics and financial support for Paxton from figures such as Elon Musk, voters broke decades of Republican dominance in the state.

Supporters gathered at the Long Center for the Performing Arts in Austin, where giant screens displayed election results. A large celebration broke out when the outcome was confirmed at 10 p.m. local time. Speaking from a lectern bearing a sign about loving one's neighbor, Talarico shared details of his faith and upbringing while calling for an end to political hostility and division. In his address, he celebrated the historic result as "a win the size of Texas" and stated that the party now possessed a strong mandate.

The political shift in a state that Donald Trump won by 14 percentage points in 2024 has sent shockwaves through the Republican Party. Some political observers and party members pointed to Trump's primary endorsement of Paxton over incumbent Senator John Cornyn as a crucial factor in the loss. Commenting on the Democratic sweep of statewide races, Florida Governor Ron DeSantis posted online that the defeat removed a key pillar of the modern Republican Party.
