Law enforcement officials are reviewing a social media message posted by Sarah Helm, an author, journalist, and the spouse of national security adviser Jonathan Powell. In her post on X, Helm commented on the situation facing writers who back Palestine and Palestine Action. Her statement was made during a television debate on Sky News regarding novelist Sally Rooney, whose books will no longer be distributed to UK retailers following her decision to allocate royalties to the group.

According to a Metropolitan Police spokesperson, authorities were alerted to the online post on Thursday, October 15, and are evaluating whether a formal investigation is required. A No 10 representative stated that Helm is a private citizen who does not represent the government. The BBC attempted to contact Helm for comment.

The post has drawn strong political reactions. Reform UK MP Danny Kruger wrote to the prime minister to highlight his concerns. The Campaign Against Antisemitism described the statement as shocking, arguing that while partners may hold separate views, Powell should publicly disavow the remarks to keep his role, given his responsibilities in national security and Middle Eastern relations. In contrast, Green Party leader Zack Polanski praised Helm's bravery and called for Palestine Action to be removed from the proscribed list immediately, labeling its designation as a terrorist group "ludicrous and authoritarian".

Palestine Action, which identifies as a direct action movement utilizing disruptive methods, was proscribed as a terrorist organization by the UK government in 2025 due to national security concerns. Expressing support for or belonging to the group carries a potential sentence of up to 14 years in prison. Earlier this year, four activists received prison terms for causing £1.2 million in damage at a UK location operated by an Israeli defense business. The proscription triggered protests resulting in around 3,700 arrests and 2,500 charges, though trials are currently paused pending a Supreme Court review of the ban scheduled for next month.

Helm previously worked as a foreign correspondent for the Independent and at the Sunday Times. Her husband, Jonathan Powell, formerly served as a diplomat and an adviser to Tony Blair, aiding in the negotiation of the Northern Ireland peace agreement. He was chosen as national security adviser by Sir Keir Starmer in 2024 and maintained his position after Andy Burnham became prime minister.
