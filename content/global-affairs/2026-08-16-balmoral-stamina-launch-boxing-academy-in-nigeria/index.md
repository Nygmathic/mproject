## A New Era in Nigerian Boxing

The Nigerian sports scene has been abuzz with the launch of the Stamina High Performance Academy, a groundbreaking initiative aimed at identifying and developing the next generation of boxing talent. Founded by Tunde Ajayi, a renowned expert in high-performance fighter development, the Academy brings together Stamina's expertise from the United Kingdom with Balmoral's Nigerian boxing and promotional platform. The launch of the Academy is a significant milestone in Nigeria's bid to create a strong and sustainable boxing program.

Launching simultaneously in Lagos and Akwa Ibom, the Stamina High Performance Academy will focus on talent identification, athlete development, and creating pathways towards professional and international boxing opportunities for Nigerian fighters. According to Ajayi, Nigeria possesses an incredible depth of boxing talent that needs to be properly harnessed. "The objective of Stamina High Performance Academy is to identify that talent, develop it properly and create a pathway for athletes with the ability and ambition to progress to the highest levels of the sport," Ajayi said.

Ajiyi's reputation for high-performance fighter development is well-established, having worked with several leading professional boxers, including Anthony Yarde, Joel Kodua, and Daniel Dubois. His System9 approach to boxing training and performance has yielded impressive results, with Yarde being a prime example of a fighter developed from an amateur novice to a world-level athlete. "Launching in Lagos and Akwa Ibom gives us an important starting point. We want to build something that can grow over time and create a genuine connection between Nigerian talent and high-performance boxing in the United Kingdom and internationally," Ajayi added.

The collaboration between Stamina and Balmoral Promotions brings together Nigerian opportunity and United Kingdom expertise, providing a unique platform for Nigerian boxers to develop their skills and compete at the highest level. According to Dr Ezekiel Adamu of Balmoral Promotions, Nigeria has both the talent and the passion for boxing. "Our collaboration with Stamina brings together Nigerian opportunity and United Kingdom expertise. The launch of Stamina High Performance Academy is an exciting first step, and we are looking forward to seeing what the next generation of Nigerian boxers can achieve," Adamu said.

The launch of the Stamina High Performance Academy is a significant development in Nigeria's boxing scene, and it is expected to have a positive impact on the sport in the country. The Academy's initial talent identification trials in Lagos and Akwa Ibom are expected to be announced in the coming days, and fans and stakeholders alike are eagerly awaiting the results. As Nigeria looks to create a strong and sustainable boxing program, the Stamina High Performance Academy is poised to play a significant role in identifying and developing the next generation of boxing talent.
