## X Abandons Revenue Sharing Model, Unveils Ambitious New Creator Rewards Programme

X has made a significant shift in its strategy for supporting creators on its platform, announcing the discontinuation of its Revenue Sharing programme and the introduction of a new Original Content Rewards programme. The move is aimed at rewarding creators who produce original content on X, marking a significant departure from the previous model.

The new programme, which was unveiled on Friday, will reward creators for producing original content, including threads, videos, memes, graphics, illustrations, reporting, analysis, commentary, and reactions that add meaningful value to existing conversations. According to X, eligible creators will earn from qualified impressions generated by their original content, with payments made every two weeks.

Under the new programme, creators will be required to meet certain eligibility criteria, including being at least 18 years old, living in a country where the programme is available, maintaining an account in good standing, and subscribing to X Premium, Premium+, or Premium Business. They must also have at least 500 verified followers and record at least 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users within the previous 90 days.

The programme's requirements are designed to ensure that creators produce high-quality original content that adds value to the platform. X has warned that accounts that violate the programme's requirements could be temporarily or permanently removed, depending on the severity of the violation. Creators will also be responsible for ensuring they have the necessary rights, permissions, or licences to use content created by others.

The new programme is seen as a significant attempt by X to incentivize creators to produce original content on its platform. By rewarding creators for producing high-quality content, X aims to make its platform more valuable and engaging for its users. The programme's eligibility criteria and requirements are designed to ensure that only the most talented and dedicated creators benefit from the programme.

X's decision to abandon its Revenue Sharing programme has sparked debate among creators and industry experts. While some have welcomed the new programme as a way to incentivize original content, others have expressed concerns about the eligibility criteria and requirements. As the platform continues to evolve, it will be interesting to see how the new programme unfolds and whether it meets its intended goals.

In a statement, X said, "Today, we're introducing the Original Content Rewards Program, a new way to reward creators who bring original ideas, expertise, reporting, creativity, and commentary to X." The company added that the programme is designed to "reward the creators who start them, shape them, and move them forward."

The new programme is set to launch on September 8, with the first payout expected on September 25. Existing Revenue Sharing participants will receive three final payouts, with two scheduled for August 14 and August 28, while the final payment for earnings accrued through September 7 is expected around September 11.

As X continues to navigate the complex landscape of social media and content creation, the success of the new programme will be closely watched by industry experts and creators alike.
