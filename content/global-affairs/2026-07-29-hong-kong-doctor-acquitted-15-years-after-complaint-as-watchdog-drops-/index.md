## Clearing of Professional Misconduct Charges against Hong Kong Ophthalmologist

A Hong Kong ophthalmologist, Dr. Yiu Man-kit, has been acquitted of professional misconduct charges after the city's medical watchdog decided to offer no evidence against him, 15 years after a complaint was lodged over his handling of laser eye surgery procedures. The three disciplinary charges stemmed from a complaint submitted by a patient, Chan Man-chi, in 2011 over a bilateral Lasik surgery performed on her in May 2010 and a subsequent keratectomy on her right eye in September that year.

The charges against Dr. Yiu included failing to explain the possible risks and complications related to recurrent corneal erosion before the operation, not giving his patient proper and timely advice on post-surgery treatment options, and inadequately performing the subsequent keratectomy to treat the patient's recurring conditions. However, at the start of the inquiry on Wednesday, legal officer David Yim Ho-ching, representing the council's secretary, informed the panel that the prosecution would offer no evidence against the doctor for any of the charges. Dr. Yiu was not present at the hearing and was represented by Johnny Mok SC.

The acquittal of Dr. Yiu marks a significant development in the city's medical regulatory framework, highlighting the complexities and challenges associated with disciplinary proceedings. The burden of proof in disciplinary proceedings rested entirely on the council's secretary, as inquiry panel chairwoman Professor Grace Tang Wai-king emphasized in her judgment. The decision not to proceed with the charges against Dr. Yiu underscores the importance of ensuring that allegations of professional misconduct are thoroughly investigated and supported by evidence.

The acquittal of Dr. Yiu has significant implications for the medical community in Hong Kong. The Medical Council's decision to drop the charges against him sends a message that disciplinary actions must be based on solid evidence and not on speculation or unsubstantiated claims. This development is likely to have a broader impact on the city's healthcare system, as it emphasizes the need for robust and transparent regulatory mechanisms to protect patients and maintain public trust in the medical profession.
