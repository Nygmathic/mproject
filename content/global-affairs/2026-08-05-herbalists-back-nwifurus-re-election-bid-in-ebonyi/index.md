## Herbalists' Support for Nwifuru Fuels Debate Over Traditional Medicine Regulation

The National Union of Medical Herbal Practitioners in Ebonyi State has reaffirmed its backing for Governor Francis Nwifuru's re-election bid in 2027, underscoring the significance of the herbal industry in the state's governance.

The union, led by Chief Ndubuisi Nwogha, inaugurated the executive committee of its Abakaliki Local Government Area chapter, marking the third council area to do so after Ezza North and Ezza South. The ceremony aimed to strengthen the union's structures across the state's local government areas and promote the ethical practice of traditional medicine.

Nwogha emphasized the importance of ensuring that herbal practitioners operate professionally and ethically, warning against involvement in ritual practices, criminal activities, or any behavior capable of tarnishing the image of the profession. "Our responsibility is to ensure that herbal practitioners do the right thing," he stated. "We are here to save lives and not to destroy them."

The union also appealed to the Ebonyi State Government to establish a Medical Herbal Board to regulate and promote traditional medicine, highlighting the need for collaboration to address the activities of fake practitioners and protect genuine members. Dr. Victor Onuoha, the state Secretary of the union, urged the government to partner with the union in "sanitizing the profession."

The group's affirmation of support for Nwifuru's re-election bid reflects the significance of traditional medicine in the state's governance and the potential implications for the industry's regulation. The debate over the role of traditional medicine in modern healthcare has been ongoing, with proponents arguing for its integration into mainstream healthcare systems and critics highlighting concerns over safety and efficacy.
