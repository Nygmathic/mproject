Afrobeats star David Adeleke, popularly known as Davido, will hold a press conference on Wednesday to address key issues surrounding the 2026 Osun State governorship election, including security, the Independent National Electoral Commission's preparations, and transparency. The event, scheduled to take place at 11 am at Imole House in Osogbo, is expected to draw significant attention given Davido's high profile and his recent appointment as Chairman of the Imole Youth Mobilisation Committee for Governor Ademola Adeleke's re-election campaign.

Davido, a nephew of Governor Adeleke, has been actively involved in the campaign, leading youth mobilisation efforts and participating in several political activities across the state. His decision to address journalists and citizens on these critical issues underscores the significance of the Osun election, which is scheduled to take place on August 15. As the governor seeks a second term in office, concerns over security, electoral transparency, and other major issues are likely to dominate the conversation.
