## Election Eve Tensions Escalate in Osun State

Intelligence warnings from the African Democratic Congress (ADC) have sent shockwaves through the political landscape of Osun State, with the opposition party accusing security agencies of planning to arrest its National Secretary, Ogbeni Rauf Aregbesola, on the eve of Saturday's governorship election. In a statement issued on Wednesday, the ADC condemned what it termed a "pattern of intimidation, harassment, and alleged misuse of state institutions against opposition figures" and warned the Federal Government and security agencies to exercise restraint and avoid any action that could disrupt the electoral process or undermine public confidence in the election.

Aregbesola, a former governor of Osun State and former Minister of the Interior, is expected to play a pivotal role in coordinating the party's activities during the election. The ADC argued that if any security agency had legitimate grounds to question the former governor, it should follow established legal procedures rather than resort to an arrest that could be perceived as politically motivated.

The ADC's warnings come amid heightened political tensions in Osun State, where the governorship election has attracted particular attention due to its implications for the broader realignment of opposition forces ahead of the 2027 general elections. The party cautioned the Federal Government against taking any step capable of worsening tensions in Osun and emphasized the need to respect democratic institutions, political competition, and the rights of opposition parties to participate fully in the election.

"The APC Federal Government must be wary of doing anything that could plunge Osun State into needless crisis," the ADC warned. "Nigeria is still a democracy, and the government would do well to remember that before Saturday."

The ADC's statement has sparked widespread concern among civil society organizations, which have expressed similar worries about alleged intimidation, inflammatory rhetoric, electoral violence, and the conduct of security agencies. The party has called on Nigerians to pay close attention to events surrounding the election and has vowed to monitor developments closely.

The ADC's allegations have also highlighted the delicate balance between security and electoral integrity in Nigeria. While security agencies have a critical role to play in maintaining order and preventing electoral violence, the ADC's warnings serve as a reminder of the need for restraint and respect for democratic norms.
