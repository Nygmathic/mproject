## A Taste of Transnationalism: The Enduring Legacy of Black and White Evaporated Milk

For over eight decades, a single canned ingredient has been quietly fueling Hong Kong's daily hustle, evolving from a simple dairy product into the soul of local diner culture. The Black and White brand of full-cream evaporated milk, created by FrieslandCampina in the Netherlands exclusively for the Hong Kong market in the 1940s, has become an indispensable element in Hong Kong-style milk tea culture and craftsmanship, imparting its signature fragrance, thickness, and smoothness to this popular drink in the city's cha chaan teng – local cafes.

The story of Black and White evaporated milk is a testament to the transnational flows of food culture, where a Dutch dairy brand has become an integral part of Hong Kong's culinary identity. Jayden Looi Ji-houng, consumer dairy commercial director at FrieslandCampina Hong Kong, notes that the product was designed to cater to local tastes, with a vision to position Black and White as the "true taste of Hong Kong experience." This sentiment is reflected in the distinctive white-and-red cans, featuring an illustration of a Dutch Friesian cow, which have become a ubiquitous sight stacked at local diners – a visual testament to a cross-continental identity that Looi describes as "owned by the Dutch, born in Hong Kong."

## The Evolution of a Staple

Black and White evaporated milk has been a staple in Hong Kong for over 85 years, its presence deeply ingrained in the city's culinary landscape. The product's enduring popularity can be attributed to its unique characteristics, which set it apart from other dairy products in the market. Looi attributes this to the company's commitment to quality and authenticity, with a focus on using only the finest ingredients to create a product that is both smooth and rich.

The significance of Black and White evaporated milk extends beyond its role in Hong Kong-style milk tea culture. The product is also essential for making other popular local desserts, such as red bean ice, egg tarts, and baked buns. This versatility has contributed to the brand's widespread appeal, with consumers seeking out Black and White for its consistency and quality.
