According to a statement signed by Adeleke's spokesperson, Olawale Rasheed, Oyetola's comments were made during a meeting with APC stakeholders and supporters in Osun State. The former governor, who lost his re-election bid to Adeleke in the 2022 governorship election, seemed to gloat over the situation, saying that Adeleke's endorsement of Tinubu would not save his administration from defeat.

Oyetola's statement was met with swift rebuke from Adeleke, who described it as a clear indication that the APC was planning to rig the election. Adeleke vowed that the people of Osun State would resist any attempt to manipulate the August 15 election and demanded a free and fair poll.

The controversy has sparked concerns about the integrity of the electoral process in Osun State. Adeleke's allegations have been met with a mix of reactions from various stakeholders, with some calling for a thorough investigation into the matter.
