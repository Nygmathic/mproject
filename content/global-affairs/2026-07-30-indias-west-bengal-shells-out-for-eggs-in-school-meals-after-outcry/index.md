## Eggs Make a Comeback in West Bengal School Meals Amid Outcry

India's West Bengal state has capitulated to mounting pressure, deciding to reinstate eggs as a staple in free school meals, a move that underscores the complexities of navigating food, politics, and cultural identity in a country where communal tensions run high. The reversal is a direct response to the backlash that erupted last month when the Hindu nationalist Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) government removed eggs from meals in some state-run schools, opting instead for vegetarian alternatives.

The controversy began when the BJP, elected for the first time in the state in May, attempted to impose its broader nationalist agenda on the education system. The government's decision to replace eggs with vegetarian food was met with fierce criticism from teachers, nutritionists, and parents, who argued that removing eggs would deprive children from poor families of a crucial source of protein. The issue has sparked a long-running debate over food and religion in India, with critics accusing the government of trying to engineer a vegetarian nation.

Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari's announcement on Wednesday that eggs would be reinstated in school meals from next month has been hailed as a victory by advocates for children's nutrition and education. The decision marks a significant concession by the government, which had faced mounting pressure from various quarters to reverse its earlier decision. Nutrition experts have long emphasized the importance of eggs as a rich source of protein, essential for child development and growth.

The West Bengal government's decision has significant implications for the country's education system, where access to nutritious meals is a critical factor in student performance and development. The controversy has also highlighted the delicate balance between promoting cultural and religious sensitivities and ensuring that children receive the necessary sustenance to thrive. As India grapples with the complexities of its diverse cultural landscape, the decision to reinstate eggs in school meals serves as a reminder of the need for inclusive policies that cater to the needs of all sections of society.
