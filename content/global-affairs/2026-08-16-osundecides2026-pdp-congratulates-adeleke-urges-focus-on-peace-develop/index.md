## A Mandate Defended

Senator Ademola Adeleke has secured his second term as Osun State Governor, defeating the All Progressives Congress candidate, Bola Oyebamiji, in Saturday's keenly contested governorship election. The Peoples Democratic Party, despite Adeleke's decision to contest on the Accord Party's platform, has congratulated the governor on his victory, urging him to focus on peace, development, and the welfare of the people.

The final result declared by the Independent National Electoral Commission showed Adeleke polling 511,067 votes, while Oyebamiji scored 444,815 votes, with the governor winning by a margin of 66,252 votes. Adeleke's victory represents a significant increase in his electoral margin compared with the 2022 governorship election, where he won by 28,344 votes. The governor won in 19 out of the state's 30 local government areas, while Oyebamiji secured 11. The African Democratic Congress candidate, Najeem Salaam, finished third with 17,180 votes.

The PDP's statement acknowledged the political circumstances surrounding Adeleke's re-election, noting that the governor had chosen to pursue his political journey on the platform of the Accord Party. Despite this, the party remained proud of its contributions to the democratic development of Osun State. The PDP urged all political actors to respect the outcome of the election, work towards maintaining peace and stability in the state, and place the interests of Osun State and Nigeria above partisan considerations.

Adeleke's victory has been hailed as a resounding vote of confidence in the governor's leadership. Natasha, a prominent figure in the state, described the outcome as a clear indication that Osun people have spoken loudly and unmistakably. The National Association of Nigerian Students (NANS) also welcomed the governor's victory, viewing it as a significant development for the state's progress.

Adeleke's re-election has significant implications for the political landscape in Osun State and Nigeria as a whole. As the governor begins his second term in office, he is expected to focus on delivering on the promises he made to the people during his campaign. The PDP's call for greater attention to governance and the welfare of the people is a reminder that Adeleke's victory should not be seen as an end in itself but rather as a new beginning for the state's development.
