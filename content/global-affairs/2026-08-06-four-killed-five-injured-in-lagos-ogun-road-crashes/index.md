## Multiple Fatalities in Lagos and Ogun Road Crashes: Route Violations and Speeding Cited

At least four persons lost their lives in separate road crashes that occurred in Lagos and Ogun states on Wednesday, with five others sustaining varying degrees of injuries. The incidents have prompted authorities to renew their appeal to motorists to desist from route violations and excessive speeding, citing the grave consequences of such actions.

According to the Ogun State Traffic Compliance and Enforcement Agency (TRACE) and the Lagos State Traffic Management Authority (LASTMA), the crashes were caused by a combination of route violation and excessive speeding. In Ogun State, a head-on collision involving a Mercedes-Benz truck and a crane along the Sagamu-Benin Expressway resulted in the deaths of three persons and injuries to another three. The incident occurred at about 5 am, with eyewitnesses attributing the crash to the crane driver's decision to drive against traffic while speeding.

The spokesperson for TRACE, Babatunde Akinbiyi, stated that the injured victims were rescued to the Babcock University Teaching Hospital, while the presumed dead were taken to the Olabisi Onabanjo University Teaching Hospital morgue in Sagamu. In a separate incident in Lagos, one person died while two others were injured in a multiple-vehicle collision along New Road by Coastal Road, inward Jakande. Authorities attributed the crash to excessive speeding by the trailer driver, who lost control and rammed into the moving Toyota mini truck, forcing it to somersault before crashing into one of the motorcycles.

The General Manager of LASTMA, Olalekan Bakare-Oki, described the crash as a needless tragedy and renewed the agency's appeal to motorists to obey speed limits and cultivate safe driving habits. Reckless driving, he noted, remained one of the leading causes of avoidable road traffic deaths. The incidents highlight the need for greater discipline and vigilance on the part of road users, particularly in high-risk areas such as the Sagamu-Benin Expressway and New Road by Coastal Road.

The road crashes have also raised questions about the effectiveness of traffic management authorities in enforcing traffic regulations and preventing such incidents. While authorities have appealed to motorists to desist from route violations and excessive speeding, the persistence of such incidents suggests that more needs to be done to address the root causes of these problems.

In the wake of the incidents, traffic authorities have commiserated with the families of the deceased and urged motorists to exercise caution on the roads. The TRACE and LASTMA have also reiterated their commitment to improving road safety and reducing the incidence of road crashes in the states. As the authorities continue to grapple with the issue of road safety, motorists are reminded of the importance of adhering to traffic regulations and exercising caution on the roads.
