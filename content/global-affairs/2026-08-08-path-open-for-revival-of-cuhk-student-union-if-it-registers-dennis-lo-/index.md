Since then, however, two college unions, SH Ho College and Morningside College, have successfully registered, demonstrating that the path to re-establishing a central union is, in fact, open.

Lo's comments on Monday came in response to claims that the university is overreliant on intake from mainland China. He argued that CUHK is actively working towards diversification, pointing to its efforts to engage with students and provide support throughout the registration process. "Among our nine colleges, SH Ho College and Morningside College have already done so – this proves the path is open," Lo said, underscoring the university's commitment to facilitating the revival of the student union.
