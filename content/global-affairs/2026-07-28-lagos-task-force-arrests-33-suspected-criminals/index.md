## Coordinated Crackdown on Crime in Lagos

Lagos task force arrests 33 suspected criminals

A coordinated raid across parts of Lagos Island and the Lekki corridor has resulted in the arrest of 33 suspected criminals, according to the Lagos State Environmental and Special Offences (Enforcement) Unit. The operation, conducted on Monday, July 27, 2026, targeted a range of individuals, including alleged street urchins, drug peddlers, suspected drug addicts, and illegal commercial motorcyclists. The task force also recovered illicit drugs and dangerous weapons, including substances suspected to be Tramadol, Indian hemp, Colorado, marijuana, and cannabis, as well as cutlasses and scissors.

The operation is part of the state's ongoing campaign against environmental offences, drug abuse, and criminal activities. The agency's spokesperson, Raheem Gbedeyan, stated that the early morning operation targeted areas where the presence of suspected criminal elements had become noticeable. The areas covered during the exercise included Ikate Bus Stop, Marwa Bus Stop, Muri Okunola Park in Victoria Island, Obalende Underbridge, Law School Junction, and other adjoining locations along the Lekki-Epe Expressway.

The suspects apprehended during the operation have been charged before the appropriate courts under the Lagos State Environmental Management and Protection Law, 2017, and the Lagos State Transport Sector Reform Law, 2018, where applicable. The Chairman of the Lagos State Taskforce, CSP Adetayo Akerele, warned that the agency would continue to conduct intelligence-driven raids to flush out hoodlums and restore public order. He reaffirmed the agency's commitment to intelligence-led enforcement and collaboration with relevant agencies to make Lagos uninhabitable for criminal elements.
