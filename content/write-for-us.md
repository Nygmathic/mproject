Veridus Post publishes independent reporting, long-form analysis, and argued essays across politics, business, climate, technology, sports, lifestyle, Africa, science, and culture. We welcome pitches from journalists, researchers, practitioners, and guest writers around the world.

## What We Commission

We look for reporting and commentary grounded in evidence, original observation, or direct domain expertise. Strong submissions explain **why a development matters** beyond breaking headlines, bring clarity to overlooked systems, and respect the reader's intelligence.

- **Reported Features & Dispatches:** 900 to 2,200 words grounded in primary documents, interviews, or field observation.
- **Guest Opinion & Commentary:** 700 to 1,300 words making a clear, well-evidenced argument on a public issue.
- **Technical & Science Explainers:** Clear breakdowns of energy grids, public infrastructure, computation, climate instrumentation, or economic policy.

## How to Pitch

Send a concise pitch (two to three paragraphs summarizing your central thesis, key sources, and why you are positioned to write the piece) using our [Newsroom Contact Form](/contact/#contact-form) with **Guest Writer Pitch** selected, or email [contact@veriduspost.com](mailto:contact@veriduspost.com).

Please disclose any prior publication of related drafts and any financial, institutional, or personal ties relevant to the subject.

## Editorial Standards & Fact-Checking

- **Verification:** Claims of fact must rest on primary records, named sources, or verifiable public data. Unsubstantiated rumor or undisclosed promotional copy will be declined.
- **Originality:** Submissions must be original work written by the named contributor. Automated filler or undisclosed AI-generated manuscripts are not accepted.
- **Conflicts of Interest:** Guest writers must disclose relevant funding, employment, advisory roles, or financial holdings prior to publication.
- **Independence:** Guest writers contribute independently; opinions expressed by guest contributors represent their own judgment and are not the official point of view of Veridus Post. All submissions are governed by our [Terms of Use](/terms/).

## Corrections Policy

When a factual error is identified after publication, we verify the record promptly, update the article transparently, and note the correction clearly. To request a correction, submit the dispatch URL and supporting evidence via our [Contact & Corrections Form](/contact/#contact-form).
