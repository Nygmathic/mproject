## 9 Sounds That Defined a Summer Pool Day in the 1990s

Imagine the warmth of a beaming hot day, the school break stretching before you, and the anticipation of a long, lazy summer. For 1990s kids, a pool day was the ultimate summertime experience. It was a time of carefree fun, laughter, and adventure, all set against the backdrop of a sun-kissed pool and the sounds that defined the era. In this article, we'll take a nostalgic journey back to the 1990s and revisit the iconic sounds that made a summer pool day truly unforgettable.

## Sounds of Summer

A 1990s pool day was a sensory experience like no other. The first sound to greet you was the thundering of feet on the Slip 'N Slide, invented by Robert Carrier in the early 1960s. The sound of pounding feet, followed by skin shooting across plastic and wild yelps, was a familiar refrain in many a summer garden across the 1990s. This treacherous water slide was the perfect way to cool off and have fun on a hot summer day.

But the Slip 'N Slide was just the beginning. The Super Soaker, invented in 1989 by Lonnie Johnson, was another iconic sound of the era. This water gun built upon the premise of the water pistol, but with a manual pump that allowed it to squirt water with greater range and accuracy. The phzz phzz of the Super Soaker being pumped up was a warning sign that a water battle was about to erupt, and kids would scramble to get their hands on one.

## Ice Cream and Sunshine

No summer pool day was complete without the sound of an ice cream truck's melodic chime. This cheerful tune signaled the arrival of the ice cream man, who brought with him a colorful array of treats that were the epitome of summertime fun. From Choco Tacos to Flintstones Push-ups, Screwballs to Bomb Pops, there was a flavor for every taste. Kids would scurry and shout with glee as they waited in line to purchase their favorite treats.

## Sprinklers and Splashing

Yard or lawn sprinklers were another essential sound of the 1990s pool day. These time-saving tools not only kept the grass in its best condition but also provided a refreshing oasis for kids to play in. The rhythmic hissing and splashing of the sprinklers created a soothing background noise that was the perfect accompaniment to a lazy summer afternoon.

## Refreshing Drinks

No pool party was complete without a few Capri-Sun drinks. First invented in 1969 in West Germany, these distinctive laminated foil vacuum packs became a staple of 1990s pool days. Kids would suck the last contents of a pouch up through a straw, savoring the sweet taste of the drink. Capri-Sun was a refreshing way to beat the heat and quench thirst on a hot summer day.

## The Fizz of Fizzy Drinks

The sound of a ring pull being bent back and pushed down was another iconic sound of the 1990s pool day. Coca-Cola Cherry, first marketed in 1985, was a flavored Coke drink that became a favorite among kids and teenagers. The fizz of the drink was a satisfying sound that accompanied the taste of the sweet and tangy flavor.

## Music and Mayhem

CD players were another staple of 1990s pool days. These compact, round players were lightweight, inexpensive, and portable, making them the perfect accompaniment to a summer afternoon. Kids would listen to the likes of TLC, Ace of Base, New Kids on the Block, and Hanson's "MMMBop" as they soaked up the sun and enjoyed the company of friends.

## Electronic Entertainment

Handheld video games were another source of entertainment for 1990s kids. The Super Mario Bros. 3 Game Watch, Nintendo Pokemon Pikachu, and Tamagotchi were just a few of the many games that kept kids entertained on poolside loungers. The electronic beeps and bloops of these games created a lively atmosphere that was the perfect accompaniment to a summer afternoon.

## Slap Bracelets and Summer Fun

Slap bracelets, invented by Stuart Anders in 1983, were another iconic sound of the 1990s pool day. Kids would slap the stiff band against their wrists, creating a satisfying slap that marked the beginning of a fun-filled afternoon. These colorful bracelets came in numerous designs and were a staple of 1990s summer fashion.

## Conclusion

A 1990s pool day was a sensory experience like no other. The sounds of summer, from the Slip 'N Slide to the Super Soaker, from the ice cream truck to the Capri-Sun drink, created a lively atmosphere that was the perfect accompaniment to a summer afternoon. As we look back on these iconic sounds, we're reminded of the carefree fun and adventure of childhood. As the summer sun sets on another season, we'll cherish the memories of these sounds that defined a summer pool day in the 1990s.
