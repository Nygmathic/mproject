## A Shadow's Weight: The Enduring Enchantment of Wiarton Willie

As the sun rises on Groundhog Day, thousands of Canadians gather in Wiarton, their eyes fixed on Willie as he emerges from his burrow to make his annual prediction. If he sees his shadow, tradition dictates that six more weeks of winter are in store. If he doesn't, spring is said to be just around the corner. The stakes may seem trivial, but for the people of Wiarton, this ritual holds a significance that goes far beyond mere entertainment.

The town has fully embraced its furry forecaster, erecting giant statues of Willie around town and incorporating his likeness into local businesses. Every February, the community comes together to celebrate, hosting parades, pancake breakfasts, fireworks, and elaborate ceremonies culminating in Willie's prediction.
