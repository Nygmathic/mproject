## Unseen Hierarchies

This seemingly trivial aspect of etiquette was a reflection of the rigid social hierarchy that governed the lives of those who claimed to be of high birth.

The stakes were high: a misstep in etiquette could spell disaster for one's social standing. One misplaced word, an ill-timed gesture, or a misjudged fashion choice could lead to ostracism, and even ruin a family's reputation.
