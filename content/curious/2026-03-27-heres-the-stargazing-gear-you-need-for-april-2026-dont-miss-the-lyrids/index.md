## The Stakes of Skygazing

The Lyrid meteor shower, one of the most ancient and mysterious of the annual meteor displays, is expected to peak on April 15-16. Originating from the constellation Lyra, the shower's radiant point lies relatively close to the bright star Vega, making it an attractive target for amateur astronomers. The shower's peak is forecast to produce rates of up to 20 meteors per hour, under ideal viewing conditions. The challenge lies in finding a location with minimal light pollution and clear skies to maximize the viewing experience. With two comets, SWAN (C/2024 K1) and ATLAS (C/2021 Y1), also gracing the skies in April, stargazers will have ample opportunity to observe these icy visitors.
