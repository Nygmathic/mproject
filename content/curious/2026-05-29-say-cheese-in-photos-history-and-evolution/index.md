## The Origins of a Timeless Tradition

The phrase "say cheese" has its roots in the early days of photography, when capturing a smile on camera was a true challenge. By asking subjects to "say cheese," photographers were able to create a standardized expression that would be easy to capture on film.

## The Science Behind the Smile

From a linguistic perspective, the phrase "say cheese" is a masterclass in cognitive linguistics. When we're asked to "say cheese," we're not simply repeating a phrase; we're also creating a visual representation of a specific facial expression. The phrase triggers a neural response in our brain, which in turn causes us to contort our face into a specific shape. This is a prime example of a linguistic phenomenon known as "iconicity," where the sound or phrase itself evokes a specific image or action. In this case, the phrase "say cheese" is an iconic representation of a smile, and it triggers a automatic response in our brain that helps us to smile on cue.
