## A Hidden Gem in the Heartland: The Cass County Dentzel Carousel

In the sleepy town of Logansport, Indiana, a piece of history is spinning its wheels, waiting to be discovered. The Cass County Dentzel Carousel, a vintage menagerie-style carousel built by the renowned Dentzel Carousel Company, has been enchanting visitors since its likely construction around 1900. As a National Historic Landmark, this magnificent attraction is a testament to the craftsmanship of a bygone era. With its hand-carved animals, brass ring tradition, and nostalgic charm, the Cass County Dentzel Carousel is a must-visit destination for anyone fascinated by the past.

The carousel's history is steeped in tradition, with the Dentzel company producing some of the finest carousels in the world. The Cass County Dentzel Carousel is one of only three stationary Dentzel carousels left in the country, a remarkable feat considering its age. Originally built in Fort Wayne, the carousel was relocated to Logansport in 1919, where it has been delighting visitors for nearly a century. With 43 hand-carved animals, including horses, deer, goats, giraffes, a lion, and a tiger, the carousel is a veritable menagerie, each one a testament to the skill of George Dentzel, the company's master craftsman.

One of the carousel's most iconic features is its brass ring tradition, where riders can attempt to snag a free ride by catching a brass ring dispensed by the carousel's original mechanism. The ring-wielding arm extends from the carousel, tantalizing riders with the promise of a free spin. For those who succeed, the reward is a thrilling ride, courtesy of the carousel's expertly crafted mechanism. Music fills the air, courtesy of the organ located in the middle of the carousel, next to the power plant, adding to the enchantment of this bygone era.

The carousel's restoration in 1993 was a meticulous process, with the original paint job carefully preserved. The result is a vibrant, colorful spectacle that is a joy to behold. With its modern home in Riverside Park, the Cass County Dentzel Carousel is now easily accessible to visitors, who can enjoy its charms during the warmer months. Open seasonally, typically from Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day, the carousel operates from 6pm to 9pm on weekdays, with extended hours on weekends and holidays. Admission is a mere $2 per ticket, making it an affordable treat for families and enthusiasts alike.

As we wander through the carousel's history, we find parallels with other vintage carousels across the country. The carousel in Watch Hill, Rhode Island, is one of the oldest in the United States, while the one in Story City, Iowa, boasts a similar hand-carved wooden design. These carousels, like the Cass County Dentzel Carousel, are a link to a bygone era, a time when craftsmanship and tradition were paramount. They remind us of the importance of preserving our cultural heritage, even as we move forward into an increasingly modern world.
