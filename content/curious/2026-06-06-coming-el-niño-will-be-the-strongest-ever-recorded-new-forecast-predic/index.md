This time around, however, the forecast suggests that the usual patterns may be upended, with El Niño's fury intensifying across an unprecedented range of latitudes and longitudes. Since the 1990s, El Niño events have become more frequent and intense, with the global climate system seemingly primed for a dramatic escalation in extreme weather events.
