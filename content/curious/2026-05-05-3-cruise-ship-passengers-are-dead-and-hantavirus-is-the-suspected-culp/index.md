According to the World Health Organization (WHO), hantavirus can cause severe respiratory and kidney problems, often leading to death. The virus is typically found in rural areas, but experts warn that its presence on a cruise ship raises concerns about the potential for widespread transmission. The fact that multiple people have fallen ill on the same vessel, while it was in the middle of the ocean, has sparked fears of a catastrophic outbreak.

Health officials are still investigating the exact source of the outbreak, but they are working under the assumption that the virus may have been present on the ship from the outset.
