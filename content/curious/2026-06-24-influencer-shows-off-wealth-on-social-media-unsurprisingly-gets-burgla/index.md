## When Social Media Boastfulness Meets Burglary Reality

Rachna Gurjar's opulent displays of wealth on social media had become a staple of her online persona – showcasing her dazzling jewelry and wads of cash to her nearly 100,000 followers. But, in a twist of fate, the Indian influencer's fixation on flaunting her riches may have inadvertently led to her downfall. Gurjar's home in Madhya Pradesh's Shivpuri district was recently broken into, with burglars making off with a significant portion of her valuables, estimated to be worth between Rs 8 lakh to Rs 10 lakh ($8,000 – $10,500).

The brazen burglary took place in the dead of night, when Gurjar and her family were asleep inside their home. Unidentified men cut the fencing around the property and made their way inside, locking the family in their room as they proceeded to ransack the storage spaces. The family's panicked calls for help eventually allowed them to escape, but not before the burglars had made off with a haul that included gold, silver jewelry, cash, and even a carton of energy drinks.

Gurjar's husband and she recounted the harrowing experience, revealing that the burglars had deliberately altered the angles of the CCTV cameras, possibly using a lathi (bamboo cane), to prevent their faces from being recorded. The Police have launched an investigation, with Gurjar's social media activity emerging as a prime lead. By repeatedly showcasing her wealth and home interiors online, Gurjar may have inadvertently made herself a target for burglars.

The incident has ignited a heated debate online, with many questioning the risks of displaying one's wealth and home interiors on social media.
