## Baffling Business Takes a Magic Realism Turn

As the sun rises over the sprawling metropolis of Lagos, Nigeria, a peculiar phenomenon is unfolding in a small office building in the heart of the city. A group of enterprising entrepreneurs, driven by a shared passion for the wizarding world of J.K. Rowling's Harry Potter series, have embarked on a most unlikely venture: a personality quiz that promises to reveal which Hogwarts house a participant truly belongs to. Dubbed "Magic Me," the quiz has taken the city by storm, attracting individuals from all walks of life who are eager to unlock their inner wizard.

At its core, Magic Me is a business that leverages the enduring popularity of the Harry Potter franchise, which has captivated readers worldwide with its richly imagined world of magic, adventure, and self-discovery. With a reported 90% accuracy rate, Magic Me has piqued the interest of Lagosians who are keen to explore the mystical world of Hogwarts and, more importantly, gain insight into their own personalities.

But Magic Me is more than just a lighthearted pastime; it has also sparked a broader conversation about identity, community, and the power of storytelling.
