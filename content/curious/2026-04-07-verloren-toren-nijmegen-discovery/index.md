## Uncovering the Secrets of Verloren Toren

Research has shown that the tower, now affectionately known as Verloren Toren or "the lost tower," was a key component of Nijmegen's second city wall, which protected the city from the 14th to the 15th century. Built to safeguard the city from potential invaders, this wall was a testament to the ingenuity and resourcefulness of the medieval inhabitants of Nijmegen. The tower's impressive dimensions, with walls approximately 2 metres thick and a height of 13 metres, would have made it an imposing structure in its time.
