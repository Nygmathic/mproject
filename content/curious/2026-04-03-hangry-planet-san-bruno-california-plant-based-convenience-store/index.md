## A Glimpse into the Whimsy of Hangry Planet

Perched on a sun-drenched corner of El Camino Real in San Bruno, California, Hangry Planet is an unlikely oasis of kitsch and innovation. At first glance, this Shell station appears like any other fuel stop – a utilitarian outpost catering to the needs of drivers on the go. However, as you draw closer, the façade gives way to a riot of color and a dash of eccentricity that sets it apart from its more mundane counterparts. Above the storefront, a trident-wielding merman and a large, upside-down melting ice cream cone seem to defy gravity, while the windows are an explosion of psychedelic posters featuring space-faring cows advocating for plant-based diets. It's a visual assault that's as bewildering as it is beguiling.

## Beyond the Kitsch: A Mission-Driven Convenience Store

Underneath its outlandish exterior, Hangry Planet harbors a serious mission. Established in 2021, it claims to be North America's first fully plant-based convenience store. The standard-issue Slim Jims that once lined the shelves have been replaced by a carefully curated selection of plant-based, dairy-free products from brands like Donut Farm, Go Max Go, and Oatly. For those seeking more than just snacks, Vegan Mob offers plant-based BBQ and soul food that's as satisfying as it is healthy. This shift away from traditional convenience store fare may seem like a departure from the norm, but it's a deliberate choice that reflects the evolving values of a growing segment of the population.
