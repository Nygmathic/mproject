The use of a thumb as a measuring device was practical and intuitive, allowing craftsmen to quickly estimate the size of their work without the need for more precise tools. Over time, the phrase "rule of thumb" evolved to refer to any guideline or principle that was based on experience and observation, rather than strict measurement or scientific calculation. Today, the phrase is used in a wide range of contexts, from finance to medicine, to describe a rough but effective approach to decision-making.
