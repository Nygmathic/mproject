## A Sky Ablaze: The 10 Best Places to Watch Fourth of July Fireworks in the U.S.

As the sun sets on the Fourth of July, the sky transforms into a kaleidoscope of colors, a testament to the ingenuity and creativity of pyrotechnicians across the nation. From small towns in the Pacific Northwest to the bustling streets of New York City, fireworks displays are a staple of Independence Day celebrations. Leaving no stone unturned, the 10 best places to watch these dazzling displays have been ranked by USA Today, and they are a must-see for anyone looking to experience the magic of the Fourth.

## A Symphony of Light and Sound

At the top of the list is Chicago's Navy Pier, where the smoky outline of the city against Lake Michigan provides a unique backdrop for the fireworks display. With a variety of viewpoints available, from rooftops to parks to the pier itself, visitors and locals alike can take in the stunning light show. The annual Ford Fireworks show in Detroit is another crowd-pleaser, with its explosive displays and live music preceding the show. The display is so massive that it requires three barges to hold all the fireworks, making it a spectacle unlike any other.

The Big Apple takes the stage with its Macy's fireworks display, launching over 80,000 shells into the sky from atop the Hudson River. The colorful bursts create a breathtaking backdrop to the city's iconic skyline, including the Empire State Building, which is always lit in red, white, and blue for the Fourth. Add music and pyrotechnics, and you have a true spectacle in the city that never sleeps.

## A Texas-Sized Celebration

Addison, Texas, a suburb of Dallas, takes the title of Kaboom Town, a Fourth of July festival that attracts over half a million people each year. With music, food, and affordable ticket prices, the show is a testament to the city's commitment to celebration. The name says it all: this is a fireworks show that's going to be absolutely fantastic.

## A Concert in the Sky

In Atchison, Kansas, the birthplace of aviation legend Amelia Earhart, thousands gather to celebrate the Fourth with friends and neighbors. The Amelia Earhart Festival features a concert, an aviation show, and an unforgettable fireworks display. The Missouri River is set aglow by fireworks that burst in rhythm with music for nearly half an hour, creating a true "concert" in the sky. Visitors bring blankets and lawn chairs to the riverbank to watch fireworks of every shape, size, and color light up a night they'll never forget.

## A Nation in Celebration

Rounding out the top 10 are Big Bay Boom in San Diego, California; Wawa Welcome America in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania; Celebrate St. Louis in St. Louis, Missouri; Let Freedom Sing in Nashville, Tennessee; and Western & Southern/WEBN Riverfest in Cincinnati, Ohio. Each of these celebrations offers a unique perspective on the Fourth of July, from the stunning ocean views of San Diego to the rich history of Philadelphia.

## The Reaction

As the countdown begins to the biggest fireworks displays of the year, cities and towns across the nation are preparing for the influx of visitors. Local businesses are gearing up for a spike in tourism, with hotels and restaurants reporting high demand. Meanwhile, residents are bracing themselves for the crowds and traffic that come with the celebrations.

## Looking to the Future

As the Fourth of July approaches, one thing is clear: the fireworks displays are going to be bigger and better than ever. With new technologies and creative designs being introduced each year, the spectacle is sure to be one for the books. Whether you're a local or a visitor, make sure to catch one of the top 10 fireworks displays in the U.S. and experience the magic of the Fourth for yourself.

The countdown has begun, and the sky is about to ablaze with color and sound. Get ready to celebrate the Fourth of July in style.
