## Attempted Assassination Unfolds in Houghton

The streets of Houghton, a suburb in Johannesburg, were bathed in an eerie silence on Sunday evening as Major General Feroz Khan, a high-ranking police officer, narrowly escaped assassination. The attempted murder has sent shockwaves throughout the nation, prompting the South African Police Service (SAPS) to launch a high-level investigation, led by the Gauteng Hawks, Gauteng Crime Intelligence, and the SAPS Detective Service.

The Acting National Commissioner, Lt Gen Puleng Dimpane, has directed the incorporation of the Political Killings Task Team (PKTT) into the investigation, ensuring that every possible lead is pursued and those responsible are brought to book. This move underscores the SAPS's commitment to protecting the rule of law and ensuring justice prevails.

Major General Khan's scheduled appearance before the Madlanga Commission on July 1, 2026, has sparked public speculation regarding the timing of the attack. However, SAPS officials are adamant that it is premature and irresponsible to speculate on any possible motive or to conclude that the incident is linked to his anticipated appearance before the Commission. Detectives will follow evidence wherever it leads, leaving no stone unturned in their pursuit of truth.

The attempted assassination of Major General Khan is a stark reminder of the risks faced by individuals involved in judicial processes. The SAPS views such attempts in the most serious light, and any attempt to intimidate, silence, or attack individuals involved in judicial processes will be met with the full might of the law. Lieutenant General Dimpane has condemned the attack and assured the public that no resource will be spared in identifying, tracing, and arresting those responsible.

The swift response from the SAPS and the incorporation of the PKTT into the investigation demonstrate a renewed commitment to addressing the scourge of political violence in South Africa. The nation is watching with bated breath as the investigation unfolds, and the public eagerly awaits updates on the progress of the case.
