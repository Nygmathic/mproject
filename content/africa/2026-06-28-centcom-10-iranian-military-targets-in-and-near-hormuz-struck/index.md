## Strikes at the Strait

US warplanes have unleashed a barrage of strikes on 10 Iranian military targets in and near the strategic Strait of Hormuz, in response to a drone attack on a Panama-flagged tanker. The US military says the strikes targeted Iranian surveillance infrastructure, communications systems, air defence sites, drone storage facilities, and minelaying capabilities.

The attacks, conducted by US Navy and Air Force fighter jets, are the latest escalation in the simmering tensions between the US and Iran. The incident began when Iran launched a one-way attack drone that struck the M/T Kiku, a tanker carrying over two million barrels of crude oil, as it transited through the Strait of Hormuz. The US military has confirmed that the drone attack resulted in damage to the vessel, but the extent of the damage remains unknown.

In response, the US military unleashed a precision strike on Iranian military targets in and near the Strait of Hormuz. According to US Central Command (Centcom), the strikes targeted multiple locations, including surveillance infrastructure, communications systems, air defence sites, drone storage facilities, and minelaying capabilities. Centcom has released a grainy, black-and-white video showing some of the strikes, but the footage does not provide clear details on the extent of the damage.

The US military has stated that commercial shipping through the strait continues uninterrupted, despite the recent tensions.
