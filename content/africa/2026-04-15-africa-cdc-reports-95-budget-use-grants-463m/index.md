## A Milestone in Health Security: Africa CDC's Unprecedented Budget Performance

As the continent continues to grapple with the complex web of health challenges, from pandemics to outbreaks of infectious diseases, a beacon of hope has emerged in the form of the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention (Africa CDC). At the heart of the organisation's remarkable achievement lies Kaseya, the director of programme management, who has been hailed by African heads of state and government for his exemplary leadership in advancing health security across Africa.

The Africa CDC has recently reported a staggering 95% utilisation of its budget, a feat that has not only impressed but also set a new benchmark for international health agencies. This remarkable performance has enabled the organisation to allocate an additional $463 million to support member states in their fight against infectious diseases. The funds will be channeled towards strengthening health systems, enhancing surveillance and detection capabilities, and supporting the development of new vaccines and treatments. This significant injection of resources is expected to have a profound impact on the continent's preparedness and response to future health crises.

## Historical Context: A Decade of Struggle and Progress

The establishment of Africa CDC in 2017 marked a significant shift in the continent's approach to health security. For decades, Africa had been plagued by a lack of coordinated response to outbreaks and pandemics, with the devastating consequences of the Ebola outbreak in West Africa starkly illustrating the need for collective action. The creation of Africa CDC was a direct response to this challenge, with the organisation tasked with providing technical assistance, capacity building, and strategic guidance to member states. Since its inception, Africa CDC has worked tirelessly to strengthen the continent's health infrastructure, enhance regional collaboration, and promote the development of new health technologies.

However, the journey has not been without its challenges. Africa CDC has faced significant funding constraints, inadequate political support, and a shortage of skilled personnel. Despite these hurdles, the organisation has persevered, leveraging its partnerships with international donors and global health agencies to secure vital resources. The current director-general, John Nkengasong, has been instrumental in driving this effort, leveraging his extensive experience in global health to build a robust and resilient health system across the continent.

## Perspectives: Leadership, Partnerships, and Sustainability

Kaseya's leadership has been widely praised for its innovative approach to health security. His commitment to decentralising decision-making, empowering local communities, and leveraging technology to enhance surveillance and detection capabilities has been instrumental in Africa CDC's success. The organisation's partnerships with global health agencies, such as the World Health Organization (WHO) and the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation, have also been critical in securing vital resources and expertise.

However, some critics have raised concerns about the sustainability of Africa CDC's budget performance. With many member states struggling to meet their financial obligations, there are fears that the organisation's future funding will be precarious. Moreover, the lack of a clear exit strategy for the organisation's reliance on international donors has raised questions about its long-term viability.
