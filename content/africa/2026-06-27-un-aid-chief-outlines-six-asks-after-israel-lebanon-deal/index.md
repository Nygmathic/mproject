## A Leap of Hope in the Face of Devastation

Tom Fletcher, the UN's aid chief, has welcomed a recent trilateral agreement between Israel and Lebanon as a "moment of hope and opportunity" that brings much-needed respite to a war-torn country. However, he has also starkly highlighted the "grave humanitarian crisis" that Lebanon continues to face, with entire communities displaced and devastated by months of Israeli military attacks.

Fletcher's comments come as Lebanon struggles to cope with the aftermath of the conflict, with thousands of people forced to flee their homes and seek shelter in makeshift camps. The humanitarian community, he noted, is mobilised and determined to translate this momentum into real change in people's lives. The UN's six key asks in this regard include sustained and extended de-escalation, safe, voluntary and dignified returns, and support for the Lebanese government to address the root causes of the crisis.

These "six asks" are critical to ensuring that the fragile peace holds and that the people of Lebanon can begin to rebuild their lives. The trilateral agreement, which was announced in Washington earlier this week, marks a significant shift in the dynamics of the region and presents a rare opportunity for progress. However, it also serves as a stark reminder of the ongoing humanitarian needs in Lebanon and the urgent need for international support.
