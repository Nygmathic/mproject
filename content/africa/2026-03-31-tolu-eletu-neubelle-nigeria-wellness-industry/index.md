As the sun rises over the bustling streets of Lagos, Tolu Eletu is already on a mission to transform Nigeria's wellness industry. For years, she has been a driving force behind Neubelle, a pioneering initiative that brings together industry conversations and real-life application to raise standards and promote holistic living. Tolu Eletu's journey into the world of wellness began at a time when the concept of holistic living was still in its infancy in Nigeria. With a background in psychology and a keen interest in alternative therapies, she was determined to make a difference. Armed with a wealth of knowledge and a contagious enthusiasm, Eletu set out to educate and empower Nigerians to take control of their health and wellbeing. Her approach was refreshingly straightforward: she would not only talk about change but actively show how it could be done. And so, Neubelle was born – a community-driven initiative that aimed to revolutionize the way Nigerians approached wellness.

At its core, Neubelle is built on the principles of inclusivity and accessibility. Eletu's vision is to make wellness a mainstream phenomenon, free from the elitism and exclusivity that often characterized the industry in Nigeria. To achieve this, she has established a network of like-minded individuals and organizations who share her passion for holistic living. Together, they have created a platform that offers a wide range of services and resources, from yoga and meditation classes to nutrition and wellness workshops. But Neubelle's impact goes beyond the physical – it has also sparked a cultural shift in the way Nigerians think about health and wellbeing.
