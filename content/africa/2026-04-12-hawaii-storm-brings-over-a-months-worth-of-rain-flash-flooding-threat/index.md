As the Hawaiian Islands continue to bear the brunt of a potent, multi-day storm, parts of the state have already seen over a month's worth of rainfall in just a few days, prompting urgent warnings of possible flash flooding. Hawaii's National Weather Service issued a flood watch for all Hawaiian islands early Saturday morning, and it will remain in effect until Monday afternoon. The warning highlights the potential for flash flooding, which could be particularly hazardous in areas with saturated grounds and swollen rivers. The recent downpour has already caused significant disruptions, with roads and highways experiencing closures due to rising water levels and flooded streets. As a result, residents and visitors alike have been advised to exercise extreme caution when traveling, especially in low-lying areas prone to flooding.
