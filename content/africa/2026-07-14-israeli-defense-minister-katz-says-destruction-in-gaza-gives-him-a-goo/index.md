## Destructive Legacy in Gaza

The streets of northern Gaza, once bustling with life, now lie in ruins, a testament to the devastating effects of Israel's military campaigns. Israeli Defense Minister Israel Katz's recent tour of the besieged enclave was met with a sense of pride, rather than remorse, as he described the widespread destruction as giving him a "good feeling". His words, spoken with a sense of satisfaction, are a stark reminder of the human cost of the conflict.

Katz's statement was made in response to a question about how he felt looking at the state of Gaza, a region that has been under Israeli blockade for over a decade. His response, "A good feeling, isn’t it?", is a chilling display of disregard for the suffering of the Palestinian people. The defence minister's justification for the destruction is rooted in a "deliberate policy" aimed at removing threats, a strategy that has been met with widespread criticism from human rights groups and international observers.

A closer examination of Katz's comments reveals a fundamental shift in Israel's military strategy. Gone are the days of temporary military raids, replaced by a more aggressive approach that sees Israeli forces entrenched in Gaza, with Palestinian militants forced to operate from outside the enclave. This new approach has led to the destruction of entire neighbourhoods, leaving thousands of civilians without homes or access to basic necessities.

Katz's announcement of plans to establish three military-linked settlement outposts in Gaza is a further blow to the already beleaguered Palestinian population. The settlements, which will be built on land seized from Palestinian families, are a stark reminder of the Israeli government's commitment to expanding its territory at the expense of the Palestinian people. This move is likely to exacerbate tensions in the region, further destabilizing an already fragile situation.

The international community has been vocal in its condemnation of Israel's actions in Gaza. Human rights groups have accused the Israeli military of committing war crimes, while the United Nations has called for an end to the blockade and the establishment of a lasting ceasefire. Despite these calls, the situation in Gaza remains dire, with thousands of Palestinians living in poverty and without access to basic necessities.

The people of Gaza are not just statistics or headlines; they are human beings who have been living under the shadow of war for far too long. Their stories are a reminder of the devastating impact of conflict on civilians, and the need for a lasting and sustainable solution to the Israeli-Palestinian conflict.

As the situation in Gaza continues to deteriorate, it is clear that international action is needed to prevent further suffering. The people of Gaza deserve better than to be treated as pawns in a larger game; they deserve dignity, respect, and a chance to rebuild their lives.
