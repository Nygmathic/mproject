## The Making of a Climate Champion

Damilola Ogunbiyi's journey to the forefront of the climate movement is a testament to her unwavering commitment and unrelenting passion. Born in Lagos, Nigeria, Ogunbiyi's early life was marked by a deep appreciation for the natural world and a desire to make a positive impact on her community. She pursued a career in engineering, earning a master's degree in electrical engineering from the University of Lagos, before transitioning into a role in the energy sector. Her expertise quickly earned her recognition, and she went on to hold senior positions in the Nigerian energy industry, including a stint as the managing director of the Rural Electrification Agency.

Ogunbiyi's rise to prominence within the UN was swift and meteoric. In 2016, she was appointed as the special representative of the UN secretary-general for Sustainable Energy for All, a position that has enabled her to leverage her expertise and experience to drive global progress on energy access and climate action. Her leadership has been instrumental in shaping the UN's Sustainable Development Goals, and her work has been instrumental in mobilizing governments, businesses, and civil society to take action on climate change.
