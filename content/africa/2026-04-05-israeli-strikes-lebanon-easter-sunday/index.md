## Easter Sunday Bloodshed: Israeli Strikes Devastate Lebanon

As Easter Sunday dawned over Lebanon's snow-capped mountains, a sense of reverence and hope filled the air. But in an instant, the joy was shattered. Israeli air strikes, launched in a coordinated effort, tore through the country, leaving a trail of death and destruction in their wake. At least 11 people lost their lives, with dozens more injured, in a bloody escalation of the conflict that has ravaged Lebanon since 2 March.

The scope of the devastation is stark. In the southern village of Kfarhata, a single strike claimed seven lives, including a young child, leaving families to mourn the loss of loved ones on a day meant for celebration. In Beirut, another strike struck the Jnah neighbourhood, killing four people and injuring 39 others, a grim reminder of the human cost of the conflict. The Lebanese health ministry, while confirming the casualties, has yet to provide a comprehensive breakdown of the extent of the damage.

The timing of the strikes, coinciding as they did with Easter Sunday, has added a sense of sacrilege to the carnage. For Lebanese Christians, Easter is a day of renewal and hope, a time to reflect on the struggles of the past and look to a brighter future. The Israeli military's actions, therefore, have sent a jarring message: that even on the most sacred of days, the pursuit of military objectives takes precedence over human life and dignity.

To understand the Israeli strikes in context, it is essential to grasp the complexities of the conflict that has been unfolding in Lebanon since 2 March. The Israeli military, citing concerns over the buildup of Iranian-backed militias in Lebanon, launched a broad campaign against what it describes as "terrorist targets." However, many Lebanese have long argued that the Israeli military's actions are not merely a response to security threats but also a bid to assert control over the country's south.

This narrative is not new. For decades, Lebanon has been embroiled in a protracted conflict with Israel, with periods of relative calm punctuated by outbreaks of violence. The Israeli war on Lebanon in 2006, which left thousands dead and injured, is a stark reminder of the devastating consequences of military action in the country.

The Israeli strikes, therefore, have also raised questions about the long-term impact on Lebanon's fragile social fabric. The country's residents, already reeling from the economic and humanitarian crises, are struggling to cope with the trauma inflicted by the conflict. The strikes have also sparked widespread condemnation from international actors, with many calling for an immediate cessation of hostilities.

In the midst of the chaos, Lebanese leaders have issued a unified call for calm, urging citizens to remain steadfast in the face of adversity. However, the stakes are high, and the international community is watching with bated breath. The United Nations, which has long been involved in efforts to broker a peace agreement between Israel and Lebanon, has issued a statement expressing deep concern over the escalation.

As the dust settles, Lebanese citizens are left to ponder the implications of the Israeli strikes. For many, the attacks have served as a stark reminder of the country's vulnerability in the face of external aggression. Others have spoken out against the Israeli military's actions, decrying the disproportionate use of force and the disregard for civilian lives.

In response to the strikes, Israeli officials have maintained that their actions were necessary to protect national security. However, many Lebanese have countered that the military campaign has only served to exacerbate the humanitarian crisis, further destabilizing a country already on the brink of collapse.

As Easter Sunday slowly gives way to a sombre Monday, Lebanon's people are left to mourn the loss of life and reflect on the future. The Israeli strikes, while a stark reminder of the country's vulnerability, also serve as a call to action: for international actors to intervene decisively in the conflict, for Lebanese leaders to unite in their opposition to Israeli aggression, and for the people of Lebanon to stand resolute in the face of adversity.

As the international community grapples with the implications of the Israeli strikes, one thing is clear: the road ahead will be long and fraught with danger. However, it is in these moments of crisis that the resilience and determination of the Lebanese people are most evident. As they navigate the treacherous landscape of conflict and adversity, they remain steadfast in their resolve to rebuild and to create a brighter future for themselves and their children.
