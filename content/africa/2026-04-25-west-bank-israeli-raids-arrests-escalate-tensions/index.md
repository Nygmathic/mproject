## A Legacy of Occupation

To fully understand the context of the current situation, it is essential to examine the history of the occupation. Since 1967, Israel has maintained a grip on the West Bank, imposing a regime of military rule that has been described as apartheid-like by many human rights organizations. The occupation has led to widespread displacement, home demolitions, and the confiscation of land, leaving Palestinian residents with limited access to basic services like healthcare and education. The situation on the ground is dire, with many communities facing severe poverty and unemployment.

The Israeli government's actions in the West Bank are also a direct result of the Israeli settlement expansion, a policy that has been widely condemned by the international community. The settlements, which are built on Palestinian land, are a affront to international law and a direct challenge to the two-state solution. The continued expansion of settlements has led to a surge in violence, as Palestinian residents become increasingly frustrated with the lack of progress towards a peaceful resolution.
