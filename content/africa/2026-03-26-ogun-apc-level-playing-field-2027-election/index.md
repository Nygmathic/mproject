## An Urgent Question of Fairness

As the 2027 election landscape in Ogun State begins to take shape, a pressing concern has emerged within the All Progressives Congress (APC) – ensuring a level-playing field for all aspirants. This is not merely a matter of internal party politics, but a critical test of the APC's commitment to democratic values and its long-term viability. The stakes are high, with the outcome of the 2027 election likely to have far-reaching consequences for the party's fortunes in the state.

The APC's quest for a level-playing field is rooted in the understanding that a free and fair contest is essential for the party's growth and relevance. In the absence of such a contest, the party risks becoming a mere appendage of the governor's office, rather than a robust and dynamic force that can mobilize support from the grassroots. This would not only undermine the party's chances in the 2027 election but also erode its legitimacy and credibility in the eyes of the people. Conversely, by ensuring a level-playing field, the APC can position itself as a champion of democracy and a force to be reckoned with in Ogun State politics.

## A History of Challenges

The APC's struggles to maintain a level-playing field in Ogun State are not new. In the 2019 elections, the party faced intense competition from the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP), which had managed to build a strong following among the state's electorate. While the APC ultimately emerged victorious, the contest was marked by allegations of electoral malpractices and vote rigging. Since then, the party has been grappling with internal power struggles and factionalism, which have threatened to undermine its cohesion and effectiveness.

The APC's challenges in Ogun State are not unique to the party or the state, but rather a reflection of broader trends in Nigerian politics. The country's electoral landscape has long been characterized by intense competition, with parties often employing unorthodox tactics to gain an edge. However, as the APC navigates its internal struggles, it must also confront the changing dynamics of Ogun State politics. The state's young and increasingly educated population is growing restless, seeking more from their leaders and demanding greater accountability and transparency.

## The Imperatives of Fairness

In this context, the APC's commitment to a level-playing field is not merely a matter of party politics, but a fundamental imperative of democratic governance. By embracing fairness and inclusivity, the party can tap into the aspirations and frustrations of the Ogun State electorate, building a broad coalition of support that can propel it to victory in the 2027 election. Conversely, by compromising on these principles, the APC risks alienating its base and creating an opening for its opponents to capitalize on its weaknesses.

The APC's success in Ogun State will depend not only on its policies and campaign strategies but also on its ability to manage internal competition with integrity. This requires a robust and transparent nomination process, as well as a clear and consistent set of rules governing the party's internal contests. By embracing fairness and inclusivity, the APC can position itself as a model of democratic governance, one that can inspire confidence and trust among the Ogun State electorate.

## Reactions and Implications

As the APC navigates its internal struggles, various stakeholders are weighing in on the party's commitment to a level-playing field. The party's national leadership has emphasized the importance of fairness and inclusivity, while key figures within the party have called for greater transparency and accountability. However, others have raised concerns about the feasibility of such a commitment, given the party's history of internal conflicts and external pressures.

The implications of the APC's commitment to a level-playing field extend far beyond the party itself. A free and fair contest in Ogun State has the potential to set a new standard for democratic governance in Nigeria, inspiring other parties to follow suit. Conversely, a failure to uphold these principles risks perpetuating a culture of manipulation and exploitation, undermining the very foundations of democratic politics.

## Looking Ahead

As the 2027 election draws closer, the APC's commitment to a level-playing field will be put to the test. The party's ability to manage internal competition with integrity, while also appealing to the aspirations and frustrations of the Ogun State electorate, will be crucial to its success. By embracing fairness and inclusivity, the APC can position itself as a champion of democracy and a force to be reckoned with in Ogun State politics. The stakes are high, but the reward is worth the risk – a free and fair contest that can propel the APC to victory and inspire a new era of democratic governance in Nigeria.
