Abuja's Akwa Ibom Stadium was once a cauldron of noise, a testament to Enyimba's dominance in Nigerian football. Enyimba's rise to prominence was meteoric. Founded in 1948 by a group of Aba-based traders, the club quickly established itself as a force to be reckoned with in Nigerian football. Their success in the 1990s and early 2000s earned them a reputation as one of the continent's premier teams, with a string of continental titles and appearances in the CAF Champions League.
