As news emerges that Professor Samuel Oladipo has been appointed Vice Chancellor of Tai Solarin University of Education, a sense of excitement and anticipation hangs in the air. Professor Oladipo's appointment is the culmination of a rigorous search process, one that drew from a pool of talented candidates from various corners of the academic world. At the heart of the decision lies a profound recognition of the Vice Chancellor-designate's research expertise and commitment to the institution's core values. As an academic with a distinguished track record, Professor Oladipo has been driving innovative research in spirituality and health, help-seeking behaviour, gender-based violence, child abuse, and psychological scale development. His scholarly contributions have not only shed light on these critical issues but have also informed policy decisions at various levels of government.

## A Life Dedicated to Empowerment

Born and bred in Nigeria, Professor Oladipo's academic journey has been shaped by a deep-seated passion for education and community development. His early research interests in spirituality and health were informed by a desire to understand the complex interplay between faith, culture, and well-being in African societies. Over the years, his work has traversed various disciplines, including psychology, sociology, and public health, as he seeks to address the intricate web of issues that underpin the lived experiences of ordinary Nigerians.

One of the defining characteristics of Professor Oladipo's approach to research is his commitment to community engagement and participatory methods. He has been instrumental in the development of psychological scales that help to measure the impact of trauma and violence on individuals and communities. His work in this area has not only informed the development of critical policies but has also empowered community leaders to take ownership of the solutions that address these issues.
