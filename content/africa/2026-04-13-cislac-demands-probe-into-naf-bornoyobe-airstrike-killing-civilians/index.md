## Tragedy Unfolds in North-East Nigeria

A hail of gunfire and a trail of destruction left in its wake, the airstrike by the Nigerian Air Force (NAF) in Borno-Yobe has claimed the lives of innocent civilians, plunging an already volatile region into even greater turmoil. Eyewitnesses report that the strike, which occurred in the early hours of a fateful morning, targeted a cluster of makeshift homes, reducing them to rubble and leaving a trail of mangled bodies in its wake. While the military has yet to release an official statement on the incident, concerns over civilian casualties have been growing, with many questioning the tactics employed by the NAF in its counterterrorism operations.

## A Pattern of Civilian Harm

The incident in Borno-Yobe is the latest in a string of civilian casualties linked to NAF operations in the region. Human rights groups have long been critical of the military's approach to counterterrorism, pointing to a pattern of reckless disregard for civilian life. With the conflict between the military and Boko Haram-related groups showing little signs of abating, the risks to civilians continue to mount. The Campaign for Public and Social Justice (CISLAC), a leading civil society organization, has expressed its deep concern over the incident, calling for an immediate probe into the circumstances surrounding the airstrike. According to CISLAC, the incident is a stark reminder of the need for greater accountability in the military's operations, and for measures to be taken to mitigate the risks to civilians.

## Context and Background

The conflict in North-East Nigeria is a complex and deeply entrenched one, with roots stretching back to the early 2000s. The rise of Boko Haram, a militant group that has pledged allegiance to the Islamic State, has led to widespread violence and displacement. The NAF, working in conjunction with regional and international partners, has been instrumental in efforts to counter the group's advance. However, the use of airstrikes has long been a contentious issue, with many questioning the effectiveness and proportionality of such tactics. In recent years, there have been a number of high-profile incidents in which civilians have been caught in the crossfire, leading to calls for greater caution and restraint in military operations.
