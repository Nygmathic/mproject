## Abiy's Ambitious Move: A Two-Term Limit Proposal that Sparks Debate

In a surprise announcement that has sent shockwaves across Ethiopia, Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has proposed a two-term limit for the head of government, a move he claims is a significant step towards consolidating democracy in the country. However, analysts are skeptical, and critics argue that this move could be a clever ploy to extend Abiy's rule, keeping him in power longer.

The proposal, made by the ruling Prosperity Party, would see the Prime Minister, who has been in office since 2018, eligible for a second term. The move has sparked intense debate, with some hailing it as a bold step towards democratic reform, while others see it as a thinly veiled attempt to circumvent the constitutional limit of two five-year terms. The Ethiopian Constitution, adopted in 1995, has been a cornerstone of the country's democratic transition, but its implementation has been patchy at best.

Abiy's critics argue that the proposal is a clever ruse to maintain his grip on power. By introducing a new term limit, Abiy can argue that the Constitution needs to be updated to accommodate his vision for the country. This, in turn, could pave the way for him to remain in office beyond 2025, when his current term is set to expire. The Prime Minister's detractors point to the fact that he has been in office for over a decade, with a stint as Foreign Minister and Defense Minister before becoming Prime Minister. This, they argue, is a clear indication of his ambition to remain in power for as long as possible.

However, Abiy and his supporters argue that the proposal is a genuine attempt to strengthen democracy in Ethiopia. They point to the country's tumultuous history, marked by periods of authoritarian rule and civil war. According to them, a two-term limit would provide stability and predictability, allowing the country to focus on its development goals. The Prosperity Party has also argued that the proposal is a necessary step towards consolidating the country's democratic gains, made possible by Abiy's leadership.

The debate over Abiy's proposal has also highlighted the deep divisions within the Ethiopian ruling class. The Prime Minister's opponents within the Prosperity Party have come out strongly against the proposal, arguing that it undermines the country's democratic institutions. Meanwhile, Abiy's supporters have defended the move, seeing it as a necessary step towards consolidating power and stability.

As Prime Minister, Abiy has continued to push for reforms, including the liberalization of the economy and the expansion of civil liberties. However, Abiy's leadership has also been marked by controversy, including human rights abuses and allegations of corruption.
