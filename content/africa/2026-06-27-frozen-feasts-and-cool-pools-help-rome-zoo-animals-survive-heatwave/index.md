## Staying Cool in the Eternal City

Rome's Bioparco Zoo is no stranger to the scorching Italian summers, but this year's heatwave has prompted the zoo staff to go the extra mile in keeping its animal residents cool and comfortable. As temperatures soar to a sizzling 36 to 37°C, the zoo has implemented a range of innovative cooling measures to ensure the health and well-being of its diverse inhabitants.

Frozen feasts and cool pools have become a staple of the zoo's daily routine, with giraffes, hippos, tapirs, capybaras, lemurs, and seals receiving specially prepared treats designed to beat the heat. These frozen delights not only provide a refreshing respite from the sweltering temperatures but also encourage natural behaviour in the animals. According to Bioparco zoological supervisor Yitzhak Yadid, some amphibious species require air-conditioned shelters or water-cooling systems to survive extreme temperatures, and the zoo has responded by providing just that.

For seals, the focus is on maintaining pools at a comfortable below 25°C to prevent overheating. This is no easy feat, especially considering the Italian capital's unrelenting summer sun. However, the zoo staff has risen to the challenge, ensuring that every animal has access to a climate-controlled indoor space or a cooled pool to escape the heat. By doing so, they are not only protecting the animals' physical health but also providing environmental enrichment, keeping them active and engaged in the midst of the prolonged summer heat.

As the heatwave continues to grip Italy, the Italian Ministry of Health has issued its highest heat warning, underscoring the gravity of the situation. However, Bioparco Zoo's proactive approach to managing the heat has set a precedent for other zoos and wildlife reserves in the region. By prioritizing animal welfare and adopting innovative cooling measures, the zoo is demonstrating its commitment to providing a safe and healthy environment for its residents.

While the heatwave has brought its share of challenges, the zoo staff's dedication to their work has been a beacon of hope for animal lovers and conservationists alike. As the situation unfolds, it will be interesting to see how other zoos and wildlife reserves respond to the heatwave, and whether they can replicate Bioparco's successful cooling strategies.
