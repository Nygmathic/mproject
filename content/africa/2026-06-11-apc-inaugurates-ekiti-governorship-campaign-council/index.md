### A New Chapter for Ekiti's Political Landscape

The sun rose over the Ekiti state capital, Ado-Ekiti, as the All Progressives Congress (APC) inaugurated its governorship campaign council. The event marked a significant milestone in the state's electoral journey, setting the stage for what promises to be a contentious battle for the top seat come 2026. As the APC's national chairman, Nentawe Yilwatda, took the podium to deliver the keynote address, the air was thick with anticipation.

The stakes are high in Ekiti, where the APC's fortunes have been on the rise under the current administration. Governor Biodun Oyebanji, who was praised by Yilwatda for his accomplishments in infrastructure, education, healthcare, and social welfare, has been instrumental in transforming the state's economic landscape. The APC's decision to endorse Oyebanji for re-election is a testament to the party's confidence in his leadership and its desire to maintain its grip on power in the state. But the governorship election is never a foregone conclusion, and the opposition parties are likely to throw everything they have at the APC in a bid to unseat Oyebanji.

### A History of Electoral Drama

Ekiti's electoral history is replete with dramatic twists and turns. The state has been a key battleground in Nigeria's political landscape, with the APC and the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) vying for control since the return of democracy in 1999. The 2018 governorship election, which saw Kayode Fayemi of the APC emerge victorious, was a particularly contentious affair, with allegations of vote rigging and electoral malfeasance dominating the headlines. The current administration has been no exception, with Oyebanji's government facing criticism for its handling of the state's finances and its perceived favoritism towards certain groups. The opposition, sensing blood in the water, is likely to exploit these weaknesses to maximum effect.

### A Divided Opposition

But Oyebanji's opponents will not be the only ones putting up a fight. The APC's own internal dynamics are far from settled, with factions within the party vying for influence. Some members have expressed dissatisfaction with Oyebanji's leadership, citing his perceived closeness to the national leadership and his alleged failure to deliver on key campaign promises. The governor's decision to seek re-election has also sparked debate within the party, with some calling for a new candidate to emerge. The PDP, meanwhile, is likely to be the main challenger to the APC's crown, with several prominent figures already expressing interest in the governorship ticket.

### The Role of the Council

As the APC's campaign council takes up the mantle of mobilizing support for the governor's re-election bid, the task ahead will be daunting. The council, comprising a diverse range of stakeholders, will need to bring the party's various factions together in a bid to present a united front. The APC's national chairman, Yilwatda, has urged the council members to be "strategic" in their approach, emphasizing the need to tap into the party's grassroots structures and mobilize support from across the state. But the opposition will not be easily swayed, and the council will need to be prepared to respond to the inevitable criticisms and allegations that will come its way.

### Reactions and Implications

As the campaign season heats up, the reactions from various stakeholders are already beginning to emerge. The PDP has vowed to give the APC a run for its money, with its national chairman, Iyorchia Ayu, promising that the party will "do everything in its power" to ensure that Oyebanji is defeated at the polls. The APC, meanwhile, is confident of its chances, with Yilwatda asserting that the party is "well-prepared" for the election. The governor himself has expressed gratitude to the party's national leadership for their endorsement, emphasizing his commitment to delivering on the party's campaign promises.

### Looking Ahead

As the Ekiti governorship election draws closer, the state's residents can expect a high level of electoral activity in the coming months. The APC's campaign council will need to work tirelessly to mobilize support and counter the opposition's attacks. But the stakes are high, and the outcome is far from certain. Will Oyebanji emerge victorious, or will the opposition parties succeed in unseating him? Only time will tell, but one thing is certain: the Ekiti governorship election will be a significant test of Nigeria's democratic credentials.
