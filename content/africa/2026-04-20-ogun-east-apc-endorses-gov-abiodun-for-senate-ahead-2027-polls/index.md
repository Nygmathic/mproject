## **A Calculated Gamble: Ogun East APC Endorses Gov Abiodun for Senate**

It is a move that has sent shockwaves through the corridors of power in Ogun State, Nigeria. In a surprising turn of events, the All Progressives Congress (APC) in Ogun East Senatorial District has endorsed Governor Dapo Abiodun for the Senate ahead of the 2027 polls. The endorsement, which has been met with both praise and criticism, is seen as a strategic move by the APC to consolidate its grip on power in the state and possibly beyond.

At the heart of the endorsement is the perceived performance of Governor Abiodun, who has been in office since 2019. According to Chairman of Sagamu Local Government, Afolabi Odulate, the governor's stewardship of the state has been exemplary, with significant investments in infrastructure, education, and healthcare. Odulate's comments were echoed by other leaders in the district, who saw the endorsement as a collective appeal to the governor to accept the senatorial bid. "We are proud of what the governor has achieved so far, and we believe he has the capacity to bring about more development and progress to our senatorial district," said Odulate.

However, not everyone is convinced by the endorsement. Some critics have argued that the move is a calculated gamble by the APC to maintain its hold on power in the state. With Governor Abiodun's term set to expire in 2023, the party may be attempting to create a new narrative that he is not done yet and still has a lot to offer. This reading is not far-fetched, given the APC's history of manipulating the electoral process to maintain its grip on power. In Ogun State, the APC has been in power since 1999, and its dominance has become a hallmark of the state's politics.
