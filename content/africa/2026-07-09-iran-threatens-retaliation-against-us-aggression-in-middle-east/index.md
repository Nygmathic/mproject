## A Warning from Tehran: Iran's Ghalibaf Threatens Retaliation Against US Aggression

In a stark rebuke to the United States, Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Baqer Ghalibaf has issued a blunt warning, warning that if Washington launches a strike, it will be met with a fierce response. The warning comes as tensions between the two nations escalate, with the collapse of a ceasefire and renewed US military strikes against Iranian targets.

Ghalibaf, a stalwart defender of Iran's national interests, has long been a vocal critic of US aggression in the region. His remarks, made in a post on X, are a clear signal that Tehran will not back down in the face of US pressure. "America still hasn’t learned that bullying and breaking promises are no longer cost-free," he stated, his words dripping with a sense of defiance. "Let me put it plainly: if you strike, you’ll get hit."

The stakes are high in this escalating conflict. The Strait of Hormuz, a vital waterway through which much of the world's oil flows, has become a focal point of contention between the US and Iran. Ghalibaf's statement makes clear that any attempt to coerce Iran into opening the Strait will be met with resistance. "Don’t flail around pointlessly, or you’ll sink even deeper: the Strait of Hormuz will only open with 'Iranian arrangements,' not American threats," he said.

This is not the first time that Ghalibaf has spoken out against US aggression. As a prominent figure in Iranian politics, he has consistently advocated for a robust national defence and a strong stance against external threats. His warnings are not mere rhetoric; they are a reflection of a deeply held conviction that Iran must stand up for its rights and interests in the face of a hostile world.

The international community is watching this conflict with growing concern. Analysts suggest that the collapse of the ceasefire and the renewed US military strikes could have far-reaching consequences, potentially drawing in other regional actors and escalating the conflict further. Meanwhile, Ghalibaf's warning serves as a reminder that Iran will not be easily intimidated or coerced into submission.

## The Context of US-Iran Tensions

The current tensions between the US and Iran are the product of a long and complex history. The two nations have been at odds for decades, with US sanctions and military interventions serving as a major source of friction. The Trump administration's withdrawal from the Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action (JCPOA) in 2018 marked a significant escalation in tensions, and the subsequent launch of US military strikes against Iranian targets has only served to heighten the stakes.

Iran's response to US aggression has been consistent: it will defend its national interests and sovereignty at all costs. Ghalibaf's warning is a clear expression of this resolve, and serves as a reminder that Tehran will not be easily swayed from its position.
