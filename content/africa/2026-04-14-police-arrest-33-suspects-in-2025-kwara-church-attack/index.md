In the midst of morning prayers, a chilling fusillade shattered the tranquility of a Kwara church, leaving behind a trail of bloodied worshippers and shattered dreams. The latest twist in this unfolding saga is the arrest of 33 suspects by the police, a development that has sparked a mix of emotions and reactions across the country.
