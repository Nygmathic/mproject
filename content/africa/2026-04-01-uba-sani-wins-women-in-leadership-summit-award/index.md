## A Governor's Vision for a More Equitable Nigeria

Governor Uba Sani of Kaduna State has been awarded the prestigious Women In Leadership Summit Award, a recognition of his unwavering commitment to promoting women's empowerment and gender inclusion in governance. The award, presented at a high-profile ceremony in Abuja, comes as a testament to the governor's trailblazing approach to leadership, one that views women's participation not as a tokenistic gesture but as a vital driver of economic growth and development.

As Nigeria struggles to overcome its myriad challenges, from economic stagnation to insecurity and inequality, Governor Sani's vision for a more equitable society has resonated with women and men across the country. By placing women at the forefront of his governance agenda, the governor has not only acknowledged their agency and capacity but also harnessed their expertise to drive policy reforms and social change. At a time when women's participation in politics remains woefully inadequate, Governor Sani's leadership serves as a beacon of hope, demonstrating that women can and should be integral to the decision-making process.

Governor Sani's award is a culmination of a series of initiatives aimed at promoting women's empowerment and social inclusion in Kaduna State. These include the establishment of a state agency dedicated to promoting women's economic empowerment, the implementation of policies to increase women's representation in the civil service, and the launch of a vocational training programme to equip women with skills in areas such as agriculture, technology, and entrepreneurship. These efforts have not only contributed to a significant increase in women's participation in the economy but also fostered a culture of inclusion and respect in governance.

The governor's approach to governance has also been shaped by his commitment to the principles of federalism and decentralization. By devolving power to local governments and empowering communities to take ownership of development projects, Governor Sani has created a platform for women and marginalized groups to participate in decision-making and reap the benefits of development. This approach has not only strengthened local governance but also helped to build a more inclusive and resilient society.

In an interview with Veridus, a senior official in the Kaduna State government noted that Governor Sani's approach to governance has been inspired by the experiences of other African countries that have successfully harnessed the power of women's participation to drive economic growth and development. "Governor Sani has been studying the experiences of countries like Rwanda and Ghana, where women's participation in politics has been a key driver of economic growth and social change," the official explained. "He believes that by empowering women and promoting their participation in governance, we can unlock new opportunities for economic growth and development that benefit all Nigerians."

While Governor Sani's award is a significant recognition of his commitment to women's empowerment, it also highlights the challenges that remain in promoting gender inclusion and social justice in Nigeria. Despite progress in some areas, women's participation in politics and the economy remains woefully inadequate, and gender-based violence and inequality continue to be major obstacles to social change. In this context, Governor Sani's leadership serves as a reminder that promoting women's empowerment and social inclusion requires a sustained and collective effort from all stakeholders, including government, civil society, and the private sector.
