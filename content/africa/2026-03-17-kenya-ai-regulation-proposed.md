However, with the rapid proliferation of AI comes the need for regulation, and Nominated Senator Karen Nyamu has taken the first step by proposing the creation of an Office of the Artificial Intelligence Commissioner. This move has sparked a nationwide debate on the role of AI in Kenyan society, the potential risks and benefits, and the need for a robust regulatory framework.

The proposed bill, which is currently in its initial stages, seeks to establish a regulatory body that will oversee the development and deployment of AI systems in Kenya. The stakes are high, with the potential for AI to permeate every aspect of Kenyan life, from healthcare and education to finance and transportation. The bill proposes a fine of up to Sh5 million for offenders who fail to comply with the regulations, a move that is seen as a signal of the government's commitment to ensuring that the benefits of AI are realized while minimizing its risks. As the use of AI becomes increasingly widespread, the need for regulation has become a pressing concern, with many experts warning of the potential consequences of unchecked AI development, including job displacement, bias, and privacy breaches.
