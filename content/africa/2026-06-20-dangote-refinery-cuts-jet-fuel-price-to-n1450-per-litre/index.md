## A Rare Glimmer of Hope for Nigeria's Aviation Sector: Dangote Refinery Cuts Jet Fuel Price

In a move that has sent ripples throughout Nigeria's aviation sector, the Dangote Petroleum Refinery has announced a significant reduction in the price of jet fuel. Effective Tuesday, the refinery's gantry price for aviation turbine kerosene (ATK), commonly known as jet fuel, has been slashed to N1,450 per litre from N1,550 per litre. This development comes less than two weeks after the refinery lowered the ex-depot price of premium motor spirit (PMS), also known as petrol, by N75 per litre.

The price adjustment, which was confirmed by a source at the refinery, is a testament to the refinery's efforts to cushion the impact of global crude oil price fluctuations on domestic consumers. The move is seen as a significant victory for airlines and other operators in the aviation sector, who have long complained about the high cost of jet fuel. Jet fuel remains one of the major operating costs for airlines, making changes in its price closely watched by operators in the industry.

The reduction in jet fuel price is also a reflection of the refinery's growing capacity to meet Nigeria's energy needs. With its current processing capacity of 700,000 barrels per day, the refinery is well-positioned to provide a steady supply of fuel to the market, helping to stabilize prices and reduce the reliance on imported fuel. The refinery's efforts to increase its production capacity and reduce the country's dependence on imported fuel have been hailed as a major step towards achieving energy self-sufficiency.

The latest price reduction is also a response to growing calls from the industry for refiners, depot owners, and fuel importers to reflect the changes in the international energy market. The Petroleum Products Retail Outlets Owners Association of Nigeria (PETROAN) had earlier called on operators in the downstream petroleum sector to reduce prices and provide relief for consumers following the decline in global crude oil prices.

While the price reduction is a welcome development, it remains to be seen whether it will have a significant impact on the prices of air tickets and other aviation services. Airlines have long complained about the high cost of fuel, which has contributed to the rising cost of air travel. However, with the reduction in jet fuel price, airlines may be able to pass on the savings to consumers in the form of lower fares.

The Dangote refinery's efforts to reduce fuel prices and increase production capacity have been hailed as a major step towards achieving energy self-sufficiency in Nigeria. As the country continues to grapple with the challenges of fuel scarcity and high prices, the refinery's initiatives offer a glimmer of hope for a more stable and reliable energy market.
