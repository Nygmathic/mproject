## Budget Blues in Nigeria: Tinubu's Failed Promises Haunt Parliament

As the Nigerian parliament convened in emergency session to discuss the implementation timeline of the 2025 federal budget, the air was thick with tension and frustration. The stakes are high, with billions of naira allocated for critical infrastructure projects, including road construction, housing, and energy development, still stuck in limbo.
