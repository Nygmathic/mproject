The WWE's willingness to experiment and push boundaries has drawn inevitable comparisons to the golden age of professional wrestling in the 1990s. The Attitude Era, as it was known, was characterised by its edgy content, bold storytelling, and a cast of larger-than-life characters. While the WWE's current approach shares some similarities with its predecessor, it is also distinct in many ways. The emphasis on character development, for instance, is a marked departure from the more sensationalist approach of the Attitude Era.
