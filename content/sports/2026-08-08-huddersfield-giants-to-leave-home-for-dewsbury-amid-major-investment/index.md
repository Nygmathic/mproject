## **A New Era for the Huddersfield Giants: Leaving Huddersfield Behind**

The Huddersfield Giants, a Betfred Super League side, have announced their intention to relocate to Dewsbury's Flair Stadium from the 2027 season onwards. This decision comes after a period of uncertainty, during which the club had been seeking a permanent location in the Huddersfield area.

The Giants' move to Dewsbury is contingent upon RFL board approval, but the club has already begun investing a projected £10m-plus in developing the ground. The upgrade will transform the venue, increasing capacity from 5,000 to around 8,000 spectators and creating a viewing area on all four sides of the pitch. The redevelopment will also feature refurbishment of fan concourses, significant upgrades to hospitality suites, new bars and food areas, player dressing rooms, and press facilities, elevating the stadium to Super League standards.

The agreement to relocate was reached after extensive and amicable negotiations with the Betfred Championship team, Dewsbury Rams. A strong partnership between the two clubs has been forged, with the Flair Stadium set to continue hosting the Rams in the newly-upgraded and dual-branded facility.

The decision to leave Huddersfield behind has been met with a mix of emotions from the club's owners and officials. Speaking about the decision, owner Ken Davy OBE said, "It's no secret that as a club, we have fought to find a home in Huddersfield. We had to move from Fartown, from Leeds Road, and now from the Accu Stadium. But the Huddersfield Giants will not retreat. We will not compromise or quietly shuffle into the shadows." Davy emphasized the club's commitment to finding a purpose-built stadium within Huddersfield, but acknowledged that this was not feasible for the 2027 season.

Executive chairman Ralph Rimmer OBE expressed his enthusiasm for the new era, stating, "The Giants' future is bright, and we are excited to lead the club into this new era. For fans, the matchday experience will be our focus. A smaller, more intimate ground means the crowd is closer to the action, letting the atmosphere grow and support the team out on the pitch." Rimmer highlighted the benefits of the new stadium, including improved parking, accessibility, and corporate hospitality options.

The relocation to Dewsbury marks a significant shift for the Huddersfield Giants, but the club remains committed to creating a purpose-built stadium within Huddersfield in the future. The investment in the Flair Stadium is expected to elevate the matchday experience for fans and provide a platform for the club to thrive in the short term.
