## A Pitch for Better Turf

The NFL may be the most popular sports league in the United States, but its stadiums are not always equipped with the best playing surfaces. In a bold move, NFL players have launched a social media campaign to pressure their owners and the league to install more permanent grass fields. The call to action is based on a long-standing demand by the NFL Players Association (NFLPA), which has been advocating for better playing conditions for years.

Seven NFL stadiums in the US temporarily switched to grass fields for the World Cup, a move that has convinced many players that it's not only possible but also desirable to have higher-quality playing surfaces. According to the NFLPA, 92% of NFL players prefer natural grass over artificial turf, citing the reduced risk of injuries as the main reason. Data has shown that artificial turf is significantly harder on the body than grass, making it a less-than-ideal option for players who are already subject to a high level of physical stress.

While the majority of NFL stadiums still use artificial turf, there are exceptions. Fifteen of the league's 30 regular stadiums have grass fields, but the other half rely on artificial turf due to the increased cost of maintenance. The NFLPA has argued that the cost of installing and maintaining high-quality grass fields is not insurmountable, and that it should be a priority for the league. The statement issued by the NFLPA just before the World Cup made a compelling case for better playing surfaces, highlighting the investments made to meet the standards for international athletes and global events.

The players who are spearheading the #WorthTheCost campaign are not just advocating for better playing conditions; they're also making a point about the value of their own bodies. Dozens of NFL players, including Caleb Williams of the Chicago Bears, Jonathan Taylor of the Indianapolis Colts, and Zaire Franklin of the Green Bay Packers, have taken to social media to express their support for the campaign. They're using their platforms to raise awareness about the issue and to put pressure on their owners and the league to take action.

The NFLPA's call for better playing surfaces is not just about the players; it's also about the long-term sustainability of the league. As the NFL continues to grow and evolve, it's essential that it prioritizes the well-being of its players. By investing in high-quality grass fields, the league can help reduce the risk of injuries and create a safer and more enjoyable environment for players, fans, and staff alike.
