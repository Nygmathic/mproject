And yet, despite his individual brilliance, Norway's chances of advancing beyond the group stages of the World Cup remain slim. The team's defensive vulnerabilities and lack of depth in midfield make it difficult to see them competing with the likes of Brazil, Argentina, and France.

The weight of expectation is crushing, and it's a burden that Haaland and his teammates will have to shoulder if they are to defy the odds and make it to the knockout stages.

It's a concern that will only be exacerbated by the loss of key players to injury or suspension.

He believes that Norway has what it takes to make a deep run in the World Cup, and he's willing to do whatever it takes to make that happen. Whether Haaland's words will be enough to inspire his teammates and galvanize the team remains to be seen. What is clear, however, is that Norway's World Cup dreams rest on the shoulders of this talented young striker.

As the team prepares to face off against one of the tournament's heavyweights, the pressure is building. Haaland and his teammates will need to dig deep if they are to find a way to overcome the odds and make it to the knockout stages. It's a tall order, but one that they are determined to achieve. The world will be watching as Norway takes to the pitch, eager to see if Haaland and his teammates can live up to the hype.
