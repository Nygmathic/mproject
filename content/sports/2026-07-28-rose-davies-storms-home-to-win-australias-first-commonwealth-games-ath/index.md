## Australia Seizes the Spotlight at Commonwealth Games with Historic Athletics Gold

Rose Davies stormed to victory in the women's 10,000m event, securing Australia's first athletics gold medal at the 2026 Commonwealth Games in Glasgow. The 26-year-old from New South Wales kicked away from Kenya's Diana Wanza in the final kilometre, crossing the finish line 10 seconds ahead of her closest rival.

Davies' triumph marked a significant milestone for Australian athletics, with the country's first gold medal in the women's 10,000m event. The Novocastrian, who holds national records in both the 5,000m and 10,000m distances, attributed her success to her consistent training and growth as a runner.

"I've just had so much consistency and every race I have grown," Davies said. "I've shown that I belong here and I've been running good times, I've just been chipping away." Davies will now shift her focus to the 5,000m event, which takes place on Friday.
