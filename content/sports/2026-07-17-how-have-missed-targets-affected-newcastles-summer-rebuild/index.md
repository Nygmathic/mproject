## The Unseen Struggle of Newcastle's Summer Rebuild

Newcastle United's summer transfer window has been a tale of missed targets, with the club's efforts to secure top talent falling short. While the Magpies have managed to land a handful of promising young players, the failure to sign Johan Manzambi, Victor Munoz, and Hugo Ekitike, among others, has left a sense of uncertainty hanging over the club.

The fact that these players have opted for other Premier League clubs, such as Aston Villa, Liverpool, and Manchester United, highlights the intense competition for top talent in the English topflight. Manzambi's decision to join Villa, for instance, was likely swayed by the prospect of Champions League football, which Newcastle cannot offer this season.

However, this approach raises questions about the club's ability to attract top talent in the short term. With Eddie Howe's side finishing 12th last season, the Magpies are still some way off from being considered a major force in English football. The average starting age of Newcastle's players is significantly higher than that of the top clubs in the league, which may make it harder for the club to attract world-class players.

"We only want players who want to play for Newcastle," according to a club spokesperson, echoing the sentiments of head coach Eddie Howe.
