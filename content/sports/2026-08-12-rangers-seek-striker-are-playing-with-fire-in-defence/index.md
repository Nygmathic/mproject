## A Fractured Defence Exposes Rangers' Vulnerabilities

Rangers are grappling with a significant defensive crisis, manager Derek McInnes revealed, warning that the club is "playing with fire" due to a dearth of left-footed defenders. The situation has been exacerbated by the long-term injury to 22-year-old striker Youssef Chermiti, who sustained an anterior cruciate ligament injury in Saturday's defeat by Hibernian. Chermiti's absence, combined with left-back Tuur Rommens' up to eight-week layoff with a groin problem, has left the club's manager anxious about the defence's ability to cope.

McInnes expressed his disappointment at Chermiti's injury, acknowledging that the young striker was a key component of the team's plans for the season. However, the manager remains optimistic that the club can respond to the setback by strengthening their attack during the transfer window. Rangers have already been linked with a bid for Couhaib Driouech, a winger from PSV Eindhoven, and chairman's expectations suggest that up to five more signings could be on the horizon.

The situation on the left flank is particularly concerning, with McInnes highlighting the lack of a left-footed defender in the squad. The recruitment team is actively seeking to address this issue, with Brentford left-back Jayden Meghoma, 20, reportedly being considered for a second loan spell at Ibrox. McInnes emphasized the need for swift action to rectify the defensive shortcomings, warning that the current situation is unsustainable.

The Europa League qualifier against Jagiellonia Bialystok on Thursday provides a timely opportunity for Rangers to demonstrate their resolve and depth in the face of adversity. McInnes' words underscore the gravity of the situation, as the club navigates a challenging period. The coming weeks will be crucial in determining whether Rangers can successfully adapt to their current defensive vulnerabilities and emerge stronger.

## A Transfer Window to Reboot

Rangers' proactive approach to seeking a new striker, coupled with their efforts to bolster their defence, suggests that the club is eager to seize the transfer window as an opportunity for rebirth. McInnes' candid assessment of the current situation serves as a stark reminder of the risks associated with an over-reliance on a select few players. The club's chairman has set a high bar, with up to five more signings anticipated, which would undoubtedly transform the team's dynamics.

The pursuit of a left-back, in particular, has taken on added urgency given Rommens' injury. The recruitment team must act swiftly to secure a suitable replacement, given the club's fragile defensive situation. The involvement of Brentford's Jayden Meghoma and PSV Eindhoven's Couhaib Driouech adds a layer of complexity to the situation, as Rangers weigh their options and consider the potential benefits of loan signings versus permanent acquisitions.
