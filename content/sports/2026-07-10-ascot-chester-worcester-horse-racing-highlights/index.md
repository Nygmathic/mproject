## Racing Action Hots Up Across the UK

A thrilling Friday of horse racing is in store for fans, with top-class action unfolding at three iconic tracks: Ascot, Chester, and Worcester. Sky Sports Racing will be broadcasting live coverage of the events, starting at 2 pm, and aficionados are in for a treat as some of the sport's brightest stars take to the tracks.

At Ascot, the quality of racing is exceptional, with several high-profile competitors vying for top honors. In the Knight Frank Conditions Stakes, Flann Sunna and Peaceful Warrior are among the standout performers, both having shown impressive form in recent outings. Flann Sunna, trained by Simon and Ed Crisford, made a stunning debut at Windsor, crossing the finish line several lengths ahead of his rivals. With a strong jockey in Jack Mitchell, he is likely to be a force to be reckoned with. Meanwhile, Peaceful Warrior, ridden by Kevin Ryan, has already demonstrated greenness in his previous start at Yarmouth but is expected to improve significantly, given his penalty and subsequent experience.

The field at Ascot also includes Mysterious Times, a Godolphin-owned horse that narrowly missed out on victory at Newmarket. With further refinement and a smoother passage, he is poised to be a significant threat. Other contenders include Washington Royale, who finished a respectable fourth on his debut, and Persazz, which made a striking debut at Ripon. These talented horses are set to provide an electrifying spectacle for spectators, showcasing their speed, agility, and endurance.

Across the country, at Chester, a competitive ESL Export Handicap has drawn a talented field of horses, including in-form Gonna Fly and Infraad. Gonna Fly, trained by Ralph Beckett, has established himself as a rising star, taking his tally to two wins from three starts with a commanding performance at Windsor. Infraad, meanwhile, bounced back from two early setbacks with a dominant display at Pontefract, demonstrating his potential as a top contender. With Ryan Moore and Harry Davies in the saddle, respectively, these horses are poised to deliver thrilling performances that will captivate audiences.

At Worcester, the Amodil Group Handicap Hurdle promises to be an electrifying spectacle, featuring the red-hot Our Guide, which has landed its last three outings. Our Guide, trained by Jamie Snowden, has been in scintillating form, defying a rating of 118 with a convincing victory at Worcester last month. Despite a 4-pound rise, the horse is expected to go close under Gavin Sheehan, with top-weight American Mike also expected to pose a significant challenge.

As the racing action unfolds across the UK, enthusiasts will be eager to witness these remarkable horses in action, each displaying their unique strengths and styles. With Sky Sports Racing providing comprehensive coverage, fans will be treated to an immersive experience that brings the excitement of the sport directly into their living rooms.
