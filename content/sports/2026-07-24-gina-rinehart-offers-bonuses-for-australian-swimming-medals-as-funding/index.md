Amidst the backdrop of a Commonwealth Games beset by high-profile withdrawals, a glimmer of hope has emerged for Australian swimmers. Billionaire Gina Rinehart has announced a bonus scheme for the country's swimmers, in a bid to bridge the funding gap that has left many athletes struggling to make ends meet. The move is a significant development, one that has been welcomed by Australian swimming's star attraction, Kyle Chalmers.

Under the terms of Rinehart's scheme, individual swimmers will pocket $20,000 for a gold medal, $15,000 for silver, and $10,000 for bronze. However, not everyone is convinced of the scheme's merits.
