At the heart of Newcastle's troubles lies the Profit and Sustainability Rules (PSR), which have limited the club's ability to spend on transfers. The PIF's withdrawal of its multibillion-dollar backing of LIV Golf in April raised questions about the club's future, but senior club figures were reassured that the decision did not affect Newcastle.
