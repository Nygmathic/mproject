## Liverpool's Turbulent Summer Takes a Dramatic Turn

The deal, which is reportedly almost agreed but may take up to a month to complete, would see Bezos, one of the world's wealthiest individuals with a personal fortune of around $257 billion (£190 billion), receive equity in the club.

However, the summer has seen a number of high-profile departures, including the departure of Mohamed Salah, who left on a free transfer to join Trabzonspor.

FSG's decision to sell a significant stake in the club comes just a year after it sold a 3% share to US private equity firm Dynasty Equity.
