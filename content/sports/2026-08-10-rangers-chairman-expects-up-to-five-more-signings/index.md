## Rangers' Transfer Window Ambitions

Rangers chairman Andrew Cavenagh has expressed his expectation of up to five additional signings before the transfer window closes, despite the club's struggling start to the season. The club has already bolstered their squad with nine new faces this summer, but Cavenagh remains optimistic about their prospects, stating that he hopes three to five more players will join the team. This sentiment was echoed by chief executive Jim Gillespie, who described the appointment of McInnes as a deliberate effort to bring in a "Klopp-style manager" who would adopt a proactive approach to the game. Gillespie noted that McInnes had been a top target for the club for an extended period and that his style and culture align with Rangers' vision for the future.

Cavenagh's comments highlight the challenges faced by the club in reconciling their ambitious targets with their current performance. The Rangers chairman acknowledged that business has been "clunky" so far, but emphasized the need for sustained winning and the importance of addressing the underlying issues that have led to the club's lack of success in recent years. He also expressed a desire to see improvement in the refereeing and VAR decision-making, particularly in light of the contentious penalty call that led to Hibernian's victory on Sunday.

The club's Europa League campaign has also been affected by their struggles in the domestic league. Rangers trail Polish outfit Jagiellonia Bialystok 2-1 after the first leg of their third qualifying round tie, and will need to deliver a strong performance in the second leg on Thursday to keep their hopes of advancing alive. The winner of this tie will then face either Northern Irish champions Larne or Iberia Tbilisi of Georgia in the play-offs.
