As the 2022 World Cup wound down, the United States Men's National Team (USMNT) found itself in a peculiar position. ## A League in Transition

The USMNT's pool of players is also being shaped by the rapidly changing landscape of Major League Soccer (MLS). The league has been working to strengthen its reputation and compete with European leagues for the world's top talent. However, the DP rule has also created a power imbalance in the league, with top teams able to attract the best players and leave smaller teams struggling to compete. This has led to concerns about the sustainability of the league and its ability to produce players who can compete at the highest level. The USMNT's reliance on MLS players is also being tested, as the league's best players continue to leave for European leagues in search of better opportunities.
