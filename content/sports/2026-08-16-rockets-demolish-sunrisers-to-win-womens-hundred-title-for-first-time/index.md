## Historic Victory for Trent Rockets

In a one-sided final at Lord's, Trent Rockets stormed to a first women's Hundred title after demolishing defending champions SunRisers Leeds by eight wickets. The decisive victory marked the culmination of a stellar campaign for the Rockets, who secured an impressive eight wins from their nine games, including their last seven.

The key to the Rockets' success lay in their clinical display with the ball, which saw them raze SunRisers for 91 from as many balls. Off-spinner Georgia Adams, playing in her fifth Hundred final, enjoyed a brilliant afternoon, taking 2-15 from nine balls and picking up crucial wickets including Annabel Sutherland, who top-scored for SunRisers with 25. Adams' outstanding performance not only turned the game around but also showcased her ability to consistently deliver in high-pressure situations.

The Rockets' batting unit, led by Sophia Dunkley and Beth Mooney, made light work of the chase, pounding 45 in the 25-ball powerplay during an opening stand of 69 from 61 balls. Dunkley, who looked on course for her fourth fifty of the campaign, eventually fell to Dani Gibson, but the damage had already been done. The SunRisers' batting lineup, which struggled to gain momentum throughout the game, slumped to 24-3 after 19 balls and then lost their final seven wickets for 35 once Deepti Sharma was bowled.

SunRisers, who had won the title last summer under their previous Northern Superchargers name, could not repeat their result against this year's winners of the league phase. The team's only bright spot came in the form of fleeting impetus added by Sutherland and Gibson, who clubbed the only six of the innings and latched on to some wayward deliveries from Kim Garth, respectively. However, their efforts were ultimately in vain, as the Rockets romped to victory with 46 deliveries to spare.

The victory marked a significant moment for the Rockets, who have established themselves as a force to be reckoned with in the women's Hundred.
