## A-League Negotiations Stall Amid Allegations of Bad Faith

Players in the Australian Professional Leagues (APL) have expressed disappointment and frustration with the A-League's latest pay offer, which was hastily taken to the media just hours after it was sent to the Professional Footballers Australia (PFA). However, the offer has been met with resistance from the PFA, which has been pushing for a fully professional A-League Women competition.

However, Rosich's comments have done little to alleviate the concerns of the PFA, which has accused the APL of revealing a fundamental misunderstanding of how good faith bargaining works.
