## The Evolution of the MLB Draft

Over the years, the MLB draft has undergone significant changes, with teams becoming increasingly savvy in their approach to identifying and acquiring top talent. The introduction of the amateur draft in 1965 revolutionized the way teams construct their rosters, allowing them to bypass the free agent market and instead focus on developing their own players from the ground up. Since then, the draft has become a critical component of team-building, with the top picks often serving as the nucleus of a championship contender.

The MLB draft has also become a global phenomenon, with international players now making up a significant portion of the top prospects. The addition of the international signing period and the rise of international scouting have opened up new avenues for teams to find top talent outside of the United States. This shift has led to a more diverse and competitive draft pool, with teams now competing for the best players from around the world.
