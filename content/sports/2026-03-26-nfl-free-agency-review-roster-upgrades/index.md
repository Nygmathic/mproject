## The Winners and Losers

At the top of the heap are the Dallas Cowboys, who have emerged as one of the winners of free agency. By securing the services of All-Pro cornerback Stephon Gilmore and bolstering their offensive line with the signing of guard Josh Jones, the Cowboys have addressed two pressing needs and fortified their Super Bowl aspirations. Other teams that have made significant strides include the Los Angeles Chargers, who have bolstered their secondary with the signing of cornerback J.C. Jackson, and the Tennessee Titans, who have upgraded their receiving corps with the addition of wide receiver Robert Woods.

On the other end of the spectrum, teams like the Cleveland Browns, who watched their top free agent target, quarterback Deshaun Watson, slip away to the Cleveland Browns, have struggled to make significant upgrades. The Indianapolis Colts, who lost their star cornerback, Xavier Rhodes, to free agency, have also failed to make meaningful additions. Other teams that have fallen short include the New York Jets, who have struggled to fill the void left by the departure of running back Le'Veon Bell.
