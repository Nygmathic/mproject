The triumph marks a historic milestone for Wakefield Trinity, who have claimed the top honour in English rugby league for the very first time since 1968. The victory at Old Trafford crowns a monumental achievement for the club, defeating Warrington Wolves in a high-stakes Grand Final matchup.
