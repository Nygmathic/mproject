## Turbulent Days for African Football

The African Football Confederation (CAF) has been plunged into turmoil in recent weeks, with the governing body's president, Patrice Motsepe, facing an unprecedented challenge to his leadership. However, with the governing body's credibility in tatters, it is hard to see how this can be achieved. The clubs are demanding that CAF take immediate action to address their concerns, including the implementation of transparent and accountable sponsorship deals, the establishment of an independent audit committee to oversee the body's finances, and the introduction of robust anti-corruption measures.
