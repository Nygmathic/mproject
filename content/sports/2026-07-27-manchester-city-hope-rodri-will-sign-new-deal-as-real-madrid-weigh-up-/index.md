## Manchester City's Rodri Saga Takes a New Turn

Manchester City's hopes for Rodri to sign a new deal have been complicated by Real Madrid's interest in the midfielder. The Spanish club has revised its stance on Rodri following his impressive displays as captain of Spain's World Cup-winning team, where he won the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player.

Rodri's contract at Manchester City expires in June 2027, and despite the club's optimism about negotiations, Real Madrid is now understood to be preparing a bid. This development may prompt interest from other elite clubs, including Barcelona, which could move for Rodri after Frenkie de Jong's medial collateral ligament injury ruled him out for months. Paris Saint-Germain, too, is known to admire Rodri.

Real Madrid's interest in Rodri may be motivated by a desire to strengthen their midfield ahead of the new season. However, the club may need to generate finance to sign the 30-year-old midfielder, particularly if they are to secure the signing of Yan Diomande from RB Leipzig for a fee understood to be more than €100m (£85m). This could lead to the sale of Aurélien Tchouaméni, with Manchester United retaining an interest in the 26-year-old Frenchman. United were earlier unsure if the fee Real would demand for Tchouaméni, around £70m, was within their budget, but the club may yet attempt to secure a deal.

Tchouaméni's teammate, Eduardo Camavinga, is also on United's radar, with a price tag of between €50m-€60m. The uncertainty surrounding Rodri's future has added a new layer of intrigue to the summer transfer window, with several clubs now likely to be vying for the midfielder's services.
