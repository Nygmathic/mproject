The Italian Football Federation (FIGC) is under immense pressure to make a decision that will resonate with fans, sponsors, and, most importantly, the players themselves.

As the Italian football establishment grapples with this unprecedented request, it's worth examining the historical parallels that make Guardiola an attractive choice.
