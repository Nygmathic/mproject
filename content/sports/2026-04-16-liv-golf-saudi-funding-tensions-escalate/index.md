From its inception, the league has been shrouded in controversy, with several high-profile players – including Phil Mickelson and Sergio Garcia – facing criticism for their involvement due to the Saudi regime's human rights record. As the stakes continue to escalate, it remains to be seen whether LIV Golf can overcome these challenges and establish itself as a viable alternative to the PGA Tour.

Meanwhile, LIV Golf's players and officials are digging in their heels, insisting that the league will press on regardless of the PIF's intentions.
