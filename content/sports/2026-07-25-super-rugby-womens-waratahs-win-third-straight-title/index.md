Chancellor's words resonated deeply with the team, who had been written off by many after losing two regular season games. However, they proved their doubters wrong with a scintillating performance that left the Fijian Drua team reeling. The Waratahs' forwards and backs worked in perfect harmony, with their tries coming at a rapid pace. This marked the beginning of a dominant display that saw the Waratahs outscore their opponents by six tries to three.
