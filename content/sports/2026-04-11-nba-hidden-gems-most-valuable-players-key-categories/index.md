One of the most fascinating categories is that of floaters – players who excel at scoring from beyond the arc. Players like Damian Lillard and Bradley Beal are masters of the floater, able to rise above the defense and release the ball at the last possible moment. But what about the players who excel at drawing fouls? Players like Joel Embiid and Karl-Anthony Towns are among the league's best at attracting contact, using their size and agility to get to the line and convert on free throws.

Rebounding is another critical area, with players like Andre Drummond and Hassan Whiteside dominating the glass. But what about the players who excel at creating turnovers?
