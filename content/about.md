## What is Veridus?

Veridus is an independent news review publication dedicated to making sense of the world's most important stories. We dig into the headlines, provide context, and offer clear-eyed analysis of events unfolding across the globe.

The name *Veridus* is rooted in truth — and that is the standard we hold ourselves to.

## What we cover

We review and analyse news across politics, culture, technology, and society. Rather than simply reporting what happened, we ask *why it matters* and *what it means* — for your community, your country, and the world.

## Who is behind Veridus?

Veridus is currently a solo publication run by **Collins Obura**, an independent writer with a passion for global affairs and honest storytelling. Collins researches, writes, and edits every piece published here.

That said, Veridus is built with growth in mind. The long-term vision is to bring in collaborators, volunteer writers, and contributors who share the same commitment to thoughtful, independent journalism. If that sounds like you, get in touch.

## Why independent?

Independent journalism is under pressure. Veridus exists because good analysis shouldn't be locked behind corporate agendas or clickbait economics. Every article here is written with care, not clicks in mind.
