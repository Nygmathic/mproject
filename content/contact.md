Veridus Post welcomes story tips, letters to the editor, corrections, and syndication inquiries.

## Editorial & News Desk

For pitches, letters, or press inquiries, write to **[contact@veriduspost.com](mailto:contact@veriduspost.com)**. Every message is read by an editor rather than an automated triage system.

## Corrections

Accuracy is our primary obligation. If you spot a factual error in any dispatch, please include the article URL and supporting documentation so our editors can verify and append a correction notice.

## Reader Support

For recurring Patron inquiries or alternative contribution methods, see our [Donate](/donate/) page or write directly with **"Supporter Inquiry"** in the subject line.
